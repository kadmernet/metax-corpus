# The Demo Notice

**Route:** `/about/universe/demo-notice/` · **Audience tier 1** — written for any reader, not for
the governance audience.

**What this instrument is.** The estate's plain statement of what currently exists, what is
scaffolding, and how a reader can tell the difference without taking anyone's word for it. It is
required by [B-20](../00-governance-map/launch-blockers.md) and
[DEF-025](../00-governance-map/defect-register.md#def-025), and it is the destination of the
`.mx-demo` provenance strip that renders on every page.

**Why it is audience tier 1.** Almost every instrument in this corpus is written for the
governance reader — tier 4 or 5, dense with identifiers. This one is written for the reader who
arrived from a search result, saw a banner saying "Demo build," and clicked it. If they cannot
understand this page, the banner is decoration.

---

## 1. The statement

**MetaX.Academy is under construction, and much of what you see is scaffolding.**

The site renders a catalogue of forty-seven course series, several credential ladders, an academy
founding path with five levels and eleven requirements, and a research umbrella with nine
programs. **Most of that content does not yet exist as written material.** What exists is the
structure that will hold it, the governance instruments that constrain it, and a design system
that has been built and tested.

That distinction is the whole content of this page, and the estate states it here rather than
letting a reader discover it by clicking into an empty page — because discovering it that way
would teach them, correctly, that the estate is not careful.

The pattern is worth noticing: **what is real is the governance and the structure; what is

estate that writes its curriculum first and its rules afterwards will discover that the rules
would have forbidden the curriculum.

## 4. How to tell, without trusting us

This is the section that matters, because a page that asks to be believed about its own honesty
has asked for the wrong thing. Four checks a reader can run.

**Read the pagestamp.** Every page carries a strip beneath its opening sentence showing a status
badge, an evidence class, a version and a review date. The badge is one of four values:

- `live` — written, reviewed, and describing something that exists
- `planned` — the structure exists; the content does not
- `research-preview` — active research, provisional conclusions, may be withdrawn
- `gated` — access is conditional, and the condition is stated

A `planned` badge on a page full of confident prose is a contradiction you should report.

**Check any number.** Every statistic must carry a date or a `data-count-src` attribute pointing
at the JSON file it was computed from. A bare number is a defect
([Page Contract §9](page-contract.md), line 13), and finding one is a valid correction.

**Read the defect register.** [Thirty-one entries](../00-governance-map/defect-register.md),
published deliberately, including the ones that are embarrassing: the site does not render without
JavaScript, nine infinite animations run with no reduced-motion escape, behavioural ad networks
run on the content plane while the estate publishes a register naming attention expropriation as
a structural harm.

**Read the corrections log.** [Sixteen entries](../00-governance-map/corrections-log.md) recording
claims the estate published and later found wrong. Nine were found by fetching the live site and
reading the code rather than the documents. Two — including a **misspelling of the estate's own
subject**, carried consistently through forty-six ledger entries — were found by the Curator,
because no automated check can catch a name that is wrong everywhere.

## 5. Why the estate publishes in this state at all

Three reasons, and the third is the honest one.

**The governance must precede the content.** The License, the harms register and the standards are
what the curriculum will be judged against. Publishing them first means they constrain what gets
written rather than being retrofitted to excuse it.

**A structure is checkable before it is full.** The route manifest, the tier contract and the
prose standard can all be criticised now, cheaply, by anyone. After 385,000 words are written,
criticising the structure costs a rewrite — which is the point at which estates stop listening.

**And: the estate wants to be read.** That is a plain motive and it should not be dressed up. A
site that stays private until complete is a site that receives no correction until it is too
expensive to accept. That is the trade, and this page is what makes the trade honest rather than
merely convenient.

## 6. When the notice comes down

**Not when the site looks finished.** That is the failure this section prevents.

The estate has committed to making it a **test rather than a judgement**, and the candidate
conditions are on the record at
[RAT-20](../00-governance-map/open-questions-and-ratifications.md#rat-20):

1. No page renders a count from a placeholder data file.
2. Every `live`-badged route exists and satisfies all twenty acceptance criteria.
3. The `FLAGSHIPS` array and all series descriptions are authored, not scaffolded.
4. MetaX's own Part H Statement is published under ML‑2.3 §H.16.
5. The defect register carries no open entry of class "honesty of publication."

The Curator has not yet ratified that list, so the honest reading governs in the meantime: **this
estate is a demo until it says otherwise, in writing, with a date.**

---

## 7. What the notice does not excuse

An estate that discloses its scaffolding and then treats the disclosure as permission has
relocated the problem rather than solved it. Three things this notice does **not** license.

**It does not license publishing pages nobody wrote.** The word floor and the acceptance
checklist close separately from this notice. A page of placeholder prose behind an honest banner
is still a page that should not have shipped, and the estate's own build rule follows from it: a
course route is never generated for a series that is not `live`, because *"emitting a 1200-word
page for an unwritten course is HX‑15 in miniature."*

**It does not license the ad slots.** Google AdSense and Adsterra run on the content plane today,
which engages HX‑01 (attention expropriation) and HX‑04 (the human as product) directly. Being a
demo does not make that acceptable; it makes it **removable before the Part H Statement is ever
published** ([RAT-15](../00-governance-map/open-questions-and-ratifications.md#rat-15)). If the
slots stay, the Statement must say so, and it will sit two clicks from the licence the estate asks
others to adopt.

**It does not license the accessibility defects.** The missing reduced-motion block
([B-16](../00-governance-map/launch-blockers.md)) and the sub-44px touch targets
([DEF-022](../00-governance-map/defect-register.md#def-022)) are defects in the **design system**,
which is one of the things this page lists as *real*. They are inherited by production intact and
they remain launch-blocking.

## 8. The falsifier for this page

This notice is wrong — and you should report it — if you find any of the following:

1. A page badged `live` whose content is placeholder.
2. A number anywhere on the site with no date and no `data-count-src`.
3. A claim in §2 ("what is real") that you cannot in fact read in full.
4. An item in §3 ("what is scaffolding") that has since been written but is still listed here.
5. The `.mx-demo` strip absent from any page, or dismissible on `/license/`, `/about/status/` or
   `/metax/bcia/`, where it must never be hidden.

Report at `/library/record/corrections-log/`. **Rejected submissions are published with reasons**,
which is what stops the correction queue being a black box.

## 9. Obligation

**For the estate:** this page is reviewed every three months as a `perishable` instrument, and
each review either records movement or records that there was none, with the date. A demo notice
that goes stale is itself a form of the dishonesty it exists to prevent — it tells a reader the
estate is still building when the truth may be that nobody looked.

**For the reader:** you are entitled to treat every unbadged claim on this site as unverified, and
to file a correction against anything in §§2–3 that is no longer accurate. Under ML‑2.3 §0.5.2 the
Curator cannot decline a valid correction, cannot delete a published defect, and cannot remove a
superseded claim from the record.

**Under ML‑2.3:** §H.15 (compliance theatre), §H.17 (non-theatre), §H.19 (authorship of record)
and §H.26 (corpus hygiene) all bear on this page. §H.15 is why it exists: an estate that knows it
is a demo and does not say so commits that harm on the exact pages a reader would consult to check
whether it commits that harm.
