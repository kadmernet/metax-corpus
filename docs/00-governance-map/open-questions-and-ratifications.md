# Open Questions and Ratifications

Some decisions cannot be made by an editor, by an agent, or by a reconciliation pass. They
require the Curator, because they are either facts only he holds or authority only he
carries. This file lists every one of them, states what is blocked, and states what was done
provisionally in the meantime.

**The rule that makes this list honest:** where a question is open, the affected instrument
says so on its own face. No instrument in this corpus presents a provisional resolution as
settled. Provisional resolutions are marked `RATIFICATION PENDING` in the text, not in a
footnote.

## RAT-01 — Fill and sign the license placeholders

**The question.** [ML-2.1](../license/ml-2-1.md) carries `[STEWARD NAME / ORGANIZATION]`,
`[REGISTRY URL]`, `[DATE]`, and `[FPR]` as unfilled fields, inherited from ML-2.0.

**Why only Maher can decide it.** These are facts about who he is, where the Registry lives,
and which key signs it. An editor filling them would be fabricating the identity of the
accountable party, which is the exact fault the corpus calls cardinal.

**What it blocks.** Everything public. This is a launch blocker and is stated as one in
[Launch Blockers](launch-blockers.md). The *Review* put it most precisely: an unsigned
charter whose entire architecture rests on accountable authorship is self-refuting. A corpus
that publishes a standard of accountable authorship while its own governing instrument has no
named author has failed its own first test.

**Provisional state.** Placeholders retained verbatim and visible. They are **not** filled
with plausible values, and no page in the estate may render as if they were.

## RAT-02 — Retire the "seven layer" and "D1–D10" framings

**The question.** [BX-004](../bcia/bx-004-observed-taxonomy.md) establishes by full-text scan
that the books self-declare three layers — Meta-X, Transition, Higher-Order — and that both
"seven layers" and "D1–D10" appear zero times in the book corpus. Does Maher formally retire
both framings as BCIA vocabulary?

**Why only Maher can decide it.** The evidence settles what the drafts *say*. It does not
settle what BCIA *means*, and under the non-doctrinal rule an editor may not promote an
observation to doctrine.

**What it blocks.** [BCIA Gate 2](../bcia/bx-003-remediation-gates.md#gate-2--taxonomy-reconciliation)
closure, and with it the Branch E gate condition.

**Provisional state.** Gate 2 stands at **Triangulated — pending owner ratification**. The
three-layer map is the working map because the books declare it on their own covers. Branch D
front matter carries `bcia_layer`, `bcia_book_code`, `tier_range`, `depth_range` taken from
the covers. `D1`–`D10` as domain tags is retired on separate grounds — it was never BCIA
vocabulary at all, per [R-04](reconciliation-ledger.md#r-04--the-d-prefix-collision-rubric-dimensions-vs-domain-tags-vs-bcia-layers) —
and that retirement needs no ratification because nothing is being decided about BCIA.

## RAT-03 — Specify or retire BSN, LPN and AMN

**The question.** Books 1–2 declare a three-language triad for BCIA's description layer.
Context suggests AMN is the human-command side, BSN the biological-signal side, and LPN the
bridge — but **no formal definition of any of the three exists anywhere in the ingested
corpus**, and none of them recurs after Book 2. Specify them, or retire them.

**Why only Maher can decide it.** There is nothing in the corpus to reconcile. This is not a
contradiction to resolve but an absence to fill or to close, and both are authorial acts.

**What it blocks.** [Gate 3](../bcia/bx-003-remediation-gates.md#gate-3--notation-amn-lpn-bsn)
and part of [Gate 7](../bcia/bx-003-remediation-gates.md#gate-7--canonical-glossary-and-errata-workflow).

**Provisional state.** The notation is **frozen**
([BX-005](../bcia/bx-005-notation-freeze.md)). The triad may be mentioned in Branch D only as
"declared future BCIA languages, specification pending", never taught as working notation and
never cited as doctrine. The inferred role split is recorded as context-inferred and
explicitly unconfirmed.

## RAT-04 — Name the ethics reviewer and the successors

**The question.** Four role holders are structurally required and named nowhere: the ethics
reviewer ([Charter §0.5](../corpus/mx-000-charter.md#05-powers-placed-beyond-the-curator)),
the successor curator and alternate ([MX-015](../corpus/mx-015-succession-and-continuity.md)),
and the Successor Prompter and alternate
([AG-000 §A.6.3](../agents/ag-000-agent-constitution.md#a6-degraded-mode-and-prompter-succession)).

**Why only Maher can decide it.** These are appointments.

**What it blocks.** More than it appears. The ethics veto is one of the three powers placed
beyond the Curator, and it is the primary evidence that MetaX is an institution rather than a
personality. An unnamed veto holder means the veto cannot be exercised, which means the
evidence is not yet in. Similarly, the succession machinery is the provision that decides
whether any of this outlives its author.

**Provisional state.** Every role is fully specified — powers, limits, dual-control
requirements, publication duties — with the holder as `[VERIFY]`. Specifying the office
without naming the officer is honest; naming a placeholder officer would not be.

## RAT-11 — How the scenario assessment resolves against the plane boundary

**The question.** [MX-DESIGN-001](../../sources/MetaX.Academy_UI-UX_and_Design_System_Documentation.md)
Part 9 feature 6 places **Deterministic Scenario Assessment** at
`/s/{id}/c/{nn}/scenario/` — a content-plane route. It specifies a seeded branching scenario
with a fixed decision tree and *published scoring logic*, no language model in scoring, and a
debrief in which the learner opens the full tree and sees every path not taken.

That cannot sit on the content plane as described. If the tree ships in the static HTML, the
learner can read the answers before deciding, and it is not an assessment. If the tree is
withheld, the page needs request-time state, and it violates
[ST-19 §2](../standards/st-19-two-plane-architecture.md#2-the-boundary-rule).

**Why only Maher can decide it.** Each option surrenders something the corpus explicitly
values, and the ranking is a curatorial judgement, not an editorial one:

| Option | Gains | Surrenders |
|---|---|---|
| **A** — seeded client-side tree, scoring logic published, tree omitted from the initial payload | Keeps the route on the content plane; keeps assessment integrity for a casual learner | Integrity is cosmetic — anyone can fetch the payload. A defence that only stops the incurious is a claim the corpus would call unfalsifiable |
| **B** — host at `me…/scenario/` | Genuine assessment integrity; clean plane boundary | The sixth beat of the lesson arc becomes unreachable to anonymous readers, so the arc is no longer wholly public |
| **C** — content plane, tree fully public, reframed as a **rehearsal** rather than a gate | Total transparency; perfect plane compliance; the debrief *is* the teaching, as Part 9 itself says | The scenario can no longer contribute to a credential decision |

**What it blocks.** The sixth beat of `mx-lessonarc` on every course page, and any credential
weight assigned to scenario performance.

**Provisional state — Option C.** Adopted provisionally on the narrowest available ground: it
is the only option that leaves the page's own description of itself true. Part 9 already
concedes the pedagogical position — *"the debrief is the actual teaching, and the assessment is
the pretext"* — and if that is so, then C surrenders the pretext and keeps the teaching.
Option A was rejected outright rather than held open, because a payload-hiding defence would be
security theatre published inside a corpus about evidence.

If Maher wants the scenario to carry credential weight, the answer is B, and the arc's public
completeness must then be restated honestly on the course page.

**Recorded at** [R-31](reconciliation-ledger.md#r-31--content-plane-routes-in-part-9-that-cannot-be-static).

## RAT-14 — Does ML-2.2 exist?

**The question.** [ML-2.3](../license/ml-2-3.md) declares `supersedes: "ML-2.2"`, its §E.3
preserves ML‑2.2 grants until 2027‑03‑31, and its changelog describes itself as the repair of
six defects **in ML‑2.2**. The estate has never held or published an ML‑2.2 text. The published
predecessor is [ML‑2.1](../license/ml-2-1.md).

**Why only Maher can decide it.** Three options exist and each costs something he owns:
publishing a retrospective 2.2 (which would be manufacturing a record); re-basing 2.3's
references onto 2.1 (which amends a governing instrument he supplied); or publishing 2.3 with
the gap declared (which puts a defect on the face of the estate's highest-tier document).

The first option should be refused on principle and this file says so plainly. Drafting a 2.2
whose defects conveniently match 2.3's repairs would be the estate fabricating its own history
inside the instrument that defines *Ṣidq* as *"do not build the machine that makes the
distinction between lying and telling the truth unrecoverable."*

**What it blocks.** §E.3's transition provision has no referent, so no licensee can determine
which version governs an existing grant. Also every `license:` front-matter field in the corpus,
which currently spans ML‑2.1, ML‑2.2 and ML‑2.3 across different files.

**Provisional state — option three.** ML‑2.3 is published with a non-operative Publication Note
stating that no ML‑2.2 exists and that **no party may rely on §E.3** until this resolves. Filed
as licence defect ML‑D‑007 and reconciliation entry
[R-38](reconciliation-ledger.md#r-38--the-ml-22-gap).

## RAT-15 — Will the estate publish its own harms as unremedied?

**The question.** ML‑2.3 §H.16 obliges a Tier 1+ licensee to publish a Part H Statement
recording a disposition for each of the twenty-five harms. §H.16.3 makes a Statement with
nothing partially remedied or unremedied **presumptively non-compliant**, and treats it as
evidence of HX‑15 (compliance theatre). Live Google AdSense and Adsterra slots on the content
plane engage HX‑01 and HX‑04 directly.

**Why only Maher can decide it.** It is a decision about publishing something unflattering
about his own estate, and it has a revenue consequence. No editor may make it for him.

**What it blocks.** `/statement/`, and the credibility of Part H generally. An instrument whose
author exempts himself is not a licence, it is a marketing position.

**Provisional state.** No Statement is published. The two honest paths are recorded and the
third is named as dishonest:

| Path | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Remove the ad networks | HX‑01 and HX‑04 become genuinely remediable; revenue falls |

## RAT-16 — The *Ḥifẓ al-ʿAql* extension

**The question.** The *maqāṣid* mapping in HX‑000 and ML‑2.3 §0.4 extends *Ḥifẓ al-ʿAql* — the
preservation of intellect, classically concerned with intoxicants — to cover
attention-engineering and engineered compulsion. **Is that extension sound as a matter of
Islamic legal reasoning, or is it a modern analogy wearing classical dress?**

**Why only Maher can decide it — and he cannot decide it either.** This is the one item on this
list that no party to the estate is competent to resolve. It requires a scholar of *uṣūl
al-fiqh*. What Maher decides is **who is asked**, and whether the answer is published even if it
is unfavourable.

The reasoning is disclosed so it can be attacked rather than assumed: the classical prohibition
protects the faculty of reasoned judgement, not the specific chemistry of the intoxicant; a
system engineered to defeat reflective self-governance by variable-ratio reinforcement injures
the same faculty by a different route. That is an *analogy*, its middle term is contestable, and
the estate does not have the standing to call it a ruling.

**What it blocks.** Nothing operationally, and this is deliberate. ML‑2.3 §0.4 provides that
*"nothing in this License requires a licensee to accept that extension as a matter of religious
law; the duties it generates stand on the secular evidence set out in HX‑000 independently."*
The duty survives the analogy's failure. That independence is the whole reason the mapping can
be published while contested.

**Provisional state.** Published as **contested**, at
`https://license.metax.academy/open-questions/rat-16/`, and marked as a reasoned lay analogy
requiring scholarly review wherever it appears. It is never presented as settled, and the
*maqṣad* column of the harm register carries that qualification.

## RAT-19 — Is `Method` a ninth pillar, or does it fold under TopTech?

**The question.** The canonical `MENU` array in
[MX‑NAV‑SPEC v2026.10.1 §3](../../sources/MX-NAV-SPEC_v2026.10.1.md) carries nine top-level
pillars, and the specifying agent discloses that one of them is its own invention: *"`Method` as
a ninth pillar is my addition, not yours."* `Method` gathers the cognitive arc, the six rails,
the epistemics and the twelve instruments — 46 routes — which previously existed as anchors
scattered across TopTech and Credentials. The alternative is `/toptech/method/`.

**Why only Maher can decide it.** Two reasons, and the second is the one that matters. First,
it is a claim about what the estate *is*: a nine-item menu with Method and License as peers of
TopTech says the apparatus and the rules are destinations, not appendices. Second and more
importantly, **an agent may not enlarge the estate it documents on its own authority.** ML‑2.3
§H.19 (authorship of record) and §H.25 (agent constraint) both bear directly: a structural
addition proposed by an agent and adopted without the Curator's ruling would be a change of
shape with no accountable human author, which is the fault the corpus calls cardinal.

**What it blocks.** More than it appears to. The pillar count fixes the nav geometry — nine items
do not fit at 1000px or 1120px, which is why the spec moves the mobile→desktop breakpoint to
**1180px** and makes the nav gap and type fluid via `clamp()`. It fixes the route census (46 of
the 321 English routes are Method's). It fixes the gradient assignment, since
[DS-00 §2](../design/ds-00-as-built-token-layer.md) assigns three gradients for three pillars and
nine surfaces need a nine-way derivation ([DEF-028](defect-register.md#def-028)). And it decides
whether "three pillars" remains a navigational statement anywhere in the corpus
([C-16](corrections-log.md#c-16)).

**The recommendation on file, recorded and not executed.** The spec's own argument is that
*"the estate's academic credibility rests on that apparatus being a first-class destination
rather than a footnote inside a marketing pillar,"* and that folding it *"costs one nav slot and
buries the most defensible thing on the site — I would keep the nine and move the breakpoint."*
This file finds that argument strong and declines to act on it, which is the whole point of the
file.

**Provisional state.** Nine pillars are adopted **provisionally**, because the route manifest is
unusable without a count and no work could proceed otherwise. Every instrument that renders the
nine carries the pending marker. If the Curator folds Method, the cost is bounded and known: 46
routes re-parent from `/method/…` to `/toptech/method/…`, the breakpoint reverts to 1000px, and
the `_redirects` file gains 46 lines. The cost is bounded **only while the pages are unwritten**,
so this question decays faster than any other on this list.

> Recorded as exemplary conduct, separately from the merits: an agent that proposes a structural
> addition to an estate it does not own, labels the proposal as its own in the specification's
> own text, and states the cost of refusing it, has satisfied §H.19 in the only way that
> matters. The estate should notice how much easier this question is to answer *because* the
> disclosure was made, and how invisible the same addition would have been without it.

## RAT-20 — When does the estate leave demo, and what does leaving require?

**The question.** The Curator has declared the live build a **demo**

## RAT-21 — Is ≈385,000 words written, reduced, or staged?

**The question.** The 1200-word floor across 321 English routes is, by the spec's own
arithmetic, **≈385,000 English words plus an Arabic mirror of equal standing** — call it 770,000
words at parity. The spec states the number deliberately: *"That number is worth stating out
loud before work starts, because it is the real cost of 'no missed details.'"* Nothing states
where the words come from, on what schedule, or under whose authorship.

**Why only Maher can decide it.** It is a resource question he owns, and it is an authorship
question the corpus cannot answer for him. Under §H.19 every page needs an accountable author;
under §H.25 agent-generated prose carries constraints; and 770,000 words of agent-generated
academic prose published under an estate's name is, whatever else it is, a claim about what that
estate has thought about.

**What it blocks.** Nothing structurally — and that is exactly why it is filed. Work can proceed
page by page indefinitely without the question ever being asked, and the failure mode the spec
names is not incompleteness but **uniformity**: *"321 pages that all sound like the same
eight-section template."* Its own mitigation is to vary which section leads — a standards page
opening on Definition, a harm page on the macro frame, a series page on the worked instance —
and it warns that agents must be told so explicitly *"or they will all open on Position."*

**The honest options, none of them free.** Write it all, and accept that most of it is
agent-drafted and must be disclosed as such. Reduce the census — fewer, better routes, which
costs the lattice property of [R-52](reconciliation-ledger.md#r-52--the-lattice-requirement).
Stage it — ship pillar by pillar with unwritten routes absent rather than thin, which is the
option the badge vocabulary (`live | planned | research-preview | gated`) was designed to
express. Or lower the floor, which the estate should not do quietly, because a word floor
abandoned without a changelog line is the same defect as a padded page pointed the other way.

**Provisional state.** The recipe, the three registers and the §7.4 exemptions are adopted. The
volume is **unbudgeted** and no page count is promised anywhere in the corpus. The staging
option is treated as the default until the Curator says otherwise, because it is the only one of
the four that never requires publishing a page nobody wrote.

## RAT-22 — Does the Arabic mirror ship with the English, or after it?

**The question.** §1.7 gives every route an `/ar/` twin at the identical path, and states that
*"the Arabic page is a translation of equal editorial standing and no superior authority; a
divergence is a defect."* ML‑2.3 §H.22 requires language parity. Zero Arabic pages exist
([DEF-002](defect-register.md#def-002), [RAT-09](#rat-09--the-arabic-authoritative-editions)).

**Why only Maher can decide it.** Arabic is authoritative in this estate, not a courtesy
([MX-020](../corpus/mx-020-language-authority.md)), and he is the only person who can write or
commission it at the standard the parity claim asserts.

**What it blocks.** The parity claim itself, and — less obviously — the cost of every route
decision made from now on. Each English page shipped without its twin increases a debt that
§1.7 characterises not as a backlog but as a **filed defect**, which is a materially stronger
posture than "translations pending."

**The interaction the estate wants him to see.** Shipping 321 English pages first makes the
Arabic mirror a 385,000-word translation project against a moving target; shipping in lockstep
halves the rate and doubles the credibility. There is a third path the spec's own structure
suggests: ship the mirror **pillar by pillar in lockstep**, so that `/license/` and `/about/`
exist in both languages before `/toptech/` exists in either — which puts parity where the estate
claims its authority is, and is consistent with wave 1 publishing governance before curriculum.

**Provisional state.** The mirror is specified at equal standing and unbuilt. Every instrument
carrying `hreflang: "en, ar"` and `prevailing_text: "en"` states parity as a **target, not a
fact**, and this file records that the distinction between *prevailing text* (a conflict rule of
last resort) and *editorial standing* (a quality obligation whose breach is a defect) is now
reconciled at [R-55](reconciliation-ledger.md#r-55--the-arabic-mirror-has-equal-standing) and
must not be collapsed.

## RAT-23 — Should the estate pay for introductions at all?

**The question.** [ST-23](../standards/st-23-affiliate-and-referral.md) specifies what a compliant
referral program would look like. It does **not** argue that there should be one. Two questions
are open: the prior question of whether any payment for a recommendation is compatible with this
estate's commitments, and — if it is — four commercial figures that no editor may set.

**Why only Maher can decide it.** The prior question is a judgement about what the estate is
willing to be. The four figures are commercial terms he owns:

| Figure | Why it cannot be set here |
|---|---|
| The referral rate | A price |
| Refund-window length before payment issues | A commercial risk allocation |
| The income-proportion threshold of §3.4 | Decides when a participant becomes a distributor |
| The unattributed-referral threshold | Sets when ST-23's own falsifier 2 fires |

**Why the prior question is genuinely open, and not a formality.** A defensible position holds
that **any** payment for a recommendation compromises it, however disclosed — that disclosure
informs a reader without restoring the recommender's disinterest, and that an estate whose central
asset is being trusted about technology should not introduce a financial interest into the one
channel it cannot audit. The contrary position is that referral is how anything reaches an
audience without advertising, and that a disclosed one-time payment is strictly more honest than
the ad slots the estate currently runs. This file does not resolve it.

**What is blocked.** `/about/access/referrals/`, the `mx_commerce` referral tables, and the
quarterly publication obligation of ST-23 §8.

**Provisional state, and the defect it carries.** ST-23 is published as `canonical` and marked in
its front matter as **designed from scratch with no upstream source** — the only instrument in
this corpus in that position. Nineteen supplied sources were read in full and none contains an
affiliate system, so the Curator's instruction to *"add affiliate system if missed"* was
discharged by design rather than by reconciliation.

That is itself recorded as a governance defect rather than a completed task. An agent-designed
commercial instrument governing **by default, because nobody supplied an alternative**, is a
different kind of artefact from an agent-drafted document reconciling supplied material, and the
estate should not let the two wear the same `canonical` badge without comment. If the Curator
holds a referral design of his own, **it supersedes ST-23 in its entirety** and ST-23 is retained
as a non-governing draft under [Charter §0.8](../corpus/mx-000-charter.md).

## Review cadence

This file is reviewed every three months, which is the Perishable interval under
[ST-05](../standards/st-05-decay-and-review-intervals.md), because an open-question list that
goes stale is worse than none: it tells a reader that nothing has moved when the truth is
that nobody looked. Each review either records movement or records that there was none, with
the date.

**Next review due:** 2027-01-01.

**Amended 2026-08-30** to add RAT-11, RAT-12 and RAT-13, arising from MX-DESIGN-001. Three new
open questions in one release is not a failure of the reconciliation — it is what happens when a
document adds money and identity to a universe that had neither, and the alternative was to
resolve them by preference and not say so.

**Amended 2026-09-02** to add RAT-14 through RAT-18, arising from ML‑2.3 and the as-built design
review. Five new open questions in one release, and the pattern in them is worth naming: four of
the five are questions about **the estate's own honesty** rather than about external facts — does
a superseded version exist, will we publish our own failures, is our religious reasoning sound,
and does our site do what we say it does. A governance corpus that grows harder to answer as it
matures is behaving correctly. The alternative — a list that shortens every quarter — would mean
the questions were being closed by preference.

**Amended 2026-10-01** to add RAT-19 through RAT-23 — the first four arising from
MX‑NAV‑SPEC v2026.10.1, and RAT-23 from ST-23, the estate's only instrument with no upstream
source. The list has now grown in three consecutive releases and stands at twenty-three open
questions with none closed, which requires a word rather than a shrug.

The four added here share a property the earlier eighteen mostly lack: they are questions about
**scale and its authorship**. Is Method the estate's ninth pillar or an agent's? When does a demo
stop being a demo? Who writes 770,000 words, and does an estate that publishes them get to say
it has thought about them? Does the second language ship with the first or trail it? Each
becomes harder to answer as more pages exist, and three of the four are explicitly
**decay-sensitive**: RAT-19 is cheap today and expensive at 100 written pages, RAT-21 is cheap
before dispatch and meaningless after, RAT-22 compounds page by page. The file has previously
treated open questions as stable pending a decision. These are not, and each now records what it
costs to answer late.

The uncomfortable reading, recorded because this file exists to record it: a list that only grows
may be an honest list, or it may be a list that has learned to substitute for deciding. Twenty-two
open questions with a governing licence, a harms register and a 321-route manifest all published
above them is a corpus that documents its irresolution with unusual care. The next review should
ask not whether the list grew, but **how many entries have moved** — and if the answer is none
again, that fact belongs in the register as a defect of governance rather than a line in this
paragraph.

**Amended 2026-09-26 (v2026.10.2)** to add RAT-24 through RAT-28. RAT-24 was already cited in eight files before it existed here. That is the dangling-reference failure this file is meant to prevent, and it is recorded rather than smoothed over. The list stands at twenty-eight, with none closed.
