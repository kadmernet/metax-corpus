# MX-029 · The Academies Charter

> **Authority.** `governing`, tier 3. In the source corpus this was **MX-004, "Terms of Top
> Levels"** — the top levels being the **academy levels L1–L5**. The v2026.10.1 pass reused the
> number and the title for the nine navigational pillars, a different subject
> ([GM-13](../00-governance-map/identifier-reallocation-record.md), [DEF-036](../00-governance-map/defect-register.md#def-036)).
> Technical manifest: [ST-31](../standards/st-31-academy-json-manifest.md). Record:
> [REG-10](../registries/reg-10-academy-registry.md).

> **Disclosure first.** **No academy exists at any level.** Requirement group 4 needs three
> calibrated reviewers, one external; there are none. Group 5 needs a named ethics reviewer; there
> is none for MetaX itself.

## 1. Position

An academy here is not a course dump. It is a **governed body** with membership, a published
standard, a named curator, an ethics reviewer with veto, and a path to permanence. Admission is
earned and never purchased. The full standard is published **before** the application opens,
because gatekeeping that hides its criteria is indistinguishable from favouritism.

## 2. The five levels

| Level | Name | Grant | May issue |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | Applicant | Thesis filed. No subdomain, no listing | — |
| L2 | Sandbox | Private subdomain, `noindex` — *so you can be wrong in private* | — |
| L3 | Provisional | Public, indexed; one complete series; directory listing marked provisional | Series Practitioner |
| L4 | Chartered | Full ladder; annual audit | + Professional Operator |
| L5 | Institution | Governance seat; own calibrated pool; may co-sign Master-Fellow; may charter sub-academies subject to ratification | + co-signature |

Progression requires evidence, not tenure. Each level page states the commonest reason
applicants stall.

## 3. The eleven binding requirement groups

1. **Curator eligibility** — verified Professional Operator in the domain, or an accepted
   equivalent dossier; public attributable identity (pseudonym only with escrowed real identity);
   conflict disclosure; no active revocation.
2. **Thesis and scope** — 800–2,000 words: claim, falsifier, audience, non-duplication. Surface
   overlap >30% with an existing academy routes the applicant to become a **branch** of the
   incumbent.
3. **Curriculum minimum** — one complete series before L3: six courses minimum, twelve target,
   six lessons each on the Cognitive Arc ([ST-25](../standards/st-25-cognitive-arc.md)); Limits,
   Bridge, 90-day capstone, pre-registered hypothesis with a null, kill criteria and unit
   economics wherever commercial claims are made ([ST-26](../standards/st-26-unit-economics-and-kill-criteria.md)).
4. **Assessment** — frozen D1–D5 without local modification; ≥3 calibrated reviewers, ≥1 external;
   calibration twice yearly; published appeals route; **absolute prohibition** on pay-for-pass,
   purchased levels, credentials without evidence.
5. **Governance** — published charter; named ethics reviewer with veto; conflict policy;
   moderation policy; **succession clause** — *the clause that proves the other four were sincere.*
6. **Publication discipline** — version control; public patch notes; changelog; no silent edits;
   AI disclosure; dated citations; decay classes.
7. **Technical** — Annex A.
8. **Language** — English or Arabic primary; a mandatory bilingual term sheet contributed to the
   Lexicon for every series.
9. **Legal and commercial** — MX-002 and MX-003 adopted by reference; content CC BY-SA 4.0 or more
   permissive with a twelve-month open-access clause; marks per [MX-027](mx-027-trademark-and-brand.md);
   one below-fold ad slot, no interstitials (**see conflict note §6**); credentials never
   purchasable; Core Riba refused.
10. **Red lines** — no content sexualising or targeting minors; no weapons, exploit, or malware
    instruction; no personalised medical, legal, or financial advice; no wet-lab biology; the
    human-cell exclusion verbatim wherever bio-adjacent; no political mobilisation.
11. **Review and enforcement** — annual dated public audit; 90-day remediation; suspension
    unpublishes while preserving archive; revocation replaces the site with a dated **tombstone**
    (what it was, when revoked, reason category, appeal outcome).

## 4. Enforcement ladder

Advisory note → published finding → remediation order (90 days) → suspension → revocation. Each
step published and appealable under [MX-016](mx-016-appeals-and-due-process.md). Charter §0.4
breaches skip to revocation.

**Why each academy is its own project.** Independent deployment, independent suspension,
independent blast radius. A suspension is one deployment action on one project. Governance
expressed as infrastructure.

## 5. Worked instance (hypothetical)

*Hypothetical.* A Level 3 academy on podcasting is audited; its Limits module omits the economic
class. Published finding; 90-day remediation order; fixed on day 60; the finding stays in the
directory with its closure date. The directory lists **suspended academies alongside active
ones**, because quietly deleting a failure is the same dishonesty as a silent edit.

## 6. Conflicts recorded (not resolved)

- **Advertising.** Group 9 permits one below-fold slot; [MX-009](mx-009-advertising-and-commercial.md)
  (v2026.10.1) sets six cumulative conditions and finds behavioural ad networks non-compliant.
  Group 9 is read **subject to** MX-009. R-58.
- **Content licence.** Group 9 requires CC BY-SA 4.0; the estate's own content is ML-2.3. Whether
  an academy's CC BY-SA content inside an ML-2.3 estate is compatible is a Part D question —
  RAT-27.

## 7. Limits

A charter can specify good governance; it cannot supply trustworthy people. Every group above
presumes the curator is acting in good faith and gives the estate audit, not prevention.

## 8. Falsifier

The charter's claim — that published criteria prevent favouritism — is falsified if any academy
is admitted, advanced, or spared suspension on grounds not traceable to §3 in its published
record.

## 9. Obligation

No application may open until MetaX itself satisfies groups 4 and 5.

## 10. Macro register

Most online education scales by removing governance: more courses, fewer standards. This charter
scales the opposite way — **each new institution must carry the whole governance apparatus,
including the means of its own shutdown**. That is slower, and it is the only way a federation of
academies stays one standard instead of becoming a marketplace.

## Annex A — Technical requirements (group 7)

- Own static hosting project on a `*.metax.academy` subdomain.
- Repository auditable by MetaX.
- WCAG 2.2 AA ([ST-12](../standards/st-12-accessibility-implementation.md)).
- No trackers beyond those declared.
- Shared chrome partials consumed at build time, refreshed within **90 days** of a chrome release
  ([ST-28](../standards/st-28-component-library.md)).
- `academy.json` manifest at the fixed path ([ST-31](../standards/st-31-academy-json-manifest.md)).
- Bilingual routing with `hreflang` ([MX-020](mx-020-language-authority.md)).

> **Tension with the route manifest.** [SITE-01](../site/route-manifest.md) is one origin with
> directory routes and no content subdomains ([C-04](../00-governance-map/corrections-log.md)).
> Academy subdomains are the **one** sanctioned exception, per
> [MX-DESIGN-001](../../sources/MetaX.Academy_UI-UX_and_Design_System_Documentation.md) and
> [ST-19](../standards/st-19-two-plane-architecture.md). Recorded at R-59 so the exception is
> explicit.
