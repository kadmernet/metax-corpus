# MX-027 · Trademark and Brand Use

> **Authority.** `governing`, tier 3. The licence mechanism is ML-2.3 §5-bis (defensive
> termination). Reuse of **content** is [MX-012](mx-012-licensing-and-reuse.md); this instrument
> governs **names and marks** only.

> **Disclosure first.** **No mark has been registered anywhere**, and whether the estate should
> register is a Curator decision with cost. The claims below are unregistered common-law or
> equivalent claims at most, and their strength is unknown without counsel
> ([B-05](../00-governance-map/launch-blockers.md)). The estate also shipped its own subject's
> name misspelt for a release ([C-13](../00-governance-map/corrections-log.md#c-13)) — a brand
> policy for a name one has misspelt is written with some humility.

## 1. Position

Trademark in a knowledge estate has an unusual job. It must stop **impersonation** — someone
selling "MetaX credentials" — without ever being used to stop **use of the ideas and terms** the
estate contributes to the commons. The source resolves this by pairing ordinary brand protection
with a licence clause that punishes anyone who tries to trademark the estate's terminology
against its users.

## 2. Definition — the marks

The MetaX name; the ✦ mark; the glyph set; pillar names (TopTech, Meta-X / MacroLifeTech,
Academies, BCIA and the others in [SITE-01 §3](../site/route-manifest.md)); credential names
([MX-025 Annex A](mx-025-credentialing-and-assessment.md)). Claimed as marks of Maher.

**Not marks:** the defined terms in the [Lexicon](../lexicon/keyword-register.md), series
identifiers, notation, rubric dimension names. These are contributed for adoption.

## 3. Mechanism

| Use | Permission |
|---|---|
| Truthful reference ("this course cites MetaX ST-04") | **Free** |
| Criticism, commentary, parody | **Free** |
| Holder stating a credential in the published format | **Free** |
| Member academy using its granted badge and subdomain within its grant | **Free within grant** |
| Suggesting endorsement | Not permitted |
| Use in a product or company name | Not permitted |
| Modifying the marks | Not permitted |
| Use in a domain other than a granted subdomain | Not permitted |
| Use by a suspended or revoked academy | Not permitted |

**Defensive termination (§5-bis).** Anyone who asserts, or procures the assertion of, a trademark
or equivalent right over terminology, notation, series identifiers or defined terms first
published in the Work, **against another user of the Work**, loses their licence rights
automatically. Their own distinct branding is unaffected.

## 4. Worked instance (hypothetical)

*Hypothetical.* A vendor registers "Limits Module" as a mark for course software and sends a
takedown to an academy using the phrase. The vendor's rights under ML-2.3 terminate automatically
under §5-bis. The academy's use is unaffected. Whether a court honours the termination is a
separate question (Track 1 of the [enforceability table](../license/enforceability-two-track-table.md)).

## 5. Failure modes

- Using trademark to suppress a critical fork — forbidden by §G.5 humility reasoning.
- Treating the Lexicon as brand property — contradicts §2.

## 6. Limits

A policy is not a registration. Without registration, enforcement against a determined
impersonator in a foreign jurisdiction may be impractical.

## 7. Falsifier

Wrong if the estate ever uses a mark claim to stop truthful reference, criticism, or use of a
Lexicon term.

## 8. Obligation

Curator: decide registration (jurisdictions, classes, cost) — filed with [RAT-07](../00-governance-map/open-questions-and-ratifications.md#rat-07--jurisdiction-governing-law-and-the-liability-cap).

## 9. Macro register

Enclosure of shared vocabulary by trademark is a recurring move in platform markets: a community
coins a term, a firm registers it, the community loses the word. §5-bis is a **commons defence
written into the licence itself**, which is cheaper than litigation and binds everyone who wants
the licence's benefits.
