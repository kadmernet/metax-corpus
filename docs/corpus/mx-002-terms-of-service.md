# MX-002 — Terms of Service

> **Authority.** `governing`, tier 3 of the
> [precedence ladder](../00-governance-map/precedence-and-conflict-rules.md).
> Subordinate to [ML-2.3](../license/the-maher-license.md) (tier 1),
> [HX-000](../harms/hx-000-the-fourteen-structural-harms.md) (tier 1a, incorporated
> by §A.10) and the [Charter](mx-000-charter.md) (tier 2). Where these terms and
> the licence conflict, **the licence prevails and these terms are defective.**

> **Read this first.** **The live site is a demonstration.** It is not a commercial
> service, it carries no availability commitment, and it has no paying customers.
> These terms are written as though they must work, because terms written only for
> a demonstration would have to be rewritten at the exact moment they started to
> matter. See [SITE-04](../site/demo-notice.md).

> **Disclosure.** These terms were drafted by an AI system and **have not been
> reviewed by a lawyer** ([REG-08 §4](../registries/reg-08-role-and-authority-registry.md)).
> Their classification as legally enforceable terms versus stated commitments is
> **self-assessed**. Read §11 before relying on any of it.

## 1. Position

Terms of service occupy an awkward tier. They are `governing`, so they bind; but
they sit **below** the licence and the harms schedule, which means the most
important limits on what the estate may do to a user are **not here**. A reader
looking for the real protections should read
[ML-2.3 Part H](../license/the-maher-license.md) and
[MX-023](mx-023-attention-and-human-sovereignty.md), both of which constrain the
estate more tightly than any clause below.

This document therefore does the narrow job terms of service can honestly do:

- state what the service **is** and is not;
- state what a user may and may not do;
- state what the estate promises, which is little, and precisely;
- point to the instruments that do the heavier work.

What it deliberately does **not** do: disclaim everything, arbitrate everything,
and reserve a unilateral right to change anything. Those three moves make terms
enforceable-looking and worthless to read, and the estate treats
readability-by-the-bound-party as a condition of legitimacy, per
[Charter §0.4](mx-000-charter.md).

No term used in these terms is defined **only** here. Each traces to a defining
instrument, per [LEX-01](../lexicon/keyword-register.md) and
[ST-01](../standards/st-01-identifier-and-url-grammar.md). Terms of service that
introduce private definitions are a common way to narrow a promise after making
it, and the estate forecloses that by refusing to define anything locally.

## 3. What the service is

1. **A document estate.** The service publishes governance, licensing, and
   educational documents. That is the entirety of the offering.
2. **A demonstration.** The live deployment exists to show the estate's form. Parts
   of it are scaffolding. [SITE-04](../site/demo-notice.md) states what is real.
3. **Static.** There are no accounts, no logins, and no user-generated content.
4. **Free.** No payment is taken. [MX-021](mx-021-payments-and-commerce.md)
   specifies a payment path that **is not active**.

### 3.1 What the service is not

- Not advice — legal, medical, financial, or professional. The corpus discusses
  legal architecture *about itself*; that is not advice to you.
- Not a platform. No user may publish here, so no moderation of user content
  arises; [MX-008](mx-008-moderation-and-enforcement.md) governs the estate's own
  content.
- Not a data service. The estate holds no dataset about people
  ([REG-06](../registries/reg-06-dataset-registry.md)).
- Not certified, accredited, or credentialling. **No credential has been issued and
  none currently can be** ([REG-05](../registries/reg-05-credential-registry.md)).

## 4. What the estate promises

The promises are short and each is falsifiable. Anything not listed is not
promised.

| # | Promise | Falsifier |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Every published document states its authority, version, and review date | Find a published document without them |
| P2 | Corrections are published, never silent | Find a substantive change absent from the [corrections log](../00-governance-map/corrections-log.md) |
| P3 | Known defects are disclosed, including embarrassing ones | Find a known fault absent from the [defect register](../00-governance-map/defect-register.md) |
| P4 | No reader tracking, no engagement instrumentation, no accounts | Find a retained record about a reader ([REG-06 §8a](../registries/reg-06-dataset-registry.md)) |
| P5 | No advertising, no affiliate links, no sponsored content in governing text | Find one; see [MX-009](mx-009-advertising-and-commercial.md), [ST-23](../standards/st-23-affiliate-and-referral.md) |
| P6 | Identifiers and routes, once published, remain resolvable or redirect | Find a dead published route with no redirect record ([SITE-03](../site/routing-law.md)) |

### 4.1 What is expressly not promised

- **Availability.** None. The service may be unreachable at any time for any
  duration. There is no uptime target, no status page, and no credit.
- **Accuracy of the estate's own self-assessment.** The estate publishes its
  compliance claims and labels their evidence class
  ([ST-07](../standards/st-07-evidence-and-verify.md)). Many are **E0 or E1**.
- **Completeness.** The corpus is openly partial. Roughly one quarter of the
  routes in [SITE-01](../site/route-manifest.md) correspond to written pages
  ([REG-02](../registries/reg-02-page-registry.md)).
- **Fitness for any purpose**, including the purposes the documents describe.
- **Arabic text**, despite Arabic being the prevailing text of every instrument.
  **No Arabic page exists** ([MX-020](mx-020-language-authority.md)).

P1–P6 are deliberately promises about **conduct the estate controls**, not about
outcomes it does not. A promise of accuracy would be unkeepable; a promise to
publish known inaccuracy is keepable and is more useful to a reader.

## 5. What a user may do

Use of content is governed by [ML-2.3](../license/the-maher-license.md), not by
this section. [MX-012](mx-012-licensing-and-reuse.md) summarises the licence and
**governs nothing**. In outline only, and without authority:

- **Read**, for any purpose, without permission, payment, or identification.
- **Quote and cite**, with attribution to the instrument's `doc_id` and version.
- **Reuse** as the licence permits, subject to Part H and the
  [eight absolutes of §H.3(a)](../license/the-maher-license.md).
- **Falsify.** Every instrument carries a falsifier and the estate invites its
  use. This is a permission, not a courtesy.

## 6. What a user may not do

1. **Misrepresent authority.** Do not present a summary as governing text, or a
   superseded version as current. Version numbers are on every page.
2. **Strip disclosure.** Do not reproduce content with its AI-authorship note,
   evidence class, or defect disclosure removed. Republishing the estate's claims
   without the estate's admissions is a material misrepresentation.
3. **Breach the licence absolutes.** [§H.3(a)](../license/the-maher-license.md)
   lists eight. Seven are cumulative; **(vii) developmental substitution is
   disjunctive** — it bites on its own.
4. **Attack the service** — no unauthorised access attempts, no attempts to
   degrade availability.
5. **Attempt to identify other readers.** There is nothing to find
   ([MX-022](mx-022-identity-and-sessions.md)), and trying is prohibited.
6. **Use the estate's name to imply endorsement or accreditation.** The estate
   endorses nothing and accredits no one.

### 6.1 Consequence of breach

The estate's only practical remedies are those in the licence
([§F.4](../license/the-maher-license.md): four conditions terminating
automatically, nineteen covenants curable within 30 or 90 days) and the red lines
in [MX-008](mx-008-moderation-and-enforcement.md).

**Stated plainly:** the estate has no technical enforcement capability. It cannot
block a user, because it has no accounts and no reader records. Its remedies are
licence remedies and publication. A reader who finds this weak has correctly
understood it, and the weakness is the direct price of P4.

## 7. Changes to these terms

1. Changes are **versioned** at the permanent path `/terms/`. No `-v2` paths exist;
   see [ST-01](../standards/st-01-identifier-and-url-grammar.md).
2. Every change is logged in the
   [changelog](../00-governance-map/changelog.md). Substantive changes are also
   logged in the [corrections log](../00-governance-map/corrections-log.md).
3. **No silent edits** ([Charter §0.8](mx-000-charter.md)).
4. The estate does **not** reserve a right to change these terms retroactively,
   and does not claim that continued use constitutes acceptance of an unread
   change. Version history is published so a user may compare.
5. The superseded text is retained per
   [GM-12](../00-governance-map/archive-register.md). One known gap exists in the
   estate's own version history — **ML-2.2 is absent and declared unrecoverable**
   ([version history](../license/version-history.md)) — which is exactly the
   failure clause 5 is meant to prevent.

## 8. Worked instance

**A user reuses a page and hits the real limit.**

A trainer wants to teach from [ST-24](../standards/st-24-page-prose-standard.md) in
a paid course. Trace the actual analysis:

| Question | Answer | Source |
|---|---|---|
| May they read it? | Yes, unconditionally | §5 |
| May they quote it? | Yes, with `doc_id` + version | §5 |
| Do these terms permit the reuse? | **These terms do not decide** | §5, first line |
| What decides? | [ML-2.3](../license/the-maher-license.md) | tier 1 |
| Does the paid context matter? | Possibly — [§H.2-bis Scale Gate](../license/scale-gate.md) tiers 0–3 | ML-2.3 |
| Which tier applies? | **Cannot be determined** — three thresholds are unpublished | [scale gate §3](../license/scale-gate.md) |
| Does absolute (vii) bite? | Only if the course **substitutes for** the learner's own development | §H.3(a) |
| Result | **Indeterminate**, and the indeterminacy is the estate's fault |  |

This is the honest outcome, and it is worth stating why the estate publishes it
rather than papering over it. A reader who follows the chain correctly arrives at
an unanswerable question because the estate has not published three numbers. The
alternative — writing a confident permission in these terms — would make the terms
*look* helpful while contradicting the tier 1 instrument, which under §1 would
make these terms **defective** rather than the licence generous.

So the user's practical position: read and quote freely; for the course, the
governing analysis cannot be completed until the scale gate thresholds are
published. That is filed as a ratification question and is a real cost imposed on
a real user by the estate's incompleteness.

## 9. Failure modes of these terms

| # | Failure | Current state |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | **Terms outrun the licence** — a permission here broader than ML-2.3 | Guarded by §1 and §5's deferral; no permission is granted here |
| F2 | **Promise inflation** — P1–P6 restated elsewhere as stronger | Live risk; README has previously overstated corpus completeness |
| F3 | **Unenforceable prohibitions** — §6 acts the estate cannot detect | **Actual** — §6.1 admits it |
| F4 | **Demo drift** — the demonstration becomes a real service without these terms being revisited | **Live**; review interval 12 months is too long for that transition |
| F5 | **Prevailing text absent** — Arabic governs and does not exist | **Systemic** ([MX-020](mx-020-language-authority.md)) |
| F6 | **No counsel review** — enforceability self-assessed | **Actual** ([§11](#11-limits)) |

## 10. Falsifier

> **IF FALSE.** These terms are falsified if:
>
> - **(a)** any of P1–P6 is breached — each has a stated test in §4;
> - **(b)** a permission granted here is shown to exceed
>   [ML-2.3](../license/the-maher-license.md);
> - **(c)** the service is shown to take payment, serve advertising, or retain a
>   reader record while these terms are published;
> - **(d)** a version of these terms is shown to have changed without a changelog
>   entry.
>
> **(a) with P4 is the sharpest available test** and requires only inspection of
> the hosting layer — which the estate itself has **not** performed
> ([REG-06 §7.1](../registries/reg-06-dataset-registry.md)). The estate's own
> assessment is that this is the promise most likely to be found breached, and it
> is published in that knowledge rather than after a check it has not run.

## 11. Limits

1. **No counsel review.** `counsel_reviewed: no`. Whether any clause here is
   enforceable is unknown to the estate. The
   [enforceability two-track table](../license/enforceability-two-track-table.md)
   classifies the licence's provisions by a **self-assessment**, and these terms
   inherit that limitation.
2. **No jurisdiction, no forum, no governing-law clause.** Deliberate: naming a
   forum the estate could not use, or a law it has not been advised on, would be a
   claim beyond its evidence. The consequence is that a dispute's forum is
   **undetermined**. A user should treat that as a real gap, not as a concession in
   their favour.
3. **No dispute-resolution process for users.** [MX-016](mx-016-appeals-and-due-process.md)
   provides three stages and **stage 2 is unstaffable**
   ([REG-08](../registries/reg-08-role-and-authority-registry.md)). A user with a
   grievance has, in practice, one route: write to the curator, who is also the
   decider.
4. **Arabic prevails and is absent** (§4.1).
5. **These terms were absent for the estate's entire prior life.** The
   `provenance_of_purpose` field records it: the site operated with no stated
   terms until this version. Nothing retroactively cures that period.

## 12. Obligation

**On the estate:**
- Honour P1–P6, or publish the breach.
- Never let these terms grant what the licence withholds (§1).
- Revisit these terms **before** the demonstration becomes a service, not at the
  next 12-month interval (F4).
- Publish the scale gate thresholds so §8's analysis can terminate.
- Obtain counsel review, or continue labelling every enforceability claim as
  self-assessed.

**On a user:**
- Observe §6; the licence, not this page, defines reuse.
- Do not strip disclosure when republishing (§6.2).
- Treat every estate self-assessment as carrying the evidence class it states.
