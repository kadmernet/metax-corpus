# MX-014 · Research Ethics and Hard Exclusions

**What this instrument governs.** What this estate will not research, will not teach, and will not
publish — stated as **exclusions** rather than as principles, because a principle can be balanced
against an interest and an exclusion cannot.

**Why exclusions rather than a framework.** An ethics framework produces a judgement in each case,
and the party making the judgement is the party with an interest in the research proceeding. A hard
exclusion removes the judgement. The estate accepts that this is coarser, and prefers coarse and
binding to sophisticated and negotiable.

---

## 1. Position

The estate's research arm is **MacroLifeTech (Meta‑X)** — nine programs, one of which is BCIA,
bio-computational inquiry ([SITE-01 §5.4](../site/route-manifest.md)). That is the pillar this
instrument exists for: a research umbrella touching biology, computation and human formation, run
by one person, with no institutional review board.

Published at `/method/notation/research-ethics/`, with the human-subject exclusion at
`/method/notation/human-subject-exclusion/`.

**The macro register.** Under **HX‑24** (developmental default) and **HX‑21** (foreclosure of the
successor's options), the characteristic failure of research ethics is not misconduct but
**incrementalism**: each step defensible, each approval reasonable, and the aggregate somewhere
nobody chose to go. Institutional review boards exist to interrupt that, and their real function is
not expertise but **friction supplied by people with no stake in the outcome.** A one-person
research program has no such friction by construction — so this instrument substitutes the only
available alternative: **exclusions fixed in advance, unamendable toward weakness, published before
the research that would test them.**

---

## 2. Definition — the three refusals

From [MX-000 §0.4](mx-000-charter.md#04-the-three-refusals). These sit **outside precedence
entirely** ([GM-01 §5](../00-governance-map/precedence-and-conflict-rules.md)) and no instrument at
any tier can weaken them.

**1. Laboratory instruction in biology.** The estate does not teach wet-lab technique, protocol or
procedure. BCIA is **theoretical and computational**, and this exclusion is why.

**2. Core Riba.** [ML-2.3 §C.9](../license/ml-2-3.md)'s four cumulative elements, enforced in
schema rather than policy ([ST-22 §4.2](../standards/st-22-platform-and-persistence.md)).

**3. The sexualisation of minors.** Absolute, with no research, artistic or contextual exception.

**No research exception. No artistic exception. No contextual reframing. No amendment toward
weakness.** The last clause is the operative one: a future Curator cannot narrow these, and
[MX-015 §3.2](mx-015-succession-and-continuity.md) binds a successor to the same limit.

---

## 3. Definition — the five hard exclusions

Beyond the three refusals, five exclusions bind all research published under any MetaX property.

**1. Human subjects.** No research involving human participants — no experiment, no trial, no
recruitment, no intervention, no data collection from people as research subjects. Routed at
`/method/notation/human-subject-exclusion/`.

**2. Human cells and human-derived material.** The **BCIA human-cell exclusion**
([BX-002](../bcia/bx-002-human-cell-exclusion.md)). BCIA's subject is bio-computational
*principle*; the exclusion means no line of inquiry proceeds through human-derived material even
theoretically modelled.

**3. Capability uplift.** No publication whose primary effect is to make a harmful capability more
available — the same *primary use* test [MX-008 §3.1](mx-008-moderation-and-enforcement.md) applies
to curriculum.

**4. Covert research on the estate's own readers.** No A/B test, no dark pattern trial, no
engagement experiment, no undisclosed variation. This is the exclusion most likely to be violated
by a well-meaning improvement, and it is absolute:
[MX-023 §3.1](mx-023-attention-and-human-sovereignty.md) forbids engagement metrics governing any
decision, and this extends it to research framing.

**5. Research the estate cannot publish in full.** If a finding cannot be published — because of a
confidentiality term, a funder restriction or a competitive interest — **the estate does not do the
research.** Unpublishable research is unauditable research, and an estate with private findings has
a private interest in what its public ones say.

### 3.1 Why exclusion 4 is the one that binds hardest

Exclusions 1–3 and 5 concern research the estate is not currently in a position to conduct anyway.
**Exclusion 4 forbids something the estate could do tomorrow, cheaply, with obvious benefit.**

Every ordinary product practice — testing two headlines, varying a call to action, measuring which
lesson ordering retains better — is covert research on readers who did not consent. It is
universal, it is not usually called research, and it is exactly the machinery
[MX-023](mx-023-attention-and-human-sovereignty.md) refuses.

**The estate's position is that a reader is not a subject.** Under
[§H.18](../license/ml-2-3.md) (consent integrity), consent obtained by being present is not
consent; and under §H.21 the burden of an experiment falls on people with no stake in its outcome.
A reader who came to learn something has not volunteered to be a data point in the estate's
optimisation.

**What the estate may do instead, and must do openly.** Publish a proposed change, invite
objections under [ST-06](../standards/st-06-objections-standard.md), and record the reasoning.
Slower, worse-powered, and it treats a reader as a party rather than a condition.

## 4. Mechanism — the pre-registration requirement

Any research published under Meta‑X or BCIA is **pre-registered before data or analysis begins**,
at `/method/instruments/prereg/`:

```
QUESTION     [what is asked]
HYPOTHESIS   [what is expected]
NULL         [what result would count as no effect]
KILL         [what result would end this line of inquiry]
EXCLUSIONS   [which of §§2–3 were considered and how they apply]
PUBLICATION  [committed, in advance, regardless of outcome]
```

**`KILL` and `PUBLICATION` are the two that do work.** A kill criterion stated in advance prevents
a line of inquiry surviving its own disconfirmation, which is the commonest way research programs
become doctrines. A publication commitment made before the result is known prevents the file-drawer
problem — and it is the same commitment
[ST-03](../standards/st-03-limits-and-bridge.md) requires of every course series, which must publish
its pre-registered hypothesis, null and kill criterion.

**`EXCLUSIONS` must be answered explicitly, not assumed.** A pre-registration stating *"no
exclusion applies"* without addressing them has not considered them —
[RB-05 §2](../rubrics/rb-05-harm-review-gate.md) treats the same pattern as a failed check in harm
audits, for the same reason.

## 5. Worked instance — a proposed BCIA line of inquiry

A researcher proposes modelling information-processing dynamics in a simple biological substrate,
using published cell-line data.

**Exclusion 1, human subjects.** Not engaged — no participants.

**Exclusion 2, human cells.** **Engaged, and this is where the proposal stops.** The published
dataset includes human-derived lines. BX-002 excludes inquiry proceeding through human-derived
material *even theoretically modelled*, and "it is already published" is not a permission — the
exclusion is about the line of inquiry, not about the provenance of the file.

**What survives.** The same question using non-human substrate data, pre-registered with a stated
kill criterion. Narrower, slower, and inside the exclusion.

**Refusal 1, laboratory instruction.** Not engaged, provided the published output describes
principle rather than protocol. The line is fine and it matters: explaining *why* a mechanism
behaves as it does is permitted; supplying steps that reproduce it is not.

**What the instance shows.** The exclusion bit at the cheapest possible moment — at proposal, before
any work — and it bit on a point the researcher had a good argument against. **That is exactly what
an exclusion is for**, and a framework that balanced interests would have permitted this: the data
is public, the work is computational, no person is touched. The estate's judgement is that a
one-person research program that permits this permits the next thing, and the friction has to come
from somewhere.

## 6. Failure modes

**Reclassification.** A study relabelled as "product improvement," "user research" or "telemetry
analysis" to escape §4's pre-registration. The test is the **activity**, not the label — the same
rule [ST-19 §5](../standards/st-19-two-plane-architecture.md) applies to ad scripts that were never
called islands.

**The exclusion that gets balanced.** *"On the whole the benefit outweighs…"* — an exclusion that
can be outweighed is a principle, and §1 explains why this instrument is not written that way.

**Pre-registration after the result.** Registered once the direction is known. Detectable only by
timestamp, which is why the register is dated and public.

**The file drawer.** Uncomfortable results going unpublished. §4's `PUBLICATION` commitment and
exclusion 5 together are the control.

**Incrementalism.** §1's central concern. Each step justified by the last, with no step large enough
to trigger a refusal. **The estate has no structural protection against this beyond fixed
exclusions and publication**, and it says so rather than implying a process exists.

## 7. Limits

**Class: structural, and decisive.** **There is no ethics board.** The ethics-reviewer role exists
in [MX-000 §0.5](mx-000-charter.md#05-powers-placed-beyond-the-curator) with a veto, and it is
**unfilled** — `[VERIFY]`, [V-04](../00-governance-map/verification-log.md),
[RAT-04](../00-governance-map/open-questions-and-ratifications.md#rat-04--name-the-ethics-reviewer-and-the-successors).
So the estate currently substitutes fixed exclusions for independent review, and the exclusions
were written by the party they constrain.

**Class: practical.** **No research has been conducted, pre-registered or published.** BCIA holds
material at
[Gate 3 currently unresolvable](../00-governance-map/defect-register.md#def-006), and the
pre-registration desk at `/method/instruments/prereg/` does not exist. Everything here is untested.

**Class: epistemic.** Fixed exclusions are coarse. §5 shows one blocking work that is arguably
harmless, and the estate accepts that cost rather than accepting a balancing test it would
administer alone.

**Class: religious.** The *Ḥifẓ al-ʿAql* extension underpinning parts of the attention reasoning is
published as **contested** and awaits scholarly review
([RAT-16](../00-governance-map/open-questions-and-ratifications.md#rat-16),
[V-11](../00-governance-map/verification-log.md)). The exclusions here rest on the secular arguments
independently and do not require it.

## 8. Falsifier

1. **Valuable research blocked by an exclusion where the block protects nobody** — §5 is arguably
   already an instance. Repeated instances would falsify the coarseness trade rather than the
   exclusions.
2. **An exclusion circumvented by reclassification**, showing §6's activity test unenforceable
   without a reviewer.
3. **The five exclusions proving incomplete** — a research activity that is plainly wrong and fits
   none of them.
4. **Fixed exclusions proving no better than a balancing framework** in preventing incrementalism.
   **Untestable until research actually happens**, which is the honest state: this instrument's
   central claim cannot yet be checked.

---

## 9. Obligation

**For the estate:** fill the ethics-reviewer role. §7 is the largest structural gap in this
instrument and it has been open since the first release — an exclusion regime administered solely
by its author is the arrangement §1 says an IRB exists to prevent.

**For a researcher under Meta‑X or BCIA:** pre-register before beginning, answer `EXCLUSIONS`
explicitly, state a kill criterion, commit to publication in advance. If a finding cannot be
published in full, **do not begin** (exclusion 5).

**For anyone proposing an A/B test, a variation trial or an engagement experiment on readers:**
exclusion 4 forbids it. Publish the proposed change, invite objections, record the reasoning.

**Under ML-2.3:** §H.18 (consent integrity — exclusion 4's foundation), §H.21 (proportionate
imposition), §H.24 (developmental default), §H.3(a)(vii) (developmental substitution —
**disjunctive**, any single limb sufficing), §H.30 (non-participation), §C.9 (Core Riba, the second
refusal), §H.27 (reversibility).
