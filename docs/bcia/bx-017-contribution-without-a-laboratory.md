# BX-017 · Contributing to BCIA Without a Laboratory

> **Authority.** `guidance`. It sits under [BX-010](bx-010-document-zero-and-research-charter.md) and
> [BX-011](bx-011-human-cell-exclusion.md).

> **Disclosure first.** No contribution intake exists yet. There is no form, address or queue. Until
> the Curator names a route, contributions have nowhere to go, and this page says so rather than
> collecting them (RAT-28(c)).

---

## 1. Position

Every useful contribution to BCIA-R at present is **desk work**. The seven gates are editorial,
bibliographic and logical. None requires a laboratory. Because of the exclusions, none ever may.

## 2. Definition — permitted contribution kinds

| K | Kind | Serves | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| K1 | Source verification | Gate 6 | Close a C-row in [BX-015](bx-015-critical-audit-and-open-problems.md) with an L-1 citation |
| K2 | Inventory reconciliation | Gates 1, 1a | Compare the two Volume I copies line by line and report where they differ |
| K3 | Terminology proposal | Gate 7 | Draft a specification, or a retirement note, for AMN/LPN/BSN |
| K4 | Position analysis | Gates 4, 5 | A structured brief on universality versus revelatory grounding |
| K5 | Ingestion | Gate 2 | Transcribe the missing chapters of Books 7–10 |
| K6 | Counter-argument | OP-1–3 | The strongest published case **against** biological advantage |
| K7 | Translation check | All | An EN↔AR check that the verbatim exclusion block is equivalent in both languages |

## 3. Mechanism

Every contribution: (1) names its K and its gate; (2) carries the AG-016 provenance stamp if AI-assisted;
(3) is published in full whether accepted or not (MX-014 exclusion 5); (4) is credited by name unless
the contributor declines.

## 4. Worked instance

A reader finds that C-02 (Shannon, 1937) refers to the thesis year, while the publication year is
1938. They file a K1 note with both citations. The row becomes "verified, with a date qualifier" and
the reader is credited in the verification log.

## 5. Failure modes

- **Scope creep toward the bench:** "a simple home experiment with yeast". This is refused under BX-011 for all substrates.
- **Unpaid-labour extraction:** contributions used without credit. Rule (4) prevents this.
- **Advocacy dressed as K4:** K4 briefs must set out both positions.

## 6. Limits

A contribution cannot close a gate. Only the Curator's ratification closes one.

## 7. Falsifier

This guidance has failed if a contribution is accepted that involves bench work, or goes unpublished.

## 8. Obligation

The Curator names an intake route, or states that there is none.

## Macro register

A programme with no laboratory can still be scrutinised, if the scrutiny is open to anyone. The
desk-work routes above are how outsiders take part.
