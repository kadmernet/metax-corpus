# BX-016 · The Refused Inference

> **Authority.** `governing`, and it applies to every Branch D page.

> **Disclosure first.** The estate's own interest is at stake here. BCIA-R is the Curator's
> programme, and the refused inference is the argument that would most help it. The estate refuses it
> anyway.

---

## 1. Position (verbatim, source MX-014 §14.5)

> MetaX will not make the rhetorical move from "silicon has ceilings" to "therefore biology is the
> answer." The first clause is engineering. The second is a hypothesis with thin evidence and a long
> literature of failed analogies behind it. If BCIA earns the second, it will be by experiment.

## 2. Definition

The **refused inference** is any argument whose premise is a limit of silicon (thermal,
Landauer-bound, lithographic, economic) and whose conclusion is that biological computation is the
remedy, with no comparative evidence in between.

## 3. Mechanism

- [RB-06](../rubrics/rb-06-structural-review.md) G4 flags any paragraph where a silicon-limit claim is followed, within the same section, by a biological remedy claim without a C-row citation ([BX-015](bx-015-critical-audit-and-open-problems.md)).
- A Branch D page may state both clauses only in separate sections, and the second must be framed as OP-1.

## 4. Worked instance

**Fails:** "Moore's law is ending; living neurons compute at a fraction of the energy; the future is biological."
**Passes:** "Moore's law scaling has slowed [VERIFY]. Separately, some biological systems show low energy per operation on narrow tasks [VERIFY]. Whether that transfers to general computation is open problem OP-1."

## 5. Failure modes

- **Enthusiasm by juxtaposition:** the two claims sit side by side and the reader supplies the inference.
- **Energy-only comparison:** joules per operation compared with no account of reliability, speed or programmability.
- **Analogy as evidence:** "the brain is a computer, so computers should be brains."

## 6. Limits

The refusal does not forbid biological computation as a topic. It forbids one argumentative shortcut.

## 7. Falsifier

The refusal has failed if any MetaX page draws the inference. It becomes unnecessary if OP-1 is
answered in the affirmative by controlled comparison. The inference would then be earned, and would
no longer be rhetoric.

## 8. Obligation

Agents apply the §4 rewrite pattern. Curators do not waive it for the estate's own material.

## Macro register

A limit in one technology is easily recast as a case for another. Keeping the two claims apart is how
the estate avoids selling its own programme.
