# BX-018 · The Register of Substrate Harms *(issued as "BX-000 v1.0")*

> **Authority.** The register that ML-B-1.0 incorporates. On shared apparatus (evidence tiering,
> roles, defect format) [HX-000](../harms/hx-000-register-v2027-1.md) governs. On substrate-specific
> matter this register governs. It is **not** a doctrine of the BCIA research programme
> ([BX-010](bx-010-document-zero-and-research-charter.md)). It is a set of **pre-registered
> predictions with named falsifiers** about harms the estate has decided in advance never to commit.

> **Disclosure first.**
> - (1) **Why "BX-018" and not "BX-000".** The source issues this as BX-000 v1.0, and the identifier BX-000 already has three other claimants (GM-16 D-09, DEF-044). The source label is kept in front matter so that citations of "BX-000 v1.0 / BX-01…BX-24" resolve here.
> - (2) **Two numbering systems.** Harm entries **BX-01…BX-24** (two digits) are not instruments **BX-000…BX-018** (three digits). See [LEX-05 CT-13](../lexicon/contested-terms.md).
> - (3) It was **written before its object existed**. Most entries are Tier 3 (DEF-2026-065), and **4 entries are flagged** (BX-02 partial, BX-07 partial, BX-11, BX-21).
> - (4) The estate holds **no substrate and no interface**. It reports no breach, and that is worth nothing as a compliance claim.

## §1 Definitions

**Substrate class.**
- **S0** synthetic: this register does not apply.
- **S1** living-derived, inactive, non-neural.
- **S2** living, active, non-neural.
- **S3** living, active, neural or neural-lineage.
- **S4** human-derived, at any activity level: **permanently excluded from the estate's programme**.

**Interface class.**
- **I0** none.
- **I1** read-only, non-invasive.
- **I2** read-only invasive, or write-capable non-invasive.
- **I3** write-capable invasive: **permanently excluded from the estate's programme**.

**New roles.**
- **Custodian.** Duties survive deployment end, funding end and the institution's dissolution, and attach to the holding.
- **Welfare Reviewer.** Must not report to the Operator, must not be paid on yield, and must be free to publish an adverse finding. *A reviewer who can be dismissed by the reviewed is a document.*

**Extended Affected Party** includes **the substrate itself**, which has standing through §7.4 and not through consent.

**Estate limit, stated more strictly than this register states it.** The estate's own programme
undertakes **no wet-lab, cultivation, bench or organism work at any substrate class**
([MX-014](../corpus/mx-014-research-ethics-and-exclusions.md), [BX-011](bx-011-human-cell-exclusion.md)).
For MetaX itself, S1–S3 are therefore excluded by MX-014, not merely gated. This register's gates
G1–G3 describe what it requires of *other* licensees (RAT-37).

## §2 The Substrate Gate

Gating is by substrate and interface class only, never by volume. G0 (S0/I0): register inert.
G1 (S1/I1). G2 (S2/I2). G3 (S3). **GX (S4/I3): refused.** The preconditions are in ML-B-1.0 Part B-C.
**The eight refusals bind at every gate and do not scale in either direction.**

## §3 The precautionary asymmetry

> Where a substrate's moral status is genuinely uncertain and the injury under one interpretation
> would be irreversible, **the burden of proof falls on the party proposing to proceed.**

Basis: *sadd al-dharāʾiʿ*. **Cost, charged to this instrument:** delayed help to identifiable
people, and the possibility that unbound parties make the aggregate worse. This is contested at
**RAT-B-03**.
