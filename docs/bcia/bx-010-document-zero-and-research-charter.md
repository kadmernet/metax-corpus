# BX-010 · BCIA Document Zero and Research Charter

> **Authority.** `governing`, tier 3. It sits under [ML-2.3](../license/ml-2-3.md),
> [HX-000](../harms/hx-000-the-fourteen-structural-harms.md), the [Charter](../corpus/mx-000-charter.md)
> and [MX-014](../corpus/mx-014-research-ethics-and-exclusions.md). If this instrument and MX-014
> disagree, MX-014 prevails.

> **Disclosure first.** In the sources, **BCIA** is a bio-computational research programme. It is
> **under remediation**. Seven gates are open or unratified ([BX-012](bx-012-remediation-gates-and-public-status.md)).
> The existing files BX-000 to BX-009 use the same acronym for something unrelated, a reading
> model (Before · Contact · Internalisation · After). That clash is recorded as **DEF-037** and
> reviewed in [LEX-05](../lexicon/contested-terms.md). Until the Curator rules, this family uses
> **"BCIA-R"** for the research programme and **"BCIA-M"** for the reading model. No ethics reviewer
> is named, so **no research activity may begin** (§3).

## 1. Position

Document Zero is the first thing a reader of BCIA-R must see. It states what the programme is, what
it is not, and what must be true before any part of it becomes doctrine. The sources require that
Branch D may teach **toward** BCIA and may not present its notation, taxonomy or quantitative claims
as settled (00_BCIA_PUBLIC_STATUS).

## 2. Definition

**BCIA-R** is a theoretical and computational inquiry into bio-computational principle, drawn from the
ten Arabic books of Branch D (S38–S47, [REG-09 §3](../registries/reg-09-branch-registry.md)). It is
**not** a laboratory programme, not a clinical programme and not an engineering programme
([BX-011](bx-011-human-cell-exclusion.md)).

**Research activity** means any of the following: generating a hypothesis for testing, designing a
simulation meant to support a BCIA claim, publishing a result, or soliciting outside contribution
toward a result.

## 3. Mechanism — the charter before the experiment

Source MX-014 §14.2: *"No research activity commences before a published charter defining its scope,
its limits, and its ethics reviewer. … a charter written after the first result is a rationalisation."*

A research charter under this instrument has six fields, and none may be left blank:

| # | Field | Current value |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | Scope: the question, stated falsifiably | *(unwritten)* |
| C2 | Limits: the exclusions in [BX-011](bx-011-human-cell-exclusion.md), quoted verbatim | fixed |
| C3 | Ethics reviewer: a named person with no stake in the result | **unnamed** |
| C4 | Gates the activity depends on ([BX-012](bx-012-remediation-gates-and-public-status.md)) | — |
| C5 | Publication commitment (MX-014 exclusion 5: publish in full or do not research) | fixed |
| C6 | Kill criterion: the result that ends the line of inquiry | *(unwritten)* |

**The ethics veto** (source MX-014 §14.3). The named reviewer may block any publication on the
grounds of the exclusions, harm, misrepresentation of evidentiary status, or Charter §0.4. **There
is no appeal to the Curator.** Decisions are published with reasons in [REG-11](../registries/reg-11-determinations-registry.md)
or, if the Curator rules so, in a dedicated ethics log (RAT-28).

## 4. Worked instance

An agent proposes a lesson that says: "LPN is the bridging language between command and signal."
The C3 reviewer does not exist, so the ethics veto cannot run. The lesson may still be published as
teaching **toward** BCIA, provided it carries the panel *"What BCIA claims here, and what is not yet
settled"* and presents LPN as a "declared future BCIA language, specification pending"
([BX-014](bx-014-notation-freeze.md)). It may not state the role split as fact.

## 5. Failure modes

- **Charter-after-result.** The scope is written to fit a finding. Test: C1 and C6 are dated before the first activity.
- **Reviewer capture.** The reviewer is the Curator, or someone who depends on the Curator. That fails C3.
- **Acronym laundering.** Authority earned by BCIA-M is cited as though it belonged to BCIA-R, or the reverse (DEF-037).

## 6. Limits

This charter governs process only. It does not decide whether BCIA-R's hypotheses are true, and it
cannot settle any open gate.

## 7. Falsifier

This instrument has failed if any BCIA-R research activity is found dated before a complete C1–C6
charter, or if any ethics-veto decision exists without published reasons.

## 8. Obligation

- **Curator:** name the C3 reviewer, or declare that BCIA-R is dormant.
- **Agents:** refuse to generate BCIA-R research content while C3 is blank. Teaching-toward content is permitted under §4.

## Macro register

The failure here is **incrementalism**: small, defensible steps whose sum nobody chose. In ML-2.3
terms this is **HX-22, pre-emption of the successor**. *(Corrected 2026-09-30. The first draft said
"HX-24", copying MX-014 §1, but ML-2.3 §H.2.2 assigns HX-24 to the substitution of the interlocutor.
See CON-002 and DEF-040.)* The charter adds friction deliberately, and the reviewer supplies it. A
reviewer who is absent supplies none.

## 9. Document Zero v1.0: the seven binding bases *(merged from the v2027.1 bundle, GM-16 D-10)*

**MLT-09 · BCIA = Bio-Computational Intelligence Architecture.** *Founding question:* what can be
learned about computation from systems that compute without having been designed to, and what,
precisely, does that permit anyone to conclude?

| # | Basis | What it forbids | Held in |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The human-cell exclusion is absolute | Any work touching human neural tissue, cell lines, organoids or cultivation. No gate opens it | [BX-011](bx-011-human-cell-exclusion.md) |
| 2 | No laboratory instruction | Protocols, bench methods, cultivation, organism engineering, acquisition guidance | BX-011, [BX-018](bx-018-register-of-substrate-harms.md) A-6 |
| 3 | The refused inference | Arguing from silicon's limits to biology's promise | [BX-016](bx-016-refused-inference.md) |
| 4 | Notation is frozen | Teaching, extending or deriving from BSN/LPN/AMN | [BX-014](bx-014-notation-freeze.md) |
| 5 | Non-doctrinal posture | Presenting any position as settled while a gate is open | [BX-012](bx-012-remediation-gates-and-public-status.md) |
| 6 | **Disclosure is never gated** | Withholding status, audit, defects or open problems, for any reason including embarrassment | BX-012, [BX-015](bx-015-critical-audit-and-open-problems.md) |
| 7 | Charter before experiment | Any activity before scope, limits and ethics reviewer are published | §3 above |

**Governance.** **Cloister** (internal, quorum 3, may not publish) and **Atrium** (public-facing,
may publish only gate-conformant material). *Neither body has members.* A quorum of 3 cannot form in
a one-person estate. RAT-38 asks for members to be named or the bodies to be retired.

**The banned comparison.** The DishBrain-versus-DeepMind speed figure is prohibited across the
corpus. Where both are discussed, the page must say why they are not comparable.

**Promotion rule (from ML-B-1.0 Annex B, B.6).** No BCIA work is promoted beyond `research-preview`
while any remediation gate is open. Disclosure is never gated. Promotion is.

**Sunset.** BCIA is wound down if, after three annual cycles, Gate 3 is still unresolvable **and**
the Gate 6 sweep retracts more dated claims than it confirms. *A research programme that cannot fail
is not research.*
