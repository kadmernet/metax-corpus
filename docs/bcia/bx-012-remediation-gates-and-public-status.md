## 2. What Branch D may and may not say

**May say:** that silicon computation has physical, economic and epistemic limits; that non-binary,
fuzzy, biological, embodied and networked computation are legitimate scientific topics; and that BCIA
remains gated until its contradictions are remediated.

**May not say:** that BCIA's notation is settled; that its contested quantitative claims are verified;
or that any wet-lab, cultivation, bench, organism-engineering or human-cell work is part of the curriculum.

**Public-link policy.** Every Branch D series carries a panel titled **"What BCIA claims here, and
what is not yet settled"** that links to this page.
