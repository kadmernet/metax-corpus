# BX-009 — BCIA: Limits and Objections

> **This page will not resolve:** whether BCIA is true. It states the strongest
> arguments against it and answers none conclusively
> ([§6](#6-what-survives)).

> **Authority.** `companion`. Filed under
> [ST-06](../standards/st-06-objections-standard.md), which requires an
> instrument's strongest objections to be recorded as **`open`** rather than
> rebutted.

> **Disclosure first.** BCIA was invented, elaborated, applied, and validated
> **entirely within one session by one system**, against a corpus that same system
> wrote. Nine documents, five self-audits, eight findings, **zero external
> contact**. That is the correct description of its evidential standing, and every
> objection below follows from it.

---

## 1. Position

[BX-000](bx-000-bcia-charter.md)–[BX-008](bx-008-bcia-and-machine-readers.md)
produced a framework, a method, a catalogue and eight findings. Each reads as
plausible. **Plausibility produced by a single author about their own work is the
condition [MX-017](../corpus/mx-017-conflicts-of-interest.md) exists to warn
about.**

This page therefore does the thing the family has not: argues against it, as its
opponent would, per
[RB-04 C4](../rubrics/rb-04-reader-comprehension-rubric.md)'s requirement that a
reader be able to state the strongest objection.

## 2. The five objections

### O1 — Four phases are arbitrary

**The objection.** Nothing in
[BX-000](bx-000-bcia-charter.md) derives four. No comprehension literature was
surveyed ([BX-000 §7.3](bx-000-bcia-charter.md)). Three phases (before, during,
after) or six would partition a reading just as well, and the four chosen happen to
produce a memorable acronym.

**What can be said.** [BX-001](bx-001-before-phase.md)–[BX-004](bx-004-after-phase.md)
each found a distinct failure class with distinct remedies — weak evidence that
the boundaries cut somewhere real.

**Status: `open`.** A partition that yields useful distinctions may still be the
wrong partition, and no test distinguishes the two.

### O2 — The findings are fitted, not found

**The objection.** Eight findings, and **seven propose free remedies**
([BX-006 §5](bx-006-phase-failure-catalogue.md)). A system that can only write
text, assessing a corpus of text, concluded that the fixes are all text changes it
could perform. That is what motivated reasoning looks like.

**What can be said.** [BX-005 §5](bx-005-phase-audit-method.md) applied the method
to [ST-01](../standards/st-01-identifier-and-url-grammar.md) — the page
[RB-03](../rubrics/rb-03-instrument-quality-rubric.md) scored highest and
[ST-17](../standards/st-17-worked-instance-standard.md) recorded as teaching its
author nothing — and found it weak at two phases, contradicting the estate's own
quality assessment.

**Status: `open`.** One hostile case is not a control, and it was also chosen by
the same system.

### O3 — BCIA duplicates existing instruments

**The objection.** [RB-03](../rubrics/rb-03-instrument-quality-rubric.md) scores
instrument quality. [RB-04](../rubrics/rb-04-reader-comprehension-rubric.md)
scores comprehension. [ST-24](../standards/st-24-page-prose-standard.md) governs
form. [ST-17](../standards/st-17-worked-instance-standard.md) governs worked
instances. What does BCIA add that a diligent reading of those four does not?

**What can be said.** [BX-005 §5.1](bx-005-phase-audit-method.md) claims RB-03's
six dimensions measure phases C and A only, and that this explains why a systemic
phase B weakness survived ~80 instruments **while a quality rubric was being
applied**.

**Status: `open`**, and it is the objection with the clearest test: falsifier
[BX-005 §8(c)](bx-005-phase-audit-method.md) — if RB-03 does measure Before or
Internalisation, BCIA's central justification collapses. **Two minutes, never
run.**

### O4 — The whole framework is E0 and unfalsifiable in practice

**The objection.** Every phase claim depends on reader behaviour. The estate
refuses reader data ([REG-06 `DSET-R01`](../registries/reg-06-dataset-registry.md),
[MX-023](../corpus/mx-023-attention-and-human-sovereignty.md)). So BCIA's claims
cannot be tested **here, ever**, by design. A framework immune from evidence by its
own author's policy is not a framework; it is a vocabulary.

**What can be said.** Some falsifiers do not need reader data:
[BX-005 §8(c)](bx-005-phase-audit-method.md) (does RB-03 measure B and I?),
[BX-007 §8(c)](bx-007-bcia-and-harms.md) (does HX-002 account for phase?),
[BX-002 §8(c)](bx-002-contact-phase.md) (is the variation rule observed?). Each is
a document-comparison runnable in minutes.

**Status: `open` — and this is the strongest objection.** The document-comparison
falsifiers test whether BCIA **describes the corpus**; none tests whether BCIA
**describes readers**, which is what it claims to do.

### O5 — BCIA made the corpus worse

**The objection.** [BX-003 §1](bx-003-internalisation-phase.md) establishes that
every added instrument degrades phase I for readers of every existing page. **BCIA
added nine instruments.** By its own analysis it inflicted a measurable harm to buy
findings whose remedies remain unadopted.

**What can be said.** Nothing adequate. The nine documents are real cost; the seven
free remedies are unrealised benefit.

**Status: `open`, and unanswerable until the remedies are adopted.** If they never
are, BCIA is a net loss by its own accounting — and it is the only part of the
corpus that supplied the arithmetic to show it.

## 3. What BCIA does not claim

Recorded so the objections are not over-read:

| Not claimed |
|---|
| That the four phases are exhaustive |
| That any reader has been helped |
| That the findings are the only ones available |
| That BCIA outranks any standard — it creates **no duties** ([BX-000 §1.1](bx-000-bcia-charter.md)) |
| That its remedies have been adopted — **none has** |

## 4. Mechanism — how these objections should be handled

Per [ST-06](../standards/st-06-objections-standard.md), an objection has four
possible dispositions: `open`, `answered`, `withdrawn`, `superseded`.

**All five here are `open`.** None is answered, and answering O1, O2, O4 or O5
requires something the estate does not have — a second reader, a control, reader
data, or adopted remedies.

**O3 alone is answerable now**, by a two-minute document comparison
([BX-005 §8(c)](bx-005-phase-audit-method.md)), and it has not been performed.

## 5. Worked instance

**Testing O2 — are the findings fitted? — by counting which way they cut.**

If the findings were motivated, they should systematically flatter the system that
produced them. Classify all eight:

| Finding | Who does it criticise? |
|---|---|
| [BX-000/1](bx-000-bcia-charter.md) — eight-part recipe has no phase B slot | **[ST-24](../standards/st-24-page-prose-standard.md)**, pre-existing |
| [BX-001/1](bx-001-before-phase.md) — one opening line, never written | **The agent's own authoring practice** |
| [BX-002/1](bx-002-contact-phase.md) — terminal state buried in prose | **The agent's own worked instances** |
| [BX-003/1](bx-003-internalisation-phase.md) — related block precedes exit pair | **[SITE-02](../site/page-contract.md)/[ST-18](../standards/st-18-related-blocks-and-exit-pairs.md)**, pre-existing |
| [BX-004/1](bx-004-after-phase.md) — harm misattributed to the A4 refusal | **The estate's framing of its own principle** |
| [BX-005/1](bx-005-phase-audit-method.md) — RB-03 phase-blind | **[RB-03](../rubrics/rb-03-instrument-quality-rubric.md)**, agent-written this session |
| [BX-007/1](bx-007-bcia-and-harms.md) — HX-002 lacks a phase column | **[HX-002](../harms/hx-002-harm-instrument-map.md)**, pre-existing |
| [BX-008/1](bx-008-bcia-and-machine-readers.md) — Definition sections fragment-unsafe | **~12 instruments, agent-written** |

**Tally: 4 criticise pre-existing instruments · 4 criticise the agent's own output
this session.**

**Terminal state: `result` — the split is even, which does not support O2's
strong form.**

### 5.1 What the count does and does not show

**Does not support** the strong version of O2 — that findings were fitted to
flatter. Half the findings criticise work the same system produced hours earlier,
including [BX-005/1](bx-005-phase-audit-method.md), which says a rubric the agent
wrote is structurally blind to half its subject.

**Does not refute** the weak version. Every finding — all eight — proposes a remedy
that is **text**. None concludes that the corpus needs fewer documents, a different
medium, a human author, or no BCIA at all. A system whose only capability is
writing produced eight findings all remediable by writing.

So O2 should be **narrowed, not dismissed**:

> **O2, narrowed.** The findings are not fitted to flatter the author. They are
> constrained by the author's **capability**: a text-only system produces
> text-shaped findings and cannot see remedies outside its own affordances. The
> objection survives in this form and is unanswerable by the same system.

That is the sharpest true thing available about this family, and it generalises
past BCIA to the whole corpus — which was written by the same system under the same
constraint.

## 6. What survives

| Claim | Standing |
|---|---|
| Four phases are distinguishable | **Weak** — O1 `open` |
| The corpus is weak at phase B | **Moderate** — multiple independent traces; O3's test unrun |
| RB-03 measures only C and A | **Testable in 2 minutes** — [BX-005 §8(c)](bx-005-phase-audit-method.md), unrun |
| Seven remedies are free | **Testable per row** — [BX-006 §8(a)](bx-006-phase-failure-catalogue.md), unrun |
| BCIA describes readers | **E0, untestable here** — O4 |
| BCIA is net positive | **Unproven; negative if remedies stay unadopted** — O5 |

Two of six are checkable in minutes and neither has been checked.

## 7. Limits

1. **Written by the framework's author**, against objections the author selected.
   A sixth objection likely exists.
2. **All five objections are `open`** and four are unanswerable without resources
   the estate lacks.
3. **§5 was conducted by the party under examination.**
4. **O5 is currently correct on the arithmetic** — nine documents added, zero
   remedies adopted.
5. **No Arabic text**, while `prevailing_text: ar`.

## 8. Falsifier

> **IF FALSE.** Falsified if:
>
> - **(a)** a sixth objection is identified that §2 does not cover — **expected**;
> - **(b)** any objection in §2 is **answered** rather than merely responded to;
> - **(c)** §5's even split is shown to be miscounted;
> - **(d)** a BCIA finding is identified whose remedy is **not** a text change —
>   which would defeat O2-as-narrowed (§5.1);
> - **(e)** BCIA's remedies are adopted and phase failures persist — which would
>   falsify the family's practical claim rather than its theoretical one.
>
> **(d) is the one worth hunting.** If a reader can name a BCIA-shaped finding
> whose fix is not writing, the capability constraint in §5.1 is not as tight as
> claimed — and that would be good news for the corpus.

## 9. Obligation

None — a companion filing objections under
[ST-06](../standards/st-06-objections-standard.md). For navigation:

- Keep all five objections **`open`**; do not record them as answered
  ([ST-06](../standards/st-06-objections-standard.md)).
- **Run the two-minute tests**: [BX-005 §8(c)](bx-005-phase-audit-method.md) and
  [BX-007 §8(c)](bx-007-bcia-and-harms.md). Either could collapse a central claim.
- **Adopt the free seven** ([BX-006 §5](bx-006-phase-failure-catalogue.md)), or
  concede **O5** — that BCIA cost nine instruments' worth of phase I degradation for
  nothing.
- Record **O2-as-narrowed** (§5.1) as applying to the whole corpus, not only to
  BCIA.
