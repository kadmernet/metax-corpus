# ML-B-1.0 · The Maher License, BCIA Profile

> **Authority.** **None until ratified.** This is a profile under ML-2.3 §E.6: distinctly named, with
> its modifications stated, **not weakening §C.9 or §H.3**, and to be registered at `/profiles/`.
> Its base, ML-2.4, is itself a draft. There are **zero** deployments.

> **Disclosure first.**
> - (1) The **inverted grant** has not been tested in litigation in any jurisdiction (DEF-2026-069).
> - (2) Its register, BX-018, rests largely on Tier 3–4 evidence (DEF-2026-065). That is permanent until the 2029-06 re-derivation.
> - (3) **§B-E.3 binds the estate more narrowly than the estate already binds itself.** It excludes S4 and I3 only. [MX-014](../corpus/mx-014-research-ethics-and-exclusions.md) and [BX-011](../bcia/bx-011-human-cell-exclusion.md) exclude **all** wet-lab, cultivation and bench work for *every* substrate. Read alone, the profile would let the estate's own programme reach S1–S3 at G1–G3. **MX-014 prevails** (tier 3 binds the estate's conduct; a lower-tier text may be stricter, never laxer), and this note is binding on reproduction (RAT-37, DEF-052).

## Part B-A · Application, definitions, precedence

- **§B-A.1 Application.** Any Work whose Deployment involves substrate class **S1+** or interface class **I1+** (BX-018 §1). Otherwise the base licence applies alone.
- **§B-A.2 Definitions.** Living and living-derived substrate, neural interface, S0–S4, I0–I3, Custodian, Welfare Reviewer, and the extended Affected Party are as defined in BX-018 §1.
- **§B-A.3 Precedence.** On shared apparatus (evidence, defect format, roles, cure, notices) HX-000 and the base licence govern. On substrate-specific matter this profile governs. **Where they conflict irreducibly, the reading more protective of the Affected Party prevails**, and the conflict is filed.
- **§B-A.4 Method withholding.** No procedure, protocol, parameter, specification, sourcing route or containment information appears here, and none may be added. A request to add such detail "for credibility" is logged as a breach request.
- **§B-A.5** This is not clinical guidance.

## Part B-B · The inverted grant

- **§B-B.1 Nothing is granted by default.** No permission arises until the preconditions of the applicable gate are met **and recorded**.
- **§B-B.2 Why.** The base licence assumes a person who can object, appeal and leave. This subject may be unable to do any of these, or may have had the capacity to object altered by the Deployment. *Where the subject cannot object, a grant that persists until objection persists indefinitely. Silence is not consent.*
- **§B-B.3 The cost, stated.** The inverted grant will delay helpful work, and it cedes the unbound field to parties under no instrument at all. Both objections are strong. The position that the asymmetry is worth its price is **contested** (RAT-B-03). **Any reproduction of §B-B.1 must include this paragraph.**
- **§B-B.4 Patent.** A licence limited to the Work as Deployed under a satisfied gate. It ends with the grant.
- **§B-B.5** There is no grant by estoppel, by course of dealing, or by undetected use.

## Part B-C · The Substrate Gate (the Scale Gate does not apply)

*One vessel of neural tissue is not a thousandth of the moral problem. It is the whole problem at
small volume.*

| Gate | Trigger | Preconditions to any grant (cumulative) |
|---|---|---|
| G0 | S0 / I0 | Profile inert |
| **G1** | S1 or I1 | Refusals A-1…A-8 · provenance chain (§B-H.4) · metabolic accounting (§B-H.18) · funded custody plan with named successor (§B-H.23) · method withholding (§B-H.19) · where I1+: attribution record (§B-H.9), read minimisation (§B-H.6), non-conditioning (§B-H.14) |
| **G2** | S2 or I2 | G1 · welfare register (§B-H.1) · interiority assessment (§B-H.3) · independent Welfare Reviewer · removability and escrow (§B-H.8) · baselines (§B-H.10, §B-H.11) · scope register (§B-H.15) · outcome-verified containment (§B-H.20) · unenhanced-path measurement (§B-H.24) |
| **G3** | S3 | G2 · precautionary handling with a published position and its uncertainty (§B-H.21) · external unannounced verification · funded custody surviving insolvency with an automatic wind-down trigger · a published sunset condition |
| **GX** | S4 or I3 | **No grant exists** (§B-E.3) |

- **§B-C.3 Recording.** A precondition counts only if it is in the Compliance File (B-1), dated, and attributed to a named natural person. A record made after the fact evidences nothing.
- **§B-C.4 The necessity doorway (*ḍarūra*) for A-2 only.** All nine elements are required:
  1. An immediate threat to life or of irreversible severe injury.
  2. No consented alternative available in time.
  3. The person cannot consent at the material time **and there is no prior refusal on record. A prior refusal binds.**
  4. The minimum act needed.
  5. **No retention.**
  6. **No secondary inference.**
  7. **No scope expansion on recovery.** Consent to continue, obtained during or shortly after the intervention, is void under A-4.
  8. Recorded within 24 hours.
  9. Independently reviewed within 30 days, with the review published in summary.
- **§B-C.5** A-1 and A-3…A-8 admit **no** exception of any kind.

## Part B-D · Notices

- **§B-D.1** Disclose: substrate and interface class; gate; Welfare Reviewer and the basis of their independence; Custodian; forum, retention and service standards; and the location of the Compliance File.
- **§B-D.2** Interface-mediated acts are disclosed in terms the person can act on.
- **§B-D.3** Claiming compliance with this profile without a satisfied gate is itself a **condition** breach.

## Part B-E · Governance

- **§B-E.1 Steward.** MetaX.Academy (Curator Maher), subject to ML-2.4 §H.33.
- **§B-E.2 Amendment.** Refusals may be narrowed **only** by the Track 2 procedure, and **only** on the falsifier in the corresponding BX-018 entry. Commercial pressure is never a ground.
- **§B-E.3 Permanent exclusion (binds the estate).** S4 and I3. No gate, no route, and no amendment may create one. *Read with the disclosure note above: MX-014 excludes more.*
- **§B-E.4 Dissent channel** that does not pass through the steward, published at first issue with the receiver named. **Not yet established (DEF-053).**
- **§B-E.5 Withdrawal** if (a) moral status is resolved contrary to the refusals; (b) the profile functions as a competitive barrier (HX-18 applied to itself); or (c) §B-E.4 cannot be maintained.
- **§B-E.6 Re-derivation** of BX-018 at 2029-06, or at the first S2+/I1+ Deployment, whichever comes first.

## Part B-F · Standing, forum, remedy

- **§B-F.1 Standing for the voiceless.** The Welfare Reviewer, any prior Custodian, any person in the provenance chain or their successors, or a recognised petitioner. Inability to consent is never a bar to standing.
- **§B-F.2 Forum and retention** as in ML-2.4 §H.32. For interfaces, the presumed claim horizon is the person's lifetime.
- **§B-F.3** A refusal breach terminates without cure. A precondition failure means **no grant ever existed**. Other breaches follow the base licence's cure structure.
- **§B-F.4 Survival.** Custody, records, forum and A-8 survive insolvency and dissolution. **Custody attaches to the holding, not the holder.**
- **§B-F.5 Tie-break.** As in ML-2.4 §H.3(a)(ix) and BX-018 §7.6: the person's deletion request prevails unless a claim is filed, in which case the record goes to escrow.

## Part B-G · Interpretation

- **§B-G.1** Ambiguity is resolved for the Affected Party.
- **§B-G.2** Where an element of a refusal is arguably absent, the refusal is treated as engaged, and the burden of showing absence is on the Licensee.
- **§B-G.3 The refusals do not sever.** Unenforceability at law in a jurisdiction does not release a Licensee there.
