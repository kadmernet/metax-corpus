# Exhibits and Notices

> **Authority.** `operative-form`. These are the notices a licensee copies. The licence governs
> their meaning. Bracketed fields are fill-ins, and a notice published with an unfilled bracket
> is **incomplete**, not a placeholder ([B-07](../00-governance-map/launch-blockers.md)).

> **Disclosure first.** Every notice below points at a Registry URL and a key fingerprint that do
> not yet exist ([ML-OPS-1](registry-and-mirrors.md)). Until they do, the estate itself cannot
> publish these notices in complete form.

## Mechanism note (meta)

Notices are the only part of a licence most users ever read. A notice that omits the unusual
features (Part C, §C.9, Part H) produces licensees who never knew the conditions existed — which
under §C.6's knowledge standard is also a defence for them. **Exhibit 2 is therefore longer than
convention on purpose**: it forecloses the "nobody told me" defence by telling.

## Falsifier

Wrong if a licensee copying Exhibit 2 or 3 verbatim can later credibly claim not to have been told
of Part C, §C.9, §C.10 or Part H.
