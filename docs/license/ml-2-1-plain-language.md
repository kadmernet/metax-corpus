# The Maher License, in plain words

> **This page governs nothing.** It is a reader's edition. The licence is
> [ML-2.3](ml-2-3.md). If this page and the licence disagree, the licence is right and this page
> has a defect — please report it.

> **Why the file name says 2.1.** Nine other documents link to this address. Moving it would break
> them. The content is for **version 2.3**.

---

## What you may do

- **Read, copy, share, and study** anything under this licence.
- **Change it** and share your changed version — if you say clearly that you changed it, what you
  changed, and that you are not the original author.
- **Use it to train AI systems.** That is allowed.
- **Criticise it**, audit it, and try to prove it wrong. That is encouraged.
- **Use it in a business**, including one that charges money.

## What you must do

- **Keep the notice** saying it is under ML-2.3, with the link.
- **Say who wrote it**, and never present the original author as the author of your changes.
- **Do not reuse the original version number** for your version.
- If your AI system was trained on it, **do not hide that** where your system shows its sources,
  and do not let the system claim to speak for the author.

## What you must not do

- **Do not use it in lending with interest, or other arrangements the licence calls "Core
  Riba."** This is the one refusal that cannot be waived by anyone, ever. (The exact test is in
  §C.9 and has four parts, all of which must be present. Ordinary payments, fixed prices and
  ordinary bank accounts are **not** caught.)
- **Do not use it to cause the kinds of harm listed in Part H** — for example, systems built to
  capture attention against people's interests, or to make people unable to leave. Small
  publishers have lighter duties than large platforms (the "Scale Gate").
- **Eight things are forbidden at every size.** They are listed in §H.3(a) and are not
  paraphrased here, because a paraphrase of an absolute rule is how absolute rules get weakened
  ([ST-09](../standards/st-09-summary-and-abstract-standard.md)).
- **Do not claim a trademark** over the terms this work introduced and then use it against other
  people who use the work.

## What happens if you break it

Some rules are **conditions**: break one and your permission ends automatically. Others are
**promises** with a chance to fix things (usually 30 or 90 days). Which is which is set out in
§F.4 and in the [enforceability table](enforceability-two-track-table.md).

## Honest warnings

- **No lawyer has reviewed this licence.** Some parts may not hold up in some courts.
- **It is not "open source"** in the official (OSI) sense, and it says so.
- **The official online copy does not exist yet**, so you cannot yet check a copy's fingerprint.
