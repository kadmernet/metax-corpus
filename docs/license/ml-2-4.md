> licensed "under ML-2.3" is not affected by this text unless its holder opts in under §E.3.

> - (1) **Unsigned.** No key exists.

## §1 Form and scope

**§1.1 Why a delta.** ML-2.3 is relied upon. A restatement would reopen every settled clause and
break citations. ML-2.3 holders read the two documents together.

**§1.2 Unchanged.** Parts A, B, C (including Core Riba §C.9), D, F and G, and duties §H.4–§H.30 and
§H.31 (Interaction), continue in their ML-2.3 form. Where this instrument is silent, ML-2.3 governs.

**§1.3 What changes.**
- Three duties are added: **§H.32**, **§H.33**, **§H.34** [RENUMBERED from §H.31].
- One absolute is added: **§H.3(a)(ix)**.
- §H.28 is amended to carry the migration obligation.
- Part E gains **§E.10** register migration [RENUMBERED from §E.6] and **§E.11** withdrawal [RENUMBERED from §E.7].
- The register reference moves from HX-000 v2026.10.1 to **v2027.1** (28 harms, 7 strata, 9 absolutes).

**§1.4 Counting.** No clause states a harm count as a literal number. It says "the harms of the
register".

## §2 Two-track adoption

**Track 1 (ordinary):** §H.33, §H.34 and the §H.28 amendment. Effective 2027-04-01, transition to
2027-09-30, during which non-compliance is recorded but is not breach.

**Track 2 (absolute):** §H.32 and §H.3(a)(ix). The steps are:
1. Publication with the evidentiary basis (2027-04-01).
2. A comment period of at least 90 days, with all comments published along with the reason for each rejection (2027-04-01 → 2027-06-30).
3. An operative statement of what becomes prohibited.
4. A statement of who is disadvantaged and by how much.
5. Ratification and the decision record (2027-07-01).
6. Effective 2027-10-01; transition to 2028-03-31.

*An instrument that adds absolutes by the procedure it uses for covenants has made the harshest
remedy the cheapest to enact.*

**§2.3 Staging.** The register recommends adopting §H.33 first, because the estate is HX-28's
clearest instance. That recommendation is not binding. A contrary decision is recorded at §7.

## §3 New and amended clauses

### §H.3(a)(ix) · Record destruction defeating a claim *(absolute; Track 2)*
> No Licensee shall destroy, fail to retain, or render inaccessible any record where the effect is to defeat a foreseeable claim by an Affected Person, whether or not that effect was the purpose.
> **Elements, cumulative:** (i) the record exists or existed within the §H.32 retention period; (ii) it is destroyed, not retained, or rendered inaccessible; (iii) the claim was foreseeable at the time; (iv) the effect is to defeat or materially impair that claim.
> **Foreseeable** means a reasonable operator would have anticipated the *class* of claim. Notice of the specific claim is not required.
> **Not caught:** deletion at the Affected Person's own request where no claim is filed; deletion required by law; deletion under a published, uniformly applied schedule that predates foreseeability. **Tie-break:** where a deletion request conflicts with a filed claim, the record goes to a neutral escrow holder (stated identically in [BX-018 §7.6](../bcia/bx-018-register-of-substrate-harms.md)).
> **Remedy:** condition. Automatic termination, no cure.

### §H.32 · Forum, aggregation, retention and service standards *(covenant; Track 2; carries §H.3(a)(ix))*
> Publish, free to find and to read:
> - (a) **Forum** and jurisdiction. It must be reachable, and the burden of showing reachability is on the Licensee.
> - (b) **Aggregation**. If aggregation is prohibited, publish annual counts of individual claims brought and resolved.
> - (c) **Retention**. Not shorter than the longest foreseeable claim horizon. If that horizon is undetermined, retain until it is determined.
> - (d) **Service standards**, with **actual performance**, including the count of matters neither decided nor refused.
>
> Tier 1: (a), (c). Tier 2: all. Tier 3: all, plus external verification. **Remedy:** covenant, 30 days to cure. A retention breach that meets §H.3(a)(ix) is a condition.

### §H.33 · Succession, decision record, dissent, external finding *(covenant; Track 1)*
> Where the Licensee is also steward of a relied-upon instrument:
> - (a) A **succession instrument** with a named successor, a trigger, and the successor's **written acceptance**.
> - (b) A **published decision record** for every amendment, including objections.
> - (c) A **dissent channel that does not route through the steward**, naming its receiver and stating that receiver's independence.
> - (d) At Tier 2 and above, an **external compliance finding** by a party the steward cannot dismiss for its content.
>
> Tier 0/1: (a), (b). Tier 2/3: all. **No Licensee may cite compliance as evidence that HX-28 is remedied** (RAT-33). **Remedy:** covenant, 90 days.

### §H.34 · Unnamed-harm review *(covenant; Track 1)* [RENUMBERED from draft §H.31]
> Annually, either (a) record at least one harm encountered that the register lacks, described so a third party could assess it, **or** (b) state that none was found and describe a search that would have found one. A (b) statement with no search described is a nullity.
> Tier 0: one internal paragraph. Tier 1: dated and authored. Tier 2: published. Tier 3: published, with the method named and Affected Persons' submissions invited.
> The steward publishes every submission, including declined ones, with reasons. **The register is a floor. Absence from it is never authority for any act.** **Remedy:** covenant, 30 days.

### §H.28 · amended (migration obligation added)
> Where the Licensee is steward of a register or governing instrument, the custody plan includes a **migration obligation**: re-express the instrument in each successor medium, encoding and language; retain every prior expression unaltered; record each migration in a **Migration Ledger** (date, actor, source and target forms, digests of both). *An unlogged copy is indistinguishable from a forgery.*

### §E.10 · Register migration [RENUMBERED from draft §E.6]
> The steward maintains HX-000 in a form independent of any renderer, font, script, network service or resolving address. Every reference degrades to a citation that is meaningful when the link is dead. **No obligation depends on an address resolving.**

### §E.11 · Withdrawal of this instrument [RENUMBERED from draft §E.7]
> This License is withdrawn in its entirety, and the withdrawal published, if: (a) it is shown to function principally as a barrier to entry (HX-18 applied to itself); (b) the steward cannot maintain the §H.33(c) dissent channel; or (c) the register's principal claims are falsified and the narrowing required by §E.5 is not published. These conditions are stated at adoption so that withdrawal never needs the consent of the party it would embarrass.

### §E.3-bis · Transition from ML-2.2 *(new; proposed by the intake, GM-16 F-01)*
> ML-2.2 was never adopted and no Work was licensed under it. ML-2.3 §E.3's second sentence has no referent and is struck for Works licensed under this version. *[Not in the source draft. Marked as an intake proposal for the Curator to accept or strike.]*

## §7 Decision record (§H.33(b): this instrument's first act of compliance)

**What changed.** The changes listed at §1.3. **Why.** Review of *Repairing the Machine* found three
missing harms (HX-26, HX-27, HX-28), and the third is instanced in the estate. **Who decided.** The
steward, alone, which is HX-28 itself (DEF-2026-071). **Who objected.** Nobody could have: **no
dissent channel existed** through which to lodge an objection to §H.33(c)'s own adoption. Comments
received during the Track 2 window will be applied retrospectively. **Rejected alternatives.**
Harms with no duties (that would be HX-15 in pure form). §H.34 at Tier 2 only (the enumeration
ceiling bites hardest on small actors). §H.33 as an absolute (a missing successor is a
counterparty's failure). Deferring to ML-3.0. **The steward's own recorded dissent.** §H.34 is the
first *recurring* Tier 0 obligation, which weakens the claim that Tier 0 duties take an afternoon.
**Intake addition (2026-09-30).** The four renumberings and §E.3-bis were made by the intake agent,
not by the steward. They await the steward's confirmation.
