# GM-20 · Decision Record Register

> **Authority.** A **record**, outside the precedence ladder. It lists every amendment decision on a
> relied-upon instrument (ML-x, HX-000, BX-018, the Charter). For each it states **who decided, who
> objected, and whether anyone could have objected.** ML-2.4 §H.33(b) (pending) requires this register.
> The estate keeps it now, because the estate is HX-28's clearest instance.

> **Disclosure first.** Every decision below was taken by **one person, or by an agent awaiting that
> person's confirmation**. No row records an objection, because **no channel existed through which one
> could be lodged** (DEF-053). This register therefore shows the absence of dissent. It does not
> show agreement.

---

## 1. Form of a row

`DR-{instrument}-{YYYY}-{NN}` · instrument and clauses · what changed · why · **decider** (natural
person, or `agent → awaiting C`) · objections (count and ids, or `no channel`) · rejected
alternatives · the steward's own dissent · effective date · the CPF-1 period that preceded it, where one applies.

## 3. What is missing for this register to discharge §H.33(b)

1. **A dissent channel** whose receiver is not the steward (DEF-053, GM-17 M-05). Without one, the "Objections" column has no source.
2. **Curator confirmation** of every `agent → awaiting C` row. Until it is given, those rows record proposals, not decisions.
3. For future Track 2 decisions, **a completed [CPF-1](cpf-1-comment-period-publication-format.md) period** linked to the row.

## 4. Falsifier

The register is incomplete if any amendment to ML-2.x, HX-000, BX-018 or MX-000 exists in the
[changelog](../00-governance-map/changelog.md) or the [version history](version-history.md) without a row here.
