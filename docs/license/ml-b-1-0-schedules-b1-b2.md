# ML-B-1.0 · Schedules B-1 and B-2

> **Authority.** These are schedules of a **draft** profile, so they have no force until ML-B-1.0 is ratified.
> **The estate cannot use them for its own programme.** [MX-014](../corpus/mx-014-research-ethics-and-exclusions.md)
> and [BX-011](../bcia/bx-011-human-cell-exclusion.md) exclude all wet-lab, cultivation and bench
> work at every substrate class, so MetaX's own answer at B-2 step 1 is always "the estate's programme
> may not proceed" (DEF-052, RAT-37). These schedules describe what the profile requires of **other**
> Licensees.

> **Disclosure first.**
> - (1) **B-2 is reconstructed.** The source points to "Part 3 §3", and that part is absent from the held text. This is filed as DEF-064 (new in this programme's series; see §6).
> - (2) **The Welfare Reviewer competence may not exist** as a recognised qualification (source DEF-2026-067, Family B DEF-2026-076). A G2+ file may therefore be impossible to complete today. That is the inverted grant working as designed: *no record, no grant.*
> - (3) **Method withholding.** Every field below records *that* something was done, *who* did it and *when*. None records *how*. A field that tempts an entrant to write *how* is a drafting defect. Report it.

### B-1.4 Gate preconditions (cumulative, §B-C table)

Tick only what is **recorded**. Each row needs the date, the named person and the evidence reference.

**G1 (S1 or I1)**
- [ ] Refusals A-1…A-8 reviewed (B-1.3 complete)
- [ ] Provenance chain (§B-H.4): every transfer from origin, with each holder named
- [ ] Metabolic accounting with its boundary stated (§B-H.18)
- [ ] Funded custody plan with a **named successor and the successor's written acceptance** (§B-H.23)
- [ ] Method-withholding confirmation (§B-H.19): a statement that no method-level detail is published or held in public artefacts
- [ ] *If I1+:* attribution record (§B-H.9) · read minimisation (§B-H.6) · non-conditioning (§B-H.14)

**G2 (S2 or I2): all of G1, plus**
- [ ] Welfare register (§B-H.1)
- [ ] Interiority assessment (§B-H.3), with its uncertainty stated
- [ ] **Independent Welfare Reviewer** appointed. The reviewer does not report to the Operator, is not paid on yield, and is free to publish an adverse finding. Record the basis of independence
- [ ] Removability and escrow (§B-H.8)
- [ ] Baselines (§B-H.10, §B-H.11)
- [ ] Scope register (§B-H.15)
- [ ] Containment verified **by outcome**, not by design document (§B-H.20)
- [ ] Unenhanced-path measurement (§B-H.24)

**G3 (S3): all of G2, plus**
- [ ] Precautionary handling with a **published** position and its uncertainty (§B-H.21)
- [ ] External, **unannounced** verification: verifier, date, and the published finding
- [ ] Funded custody that **survives insolvency**, with an automatic wind-down trigger
- [ ] A published sunset condition

**GX (S4 or I3):** no precondition exists, and no grant exists (§B-E.3).

### B-1.8 Retention and survival (closes source DEF-2026-079 for the profile)

The file is retained for the **longest** of: the CF-1 §8 period; the presumed claim horizon in B-1.6; and the life of the holding plus the longest foreseeable claim horizon. **Custody of the file attaches to the holding, not the holder** (§B-F.4). It survives insolvency and dissolution. Destroying it while a claim is foreseeable is **A-8**.

## B-2 · Substrate Gate worksheet *(reconstructed from Part B-C; see Disclosure 1)*

Work through the steps in order. **Stop** at the first "stop".

| Step | Question | If yes | If no |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Is the applicant **MetaX's own programme** (MLT-09 or any other)? | **Stop.** MX-014 excludes all wet-lab, cultivation and bench work at every class. Literature synthesis, formal modelling, simulation and ethics only (BX-011, BX-017). | Go to 1 |
| 1 | Does the Deployment involve human-derived material at any activity level (S4)? | **Stop: GX.** No grant exists. | 2 |
| 2 | Does it involve a write-capable invasive neural interface (I3)? | **Stop: GX.** | 3 |
| 3 | Living, active, neural or neural-lineage substrate (S3)? | Gate **G3** | 4 |
| 4 | Living, active, non-neural (S2), or I2? | Gate **G2** | 5 |
| 5 | Living-derived, inactive, non-neural (S1), or I1? | Gate **G1** | 6 |
| 6 | Synthetic substrate only (S0) and no interface (I0)? | **G0**: the profile is inert, and the base licence applies alone (§B-A.1) | Re-classify. One of 1–5 must apply |
| 7 | Is any refusal A-1…A-8 **engaged or arguably engaged** (§B-G.2)? | **Stop.** No cure is possible (§B-F.3) | 8 |
| 8 | Are **all** preconditions for the gate (B-1.4) recorded, dated and attributed? | Grant subsists **from the date of the last record** | **No grant exists.** A precondition failure means no grant ever existed, so there is nothing to cure (§B-F.3) |

**Note on volume.** No step asks "how much". The Scale Gate does not apply. *One vessel of neural tissue is not a thousandth of the moral problem.*
