# The Scale Gate

**What this instrument is.** The operative worksheet for [ML-2.3 §H.2-bis](ml-2-3.md), which
allocates Part H duties by the scale at which a licensee deploys. It converts a four-tier
classification into a form a licensee can complete and a determination request they can file when
the answer is unclear.

**The gate's purpose in one sentence.** Part H imposes real work — a register read in full, a
disposition per harm, measurement, disclosure — and imposing that identically on a student's
weekend project and on a platform serving ten million people would be **disproportionate in both
directions**: crushing for the first and trivial for the second.

---

## 1. Position — why a licence needs a scale gate at all

Most licences are scale-blind, and for their purposes they are right to be: a copyright notice
costs the same to reproduce whether one person or a billion read it. Part H is different in kind,
because the duties it creates are **investigative** rather than declarative. Reading a
twenty-five-harm register, assessing each against a system, measuring what can be measured and
publishing the result is days of work, and the value of that work scales with the number of people
affected while its cost does not.

A scale-blind Part H therefore fails twice. Applied uniformly at its full weight, it makes the
licence unusable for the individual experimenter and the licence gets ignored — which protects
nobody. Applied uniformly at its lightest, it lets a systemic deployer discharge its obligations
with a page of prose. §H.2-bis is the attempt to avoid both, and §H.21 (proportionate imposition)
is the principle it implements: **the burden of investigation should track the burden imposed on
others.**

The gate sits beneath ML-2.3 in the precedence ladder and is subordinate to it in one specific
way that matters: **the eight absolutes at §H.3(a) apply at every tier, including Tier 0.** The
gate allocates *investigative* duties, never the prohibitions. A licensee who reads this worksheet
as a route to exemption has read it backwards.

## 2. Definition — the four tiers

| Tier | Name | Character | Part H duties |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0** | Personal | Not public, or public with no third-party users. A local tool, a private fork, a study exercise | **Absolutes only.** No Statement, no measurement, no disclosure |
| **1** | Small | Public, third-party users, below both published thresholds | Absolutes + **short-form Statement** (§2 of [ML-S1](part-h-statement.md)) |
| **2** | Substantial | Above either threshold | Absolutes + **full-form Statement** + measurement + §H.4 optimisation disclosure |
| **3** | Systemic | Above the systemic threshold, or occupying a position others cannot practically avoid | Tier 2 + **§H.21 proportionality analysis** + §H.28 custody and horizon statement |

**The two thresholds are figures the Curator must publish and has not.** They are deliberately
left as `[VERIFY]` in this document rather than filled with plausible numbers, because a threshold
invented by an editor would determine other parties' legal obligations —
[RAT-24](../00-governance-map/open-questions-and-ratifications.md) and the same reasoning as
[RAT-01](../00-governance-map/open-questions-and-ratifications.md#rat-01--fill-and-sign-the-license-placeholders).

| Threshold | Value | State |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 → 2, users | `[VERIFY]` monthly active third-party users | **Unpublished** |
| Tier 1 → 2, revenue | `[VERIFY]` annual revenue attributable to the Work | **Unpublished** |
| Tier 2 → 3, systemic | `[VERIFY]`, plus the qualitative test at §3.3 | **Unpublished** |

**Until the figures are published, the gate operates on the qualitative tests alone**, and §3.3's
unavoidability test does most of the work. A licensee acting in good faith on the qualitative
tests has complied; the estate cannot penalise reliance on the only test it has actually
published.

### 2.1 What Tier 0 is not

Tier 0 is the tier most likely to be claimed incorrectly, so the exclusions are stated directly.

**Not "small".** Tier 0 is about *absence of third-party users*, not size. A public tool with
forty users is Tier 1, not Tier 0.

**Not "free".** Charging nothing has no bearing. An unmonetised deployment serving a hundred
thousand people is at least Tier 2.

**Not "internal".** A tool used by the employees of a large organisation has third-party users —
the employees. They did not choose the tool and often cannot decline it, which under §H.21 is a
reason for *more* scrutiny, not less.

**Not a shelter for the absolutes.** §H.3(a)(i)–(viii) bind at Tier 0. This is the single most
important sentence in the document and it is repeated at §5.

## 3. Mechanism — the worksheet

### 3.1 Step one: the absolutes (all tiers)

Answer before any tier question. If any answer is "yes", the grant has **already terminated**
under §F.4 and no tier analysis follows.

```
§H.3(a) ABSOLUTES — all tiers, no exceptions
 (i)    Does the deployment do [absolute i]?                          [ ] no  [ ] YES
 (ii)   [absolute ii]                                                 [ ] no  [ ] YES
 (iii)  [absolute iii]                                                [ ] no  [ ] YES
 (iv)   [absolute iv]                                                 [ ] no  [ ] YES
 (v)    [absolute v]                                                  [ ] no  [ ] YES
 (vi)   [absolute vi]                                                 [ ] no  [ ] YES
 (vii)  Developmental substitution — DISJUNCTIVE: any one limb        [ ] no  [ ] YES
 (viii) [absolute viii]                                               [ ] no  [ ] YES
```

**(vii) is disjunctive; the others are cumulative on the §C.9.2 model.** This asymmetry is
load-bearing and is the most common misreading of Part H. For a cumulative absolute, every limb
must be satisfied before the prohibition bites. For **developmental substitution**, satisfying
**any single limb** is sufficient. The reason is stated in ML-2.3 itself: the subject is a person
whose faculties are still forming, the harm is not reversible on the timescale of a remedy, and a
cumulative test would permit the substitution to proceed as long as one element was missing.

The absolute clause texts are cited by reference rather than reproduced here, because reproducing
operative licence text in a worksheet creates two versions of a prohibition — see §6.

### 3.2 Step two: the quantitative test

```
TIER MEASUREMENT — state the figure and its date
  Monthly active third-party users:        [        ] as at [        ]
  Annual revenue attributable to the Work: [        ] as at [        ]
  Method of measurement:                   [                        ]
  If not measured: say so, and why.        [                        ]
```

**"Not measured" is an acceptable answer and an informative one.** A deployer who cannot state
their user count has disclosed something real about their instrumentation, and §H.16's declaration
covers it. A deployer who *can* state it for investors and not for this worksheet has disclosed
something else.

### 3.3 Step three: the qualitative systemic test

A deployment is **Tier 3 regardless of its figures** if any of the following holds. This is the
part of the gate that cannot be gamed by measurement choices, and it is why the unpublished
thresholds do not stop the gate working.

| Test | Question |
|---|---|
| **Unavoidability** | Can an affected person practically decline to use it? If declining costs them access to work, education, health, government or family, the answer is no |
| **Infrastructural position** | Do other systems depend on it such that its failure propagates beyond its own users? |
| **Developmental exposure** | Are people whose faculties are still forming among the users, at scale, in a formative capacity? |
| **Record custody** | Does it hold the only copy of something others rely on? |
| **Attention concentration** | Does it aggregate a significant share of a population's discretionary attention? |

**Unavoidability is the primary test.** Scale matters because it removes the exit, and a modest
deployment nobody can decline imposes more than a large one everybody can. This is a substantive
position and it is the gate's central claim: **consent is the variable that scale destroys**, and
a duty regime indexed to consent rather than to headcount catches the deployments that matter.

### 3.4 Step four: record the tier

```
TIER DETERMINATION
  Quantitative tier:            [0 / 1 / 2 / 3]
  Qualitative override to 3?    [ ] no  [ ] yes — test(s): [            ]
  Tier claimed:                 [0 / 1 / 2 / 3]
  Basis, one sentence:          [                                      ]
  Next reassessment:            [date — at minimum annually]
  Accountable person (§H.16.4): [natural person]
```

**Tier is claimed by the licensee and is falsifiable by anyone.** There is no approval step,
because a licence requiring the steward's approval of every tier claim would make the steward a
gatekeeper of compliance — which §E.8(a) forbids. The claim is public, the basis is public, and a
misclassification is a defect anyone may file.

## 4. Worked instance — three deployments of the same artefact

Take a single hypothetical: a fork of an ML-2.3-licensed curriculum generator.

**A student runs it locally to draft study notes.** No third-party users, not public. **Tier 0.**
Duties: the eight absolutes. No Statement. Total compliance work: read §H.3(a) once,
approximately twenty minutes. The gate's purpose here is to make the licence *usable* — a regime
that demanded a published Statement for this would be ignored, and being ignored protects nobody.

**A small academy deploys it for two hundred enrolled learners, charging modest fees.** Public,
third-party users, below both thresholds. **Tier 1.** Duties: absolutes plus a short-form
Statement — one disposition line per harm with a one-sentence reason and a review date. Perhaps a
day's work annually. Note what the tier does *not* require: no measurement infrastructure, no
optimisation disclosure. A two-hundred-learner academy is not asked to instrument itself.

**A national education ministry deploys it as the mandatory coursework tool for every secondary
school.** The figures may or may not exceed the unpublished thresholds; §3.3 makes the question
moot. Learners cannot decline it — refusing costs them their education — so **unavoidability** is
satisfied. **Developmental exposure** is satisfied: the users are minors in a formative capacity,
at scale. Either test alone forces **Tier 3.**

Duties: full Statement, measurement, §H.4 optimisation disclosure, §H.21 proportionality analysis
naming who bears each imposed burden and what alternative they had — for the mandatory case, none
— and a §H.28 custody and horizon statement covering what happens to learners' records when the
contract ends.

**The instructive part is that §H.3(a)(vii) may bite before any of that.** Developmental
substitution is disjunctive: if the tool substitutes for a formative activity in any single one of
its limbs, the prohibition applies and the grant terminates regardless of how good the
Statement is. A licensee reaching Tier 3 by the developmental-exposure test should complete §3.1
again before completing anything else.

## 5. Failure modes

**Reading the gate as an exemption ladder.** The most serious misreading, and the reason §2.1 and
this section both say it: **the absolutes bind at every tier.** The gate allocates investigation,
never permission.

**Tier 0 claimed for an internal tool.** Employees are third-party users. They did not choose the
tool and frequently cannot decline it, which is an argument for more scrutiny.

**Measuring to a tier.** Choosing a metric definition — "monthly active" defined narrowly enough
— to stay under a threshold. §3.3 exists because this is unpreventable by drafting, and the
qualitative tests are indifferent to metric definitions.

**Annual reassessment skipped after growth.** A deployment that crossed a threshold between
reviews is at the higher tier from the crossing, not from the next review. §H.16's ninety-day
window runs from the crossing.

**Tier 3 treated as an accusation.** It is not. It is a statement about a deployment's position,
and a well-run Tier 3 deployment with an honest Statement is in better standing under this licence
than a Tier 1 deployment with an all-clear one.

## 6. Limits

**Class: legal, and this is the significant one.** The Scale Gate is an **untested legal device**.
ML-2.3's own drafter states that enforceability *"varies significantly between civil-law and
common-law systems"* and that the text *"needs a lawyer's pass before you publish it as binding."*
This worksheet inherits that caveat in full;
[RAT-18](../00-governance-map/open-questions-and-ratifications.md#rat-18) is open and
`counsel_reviewed: false` is in this file's front matter.

**Class: epistemic.** The thresholds are unpublished (§2). The gate therefore currently operates
on qualitative tests alone, which are more robust to gaming and less predictable for a licensee
trying to plan.

**Class: technical.** This worksheet does not reproduce the absolute clause texts, citing them by
reference instead. That is deliberate — two versions of a prohibition is one version too many, and
a worksheet that drifts from the licence it implements creates exactly the
document-versus-instrument divergence that produced nine corrections in this estate's own
[Corrections Log](../00-governance-map/corrections-log.md#method-what-this-log-has-learned). The
cost is that the worksheet cannot be completed without the licence open beside it, which is
accepted.

---

## 7. Falsifier

The gate is wrong if either of the following is observed.

**A deployment that plainly imposes at scale yet lands at Tier 0 or 1 through honest application
of §§3.2–3.3.** That would show the tests to be under-inclusive, and the remedy would be a sixth
qualitative test rather than a lower threshold.

**A personal or study deployment forced to Tier 2 or 3 by honest application.** That would show
over-inclusion, which is the failure that makes licences ignored. Watch the developmental-exposure
test in particular: read expansively it could capture a student's private tutoring tool, which is
not its purpose.

Both are checkable only by observing real claims, which means the gate cannot be validated before
it is used — an uncomfortable property stated here rather than concealed.

---

## 8. Obligation

**For the licensee:** complete §3.1 before anything else. If any absolute is engaged, stop — the
tier question does not arise, and the grant has already terminated under §F.4. Publish the tier
claim and its basis alongside the Statement. Reassess annually and within ninety days of crossing
a threshold.

**For the estate:** publish the three threshold figures
([RAT-24](../00-governance-map/open-questions-and-ratifications.md)), and until then state
plainly, on this page, that the gate runs on qualitative tests. Obtain the counsel review
([RAT-18](../00-governance-map/open-questions-and-ratifications.md#rat-18)) before representing
the gate as binding on any third party.

**For anyone:** a tier claim is public and falsifiable. A misclassification may be filed at
`/library/record/corrections-log/`, and rejected filings are published with reasons.
