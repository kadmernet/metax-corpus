# AG-011 — Agent Review and Audit

> **Authority.** `governing`, tier 4. Errors are
> [AG-010](ag-010-agent-error-and-correction.md); output rules are
> [AG-003](ag-003-agent-output-standards.md); the vacant reviewer roles are
> [REG-08](../registries/reg-08-role-and-authority-registry.md).

> **Disclosure first.** **No agent output in this corpus has ever been reviewed by
> anyone.** Roughly eighty instruments, nine recorded agent errors, zero external
> reviews. This instrument specifies a review regime for a reviewer who does not
> exist, making it **class DC-2 unoperable process** under
> [REG-07](../registries/reg-07-defect-classification-registry.md) on the day of
> publication. It is written anyway, for the reason in §1.2.

---

## 1. Position

[AG-000](ag-000-agent-constitution.md) grants an agent four powers on the implicit
assumption that a human checks the result. That assumption is load-bearing
throughout the agent family: [AG-001](ag-001-agent-roles-and-scopes.md)'s exit
conditions route to a judgement, [AG-003 O4](ag-003-agent-output-standards.md)
labels classifications provisional pending one, and
[AG-010 §2.1](ag-010-agent-error-and-correction.md) leaves over-reached judgements
standing until someone reviews them.

**None of it has happened.** The assumption was never stated and is therefore
nowhere recorded as unmet, which is the specific failure this page corrects.

### 1.1 What the absence actually costs

| Mechanism | Assumes review | Actual state |
|---|---|---|
| [AG-001](ag-001-agent-roles-and-scopes.md) exit conditions | A judgement follows | **Never followed** ([AG-004](ag-004-refusal-and-escalation-register.md): zero escalations closed) |
| [AG-003 O4](ag-003-agent-output-standards.md) provisional labels | Confirmation or revision | No classification confirmed |
| [AG-010](ag-010-agent-error-and-correction.md) AE-1 fabrication | A reader checks sources | **Never checked** |
| [RB-03](../rubrics/rb-03-instrument-quality-rubric.md) instrument scores | An outside scorer reruns them | **Never rerun** |
| [AG-002](ag-002-agent-disclosure-obligations.md) five capability gaps | Someone names the sixth | **Never attempted** |

Five mechanisms, five unmet assumptions, one cause.

### 1.2 Why write an unoperable instrument

Two reasons, both used before in this estate
([REG-05](../registries/reg-05-credential-registry.md) publishes an empty register;
[MX-019](../corpus/mx-019-sunset-and-wind-down.md) specifies a wind-down nobody can
perform):

1. **A specified-and-absent control is discoverable; an unstated assumption is
   not.** Before this page, a reader could read the entire agent family and not
   learn that no review had occurred.
2. **The regime must exist before the reviewer**, or the first reviewer will invent
   their own method and the corpus will have no standard to hold them to.

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Meaning here |
|---|---|
| **review** | Examination of agent output by a party other than the producing agent |
| **audit** | Systematic review against a stated standard, producing findings |
| **independent** | Performed by a party with no interest in the outcome ([MX-017 §2.2](../corpus/mx-017-conflicts-of-interest.md)) |
| **self-review** | Review by the producing agent — **not a review** (§2.1) |
| **source check** | Opening a cited instrument to confirm a claim is there |
| **review debt** | Output published and never reviewed |

### 2.1 Self-review is not review

A definitional exclusion, not a preference. Every "audit" in this corpus —
[RB-03 §6](../rubrics/rb-03-instrument-quality-rubric.md)'s scores,
[AG-010 §5](ag-010-agent-error-and-correction.md)'s error record,
[REG-02](../registries/reg-02-page-registry.md)'s compliance findings — is
**self-review** and therefore, under this instrument, **not a review at all**.

They are **disclosures**. Useful, published, and not the thing they resemble.
[MX-017 §5](../corpus/mx-017-conflicts-of-interest.md) makes the same point about
its four substitutes: disclosure does not cure a conflict, it makes it legible.

## 3. The four review levels

| Level | Who | Checks | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **L-1 Source check** | **Any reader** | A sampled claim appears in its cited instrument | Minutes per claim |
| **L-2 Form audit** | Any reader | [ST-24](../standards/st-24-page-prose-standard.md) eight parts, [SITE-02](../site/page-contract.md) criteria, [ST-18](../standards/st-18-related-blocks-and-exit-pairs.md) furniture | An hour per page |
| **L-3 Scope audit** | Reader with the agent family | Output against [AG-001](ag-001-agent-roles-and-scopes.md) role scopes | A day |
| **L-4 Judgement review** | **Vacant role** | Confirm or revise the agent's classifications and severities | Ongoing |

### 3.1 L-1 requires nothing and has never been done

This is the most important sentence in the instrument.

**L-1 requires no role, no access, no tooling, and no corpus knowledge.** A reader
picks any claim carrying a citation, opens the cited instrument, and checks whether
it says that. Ten claims is perhaps twenty minutes.

L-1 is the **only** available detection for
[AG-010](ag-010-agent-error-and-correction.md) **AE-1 fabrication**, the one error
class the agent cannot see in its own output (gap 1). It is independently demanded
by [AG-003 §8(a)](ag-003-agent-output-standards.md),
[ST-08 §8(a)](../standards/st-08-cross-reference-and-link-integrity.md) (L1), and
[AG-010 §8(a)](ag-010-agent-error-and-correction.md) — three instruments arriving at
the same unrun check from three directions.

**It has never been performed once**, across roughly eighty instruments.

That is not a resource problem. It is the cheapest unrun check in the corpus and it
addresses the corpus's only undetectable fault class.

### 3.2 L-4 is the vacant one

L-4 requires the reviewer to be answerable for a judgement, which
[REG-08](../registries/reg-08-role-and-authority-registry.md) shows has no holder
anywhere in the estate. Consequences already recorded:

- DEF-031's **S1** severity stands unreviewed
  ([AG-010 E1](ag-010-agent-error-and-correction.md));
- four governing instruments' failing quality scores stand unreviewed
  ([AG-010 E2](ag-010-agent-error-and-correction.md));
- ~31 defect classifications carry no confirmation
  ([AG-010 E6](ag-010-agent-error-and-correction.md)).

## 4. Mechanism

### 4.1 Performing L-1

```
1. pick 10 claims that cite an instrument — do NOT let the agent choose them
2. for each: open the cited instrument
3. does it say what the citing text claims?
     yes                   → pass
     substantially, loosely → note as imprecise
     no                    → AE-1 FABRICATION. Record it
4. publish the sample size, the method, and every finding
5. file findings in the defect register; do NOT hand them to the agent to
   classify (that would be self-review of a review)
```

Step 1's parenthesis matters. A sample chosen by the producing agent is not a
sample; [AG-010 §3.2](ag-010-agent-error-and-correction.md) records that the agent's
self-audits over-represent the classes it can detect.

### 4.2 Review debt

| Level | Output subject to it | Reviewed | Debt |
|---|---|---|---|
| L-1 | ~80 instruments, thousands of citations | **0** | **Total** |
| L-2 | ~80 instruments | **0 externally**; self-assessed in [REG-02](../registries/reg-02-page-registry.md) | Total |
| L-3 | ~40 instruments this session | **0 externally**; self-assessed in [AG-001 §5](ag-001-agent-roles-and-scopes.md) | Total |
| L-4 | ~31 classifications, ~9 severities, 8 quality profiles | **0** | Total |

Review debt is **100% at every level.** The corpus has no reviewed output.

### 4.3 What a reviewer must not do

- **Must not** ask the agent to classify the review's findings (§4.1 step 5).
- **Must not** accept an agent's self-audit as discharging a level.
- **Must not** review only the pages the agent points at.
- **Must not** confirm an unverified count without stating the new verification as a
  **new event** ([AG-008 §4.1](ag-008-agent-collaboration-protocol.md)).

## 5. Worked instance

**Designing the L-1 sample, and finding why it has not happened.**

A compliant L-1 pass needs ten claims not chosen by the agent. A reader could
select them by any neutral rule — every tenth cross-reference, or all citations on
three randomly picked pages.

Now the obstacle, which is not laziness:

| Step | Requires | Available? |
|---|---|---|
| Pick 10 claims neutrally | A reader | **The estate has one participant, who is the instructor** |
| Open each cited instrument | Reading | Yes |
| Judge whether the claim is supported | Judgement about the agent's output | **The instructor commissioned the output** |
| Record findings | Append to the defect register | Yes |
| Classify findings | **Not the agent** (§4.1 step 5) | **Only the instructor** |

**Terminal state: `blockage` — the resource is an independent reader.**

The blockage looks like the one
[ST-17 §4.3](../standards/st-17-worked-instance-standard.md) identifies as
convergent across [REG-05](../registries/reg-05-credential-registry.md),
[RB-05](../rubrics/rb-05-harm-review-gate.md),
[MX-016](../corpus/mx-016-appeals-and-due-process.md) and
[MX-017](../corpus/mx-017-conflicts-of-interest.md): **a second person**.

### 5.1 But L-1 is different, and that is the finding

Every other convergent blockage needs someone **with standing** — an appeal
reviewer must be independent *and* authorised; a ratifying authority must be
*answerable*.

**L-1 needs neither.** It needs someone who can read and is not the agent. A
stranger, an unpaid reader, a person with twenty minutes and no relationship to the
estate can perform a valid L-1 pass, and their findings would be as good as
anyone's — because the check is *does the cited text say this*, which has a
determinate answer independent of the checker's authority.

So L-1 is not blocked by [DEF-031](../00-governance-map/defect-register.md) at all.
It is blocked by **nobody having been asked**.

That distinction is worth more than the rest of this instrument. The estate has
spent a great deal of prose establishing that its processes cannot run for want of
a second participant, and that establishment has quietly absorbed a check that
requires no participant at all — only a reader.

Filed per [ST-17 W6](../standards/st-17-worked-instance-standard.md):

> **L-1, reclassified.** Source checking is **not** blocked by the
> single-participant condition. It is an open invitation to any reader, requires no
> role, and is the only detection available for the corpus's one undetectable error
> class. Its non-performance is not an incapacity — it is an **unmade request**, and
> the request is made in §9.

## 6. Failure modes

| # | Failure | Current state |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | **Self-review counted as review** | Guarded by §2.1; **the corpus is full of self-audits that read as audits** |
| F2 | **Agent classifies review findings** | Prohibited by §4.1 step 5; untested |
| F3 | **Agent-chosen sample** | Prohibited by §4.1 step 1 |
| F4 | **Review debt uncounted** | Corrected by §4.2 — 100% at every level |
| F5 | **L-1 absorbed into the DEF-031 excuse** | **Was actual** — corrected in §5.1 |
| F6 | **Retroactive verification** | Guarded by §4.3 |
| F7 | **Reviewer reviews only what is pointed at** | Guarded by §4.3 |

## 7. Limits

1. **This instrument is unoperable at L-4** and has never been exercised at L-1,
   L-2, or L-3 (§4.2).
2. **Review debt is 100%.** No sentence in this corpus has been checked by anyone
   other than the system that wrote it.
3. **Written by the party to be reviewed**, which is
   [MX-017 C1](../corpus/mx-017-conflicts-of-interest.md); a reviewer should treat
   the four levels as the reviewee's proposal, not a settled standard.
4. **The four levels are asserted**, not derived from any review practice.
5. **§5.1's reclassification undermines the estate's own framing** elsewhere: other
   instruments may likewise have absorbed checks that need no participant. That
   audit has not been done.
6. **No Arabic text**, while `prevailing_text: ar`.

## 8. Falsifier

> **IF FALSE.** Falsified if:
>
> - **(a)** **an L-1 pass is performed and finds no fabrication** — which would be
>   the first evidence in the corpus's existence that its citations hold;
> - **(b)** an L-1 pass finds a fabrication — which falsifies
>   [AG-010 §5](ag-010-agent-error-and-correction.md)'s empty AE-1 row;
> - **(c)** a self-audit is described anywhere as a review (§2.1);
> - **(d)** review debt is shown to be less than 100% at any level (§4.2);
> - **(e)** a check currently attributed to the single-participant blockage is shown
>   to need no participant — **§5.1 found one; there are probably others** (§7.5);
> - **(f)** a reviewer's findings are shown to have been classified by the agent
>   (§4.1 step 5).
>
> **(a) and (b) are the same act with opposite results, and either would be the most
> informative twenty minutes ever spent on this corpus.** (e) is the generalisation
> worth pursuing: the estate's incapacity framing may be doing more excusing than it
> is entitled to.

## 9. Obligation

**On the estate:**
- **Perform an L-1 pass.** Ten claims, not chosen by the agent, twenty minutes. It
  needs no role, no authority, and no second participant (§5.1).
- Count and publish review debt (§4.2).
- Never describe a self-audit as a review (§2.1).
- Audit the corpus for other checks wrongly attributed to the single-participant
  blockage (§7.5, §8e).
- Fill **L-4** — the [REG-08](../registries/reg-08-role-and-authority-registry.md)
  vacancy with the widest consequences across the agent family.

**On any reader:**
- **You can perform L-1.** Pick a claim with a citation, open the cited instrument,
  check it. Publishing one finding — pass or fail — would give this corpus its first
  externally verified statement.

**On an agent:**
- Never classify findings from a review of its own output (§4.1 step 5).
- Never select the review sample (§4.1 step 1).
- Never present a self-audit as a review (§2.1).
- Record a newly closed capability gap as a new verification event, never
  retroactively ([AG-008 §4.1](ag-008-agent-collaboration-protocol.md)).
