# LEX-03 — Term Set: Governance and Authority

> **This page will not resolve:** what any term means. It records **where each
> term is defined** and sends you there. Entries never state the operative meaning
> ([LEX-02 §1.1](lexicon-entry-standard.md)).

> **Authority.** A **record** — outside the precedence ladder
> ([GM-01](../00-governance-map/precedence-and-conflict-rules.md)). Entry form is
> [LEX-02](lexicon-entry-standard.md); the rules about which terms get defined are
> [ST-10](../standards/st-10-terminology-and-definition-standard.md).

> **Disclosure first.** **Every entry below is missing field 2, the Arabic term**,
> which [LEX-02 X1](lexicon-entry-standard.md) makes mandatory. Under
> [LEX-02 §2.2](lexicon-entry-standard.md) this means the corpus's governance
> vocabulary **has no authoritative form** — the prevailing text is Arabic and the
> Arabic does not exist. Twelve non-compliant entries follow.

---

## 1. Scope of this set

The twelve terms a reader must hold to parse **any authority note in the corpus**.
[BX-003 §3.1](../bcia/bx-003-internalisation-phase.md) calls this the
"precedence-ladder tax": every page's first sentence assumes this vocabulary.

Sibling sets (not yet written) would cover harms, records, and site terms.

## 2. The entries

Nine fields per [LEX-02 §2](lexicon-entry-standard.md); 1–5 mandatory. **Field 2 is
absent throughout.**

## 3. What this set exposes

| Observation | Consequence |
|---|---|
| **12 of 12 lack field 2** | The governance vocabulary has no authoritative form ([LEX-02 §2.2](lexicon-entry-standard.md)) |
| **3 carry `uncertain` divergence** | The licence's enforceability architecture rests on imported terms nobody competent has checked |
| **2 T1 breaches are split here** | *record* and *designated* — remedies unavailable at source, available in the lexicon ([LEX-02 §4.1](lexicon-entry-standard.md)) |
| **12 terms for one authority note** | The [BX-003 §3.1](../bcia/bx-003-internalisation-phase.md) tax, quantified |

### 3.1 The tax is twelve terms

[BX-003 §3.1](../bcia/bx-003-internalisation-phase.md) asserted that the
precedence ladder imposes a cost on every page's first sentence but did not size
it.

This set sizes it: **twelve defined terms**, of which four are authority classes,
three are imported legal terms with uncertain divergence, and two are ambiguous
across senses.

A reader must hold all twelve to parse *"`governing`, tier 4, subordinate to tier
1a incorporated by §A.10"* — a sentence appearing at the head of nearly every
instrument.

Filed per [ST-17 W6](../standards/st-17-worked-instance-standard.md):

> **Finding LEX-03/1.** The authority note's vocabulary cost is **twelve terms**.
> [ST-16 §6](../standards/st-16-audience-tier-standard.md) found three of four
> tier 1 claims fail because they open with precedence reasoning; this quantifies
> why. A tier 1 page cannot open with an authority note **at all** — not as a style
> matter, but because the note is a twelve-term dependency and
> [ST-16 U2](../standards/st-16-audience-tier-standard.md) forbids a tier 1 page
> from using an undefined corpus term.

That converts [ST-16](../standards/st-16-audience-tier-standard.md)'s soft finding
into a hard one: the three failing tier 1 pages are not carelessly written, they
are **structurally impossible** in their current opening form.

## 4. Limits

1. **Field 2 absent throughout** — the set is non-compliant with
   [LEX-02](lexicon-entry-standard.md) at its mandatory field.
2. **Twelve terms is one set** of an estimated 100 across the corpus
   ([ST-10 §7.1](../standards/st-10-terminology-and-definition-standard.md)).
3. **Three `uncertain` divergences** cannot be resolved without counsel.
4. **Sibling term sets do not exist** — harms, records, and site vocabularies are
   unregistered.
5. **No orientation has been tested** on a reader who did not already know the term.

## 5. Falsifier

> **IF FALSE.** Falsified if:
>
> - **(a)** a term here is shown to be defined in an instrument other than its
>   field 3 — an [ST-10 T1](../standards/st-10-terminology-and-definition-standard.md)
>   breach found through the lexicon
>   ([LEX-02 §7(f)](lexicon-entry-standard.md));
> - **(b)** an orientation exceeds 25 words or states an obligation
>   ([LEX-02 X3](lexicon-entry-standard.md));
> - **(c)** a field 5 is shown not to describe a real confusion risk;
> - **(d)** a thirteenth term is shown to be required to parse an authority note —
>   which would strengthen §3.1;
> - **(e)** any entry is shown to carry field 2 — which would be **good news** and
>   falsify the disclosure block.
>
> **(a) is the valuable one** and takes an hour across twelve entries.

## 6. Obligation

None — a record. Bearing duties:

- Supply **field 2** for all twelve, or state expressly that the governance
  vocabulary has no authoritative form
  ([LEX-02 §2.2](lexicon-entry-standard.md)).
- Register the remaining ~88 terms
  ([ST-10](../standards/st-10-terminology-and-definition-standard.md) T4).
- Record **LEX-03/1** (§3.1) against
  [ST-16](../standards/st-16-audience-tier-standard.md): tier 1 pages cannot carry
  an authority note.
- Never state an operative meaning in field 4
  ([LEX-02 §1.1](lexicon-entry-standard.md)).
- Keep the three legal imports at **`uncertain`**; do not guess
  ([LEX-02 X5](lexicon-entry-standard.md)).
