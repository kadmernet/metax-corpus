# HX-000 v2027.1 · The Register of Structural Harms

> **Authority.** This is the source register for Part H. Its reading law (§0) states that **where a
> derived document differs from this register, the register governs and the difference is a defect**.
> ML-2.3 §E.9 states the opposite: the licence governs. The two rules are reconciled by role.
> **ML-2.3 governs which identifier names which harm and which harms are absolute, because it is the
> signed-to-be tier-1 text. This register governs the content of each entry** (mechanism, evidence,
> falsifier). On both points this release agrees with ML-2.3 §H.2.2, so no conflict arises today.

> **Disclosure first.**
> - (1) **Not yet in force.** ML-2.4, which incorporates this version, is a pending draft. Until it is ratified, ML-2.3 incorporates [v2026.10.1](hx-000-structural-harms.md).
> - (2) That earlier text **disagrees with ML-2.3 on the identity of most of its harms**. [CON-002](con-002-harm-numbering-concordance.md) maps the two by subject, and the divergence is DEF-040.
> - (3) A second "HX-000 v2027.1", with different harms behind the same numbers, arrived in the same folder. It is not adopted ([GM-16 D-01](../00-governance-map/source-intake-v2027-1.md)), and its candidates are listed in §15.

## §0 Standing

**§0.1 Three constitutional properties.**
- **It is a floor, not a ceiling.** A harm absent from the register is not thereby permitted (HX-26).
- **Every entry is retirable.** Each carries a falsifier, and §E.5 obliges publication of any narrowing.
- **It expects to be superseded.** A claim that it is permanent would itself be HX-22 and HX-18.

**§0.2 Which version binds.** Version selection follows the licence. A Work under ML-2.3 is governed
by the register at the version ML-2.3 names (v2026.10.1). A Work under ML-2.4 is governed by this
version. Because v2026.10.1 is internally inconsistent with ML-2.3 (DEF-040), **the ML-2.3 §H.2.2
table is the operative statement of harm identity for every ML-2.3 Work**, and §§3–6 below supply
the content.

## §1 The four principles

*Amanah* (faithful stewardship) · *Ṣidq* (structural truthfulness) · *ʿAdl* (justice and
proportionality, including that a duty only incumbents can afford is not just) · *Shafāfiyah*
(transparency in terms a person can act on). Beneath them sit the *maqāṣid*. Each mapping is
flagged **settled** or **reasoned**. Reasoned mappings are open at RAT-16 and **RAT-30** (re-keyed
from the source's RAT-17, D-16). **Every secular duty stands on its own evidence**, and the failure
of a mapping vacates no duty.

## §2 Roles

**Author · Integrator · Operator · Commissioner**, as in ML-2.3 §H.1-bis. Unallocated harm classes
default to the Integrator. Where allocation is disputed, the duty is held **jointly** until one
party accepts it in writing. **Custodian** and **Welfare Reviewer** apply only to substrate
deployments ([BX-018 §1](../bcia/bx-018-register-of-substrate-harms.md)).

## §3 The Scale Gate

The thresholds are ML-2.3 §H.2-bis.1's, which govern. The source's simplified four-row table is
an approximation and does not override them. **The absolutes never scale down.** §H.21 test: can a
single unfunded builder discharge the Tier 0 form of a duty in one afternoon? If not, the duty is
defective.

## §9 Evidence and decay

**EV-4 tiers.** Tier 1 is documented intent from the inside. Tier 2 is measured, replicated effect.
Tier 3 is small or contested. Tier 4 is unsettled. **No harm may rest solely on Tier 3–4.** Flagged
count: **0**. **No fabricated citations.** Unverifiable candidates carry `[CITE-UNVERIFIED]`.

**Decay rule, corrected.** The source rule `2027-01 + (n mod 12)` sends n = 12 and n = 24 to month 0.
ML-2.4 corrects it to `2027-01 + (((n − 1) mod 12) + 1)` months. **This document applies the
corrected rule.** The source's own per-entry verify dates do not all follow either rule (e.g. HX-05
2027-06, HX-25 2027-02). DEF-051 records this, and the dates in §6 are the source's as given,
pending a recomputed Decay Calendar.
