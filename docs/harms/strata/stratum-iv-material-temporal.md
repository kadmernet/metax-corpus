# Stratum IV · Material and Temporal

> **Authority.** A companion page. [ML-2.3 §H.2.2](../../license/ml-2-3.md) governs.

**Injures** the world and the record. **Characteristic remedy:** accounting, redundancy, preservation and retained judgement.

| Id | Harm | Operative statement (ML-2.3, verbatim) | Duty | Absolute | Verify |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| HX-10 | Extractive materiality | "\"Immaterial\" computing runs on mines, water, power and a waste stream." | §H.14 | — | 2027-11 |
| HX-11 | Fragility and the integral accident | "Efficiency buys coupling; coupling buys catastrophe; the accident is invented with the system." | §H.15 | — | 2027-12 |
| HX-12 | Digital amnesia, and its inverse | "A civilisation that records everything preserves nothing — and forgets nothing it should." | §H.28 | — | 2028-01 |
| HX-14 | Moral deskilling and delegated judgement | "Judgement delegated to a system does not move — it disappears, along with its author." | §H.19 | **§H.3(a)(v)** authorless judgement | 2027-03 |

**Falsifiers, in substance:**
- **HX-10:** a full-boundary footprint shown to be immaterial.
- **HX-11:** dependency mapping shown not to reduce cascades.
- **HX-12:** ordinary incentive shown to preserve and forget adequately.
- **HX-14:** named authorship shown not to improve quality.

**The estate's own position:** **HX-10 is unremedied** (inherited accounting gaps).

**Two-sided harm.** HX-12 has a positive limb, the preservation duty in §H.28(c), and an inverse limb, expiry, in §H.28(e). Conflicts between the two are resolved and recorded under §H.31.3.
