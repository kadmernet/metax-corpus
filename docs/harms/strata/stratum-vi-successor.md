# Stratum VI · Successor

> **Authority.** A companion page. [ML-2.3 §H.2.2](../../license/ml-2-3.md) governs.

**Injures** a party who is not present to object: a future person, a future holder, or a synthetic agent standing where a person stood.

| Id | Harm | Operative statement (ML-2.3, verbatim) | Duty | Absolute | Verify |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| HX-20 | Synthetic agency without a principal | "An agent acts in the world and no human is answerable for the action at the moment it occurs." | §H.25 | **§H.3(a)(vi)** unprincipled agency | 2027-09 |
| HX-21 | Corpus poisoning and model collapse | "Synthetic output re‑enters the corpus unlabelled; the successor trains on the residue of the predecessor and the commons degrades irreversibly." | §H.26 | — | 2027-10 |
| HX-22 | Pre‑emption of the successor | "A choice is foreclosed for people who do not yet exist and cannot be consulted; irreversibility is treated as an implementation detail." | §H.27 | **§H.3(a)(viii)** irreversible foreclosure | 2027-11 |
| HX-23 | Custody beyond the horizon | "Data and systems outlive the competence, solvency or intent of the party that assembled them, and pass to a holder the subject never contemplated." | §H.28 | — | 2027-12 |
| HX-24 | Substitution of the interlocutor | "A synthetic counterpart occupies the place of a human relation — teacher, friend, counsellor, intimate — and the person's capacity for, and access to, the human relation degrades." | §H.29 (+§H.24) | **§H.3(a)(vii)** developmental substitution (**disjunctive**) | 2028-01 |
| HX-25 | Erosion of exit from the technical order | "Non‑participation ceases to be possible: the digital route becomes the only route, and abstention becomes exclusion." | §H.30 | — | 2027-02 |

**The estate's own position:**
- **HX-25 is unremedied** (dependence on third-party identity providers).
- **HX-21 is a self-instance risk.** This corpus is largely agent-drafted. §H.26(a) marking is met by `authorship_note` and `human_read` front matter, and only where those fields are present.

**Do not confuse.** "HX-24 = developmental default" (MX-014 §1) is wrong: developmental default is the *duty* §H.24. "HX-21 = foreclosure of the successor's options" is HX-22 (GM-17 X-14). "HX-20 = inheritance of unauditable systems" and "HX-25 = silence in the record" come from HX-002's superseded vocabulary (X-11, X-15).
