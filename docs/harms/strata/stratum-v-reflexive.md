# Stratum V · Reflexive

> **Authority.** A companion page. [ML-2.3 §H.2.2](../../license/ml-2-3.md) governs.

**Injures** through the remedy itself. **Characteristic remedy:** falsifiability and proportion. ML-2.3's own note: Strata V and VI "are complaints about **this instrument**."

| Id | Harm | Operative statement (ML-2.3, verbatim) | Duty | Absolute | Verify |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| HX-15 | Compliance theatre | "The disclosure becomes the product; the artefact of accountability substitutes for accountability and immunises the practice it describes." | §H.16, §H.17 | — | 2027-04 |
| HX-16 | Consent laundering | "Consent is manufactured as a ritual — bundled, unrefusable, or extracted at a moment of need — and then cited as the authority for what follows." | §H.18 | — | 2027-05 |
| HX-17 | Responsibility diffusion | "The supply chain is arranged, by accident or design, so that every party can truthfully say the decision was not theirs." | §H.20 | — | 2027-06 |
| HX-18 | Standard capture | "Compliance cost is raised until it functions as a moat: the duty entrenches the incumbent who can afford it and eliminates the small actor who cannot." | §H.21 | — | 2027-07 |
| HX-19 | Ethical enclosure and values monoculture | "One estate's norms, language and defaults are exported as universal infrastructure, and the alternatives stop being buildable." | §H.22 | — | 2027-08 |

**The estate's own position:**
- **HX-18 is unremedied.** There is no internal check against this register acting as a barrier.
- **HX-19 is unremedied.** There is no Arabic mirror, and the evidence base is Anglophone.

**Do not confuse (CON-002, GM-17 X-11, X-12).** Older estate texts use "HX-18" for *consent integrity*, which is HX-16 here, and "HX-17" for *the audit that certifies itself*, which is closest to HX-15 here. Family B's bundle assigns HX-15…HX-19 to different harms (governance volume, self-administered adjudication, uncalibrated attestation, and others). **Those are candidates UH-01…UH-05, not register harms** (HX-000 v2027.1 §15).
