# Stratum III · Economic and Structural

> **Authority.** A companion page. [ML-2.3 §H.2.2](../../license/ml-2-3.md) governs.

**Injures** the distribution of power, work and worth. **Characteristic remedy:** exit, portability and attribution.

**The numbers are not contiguous.** HX-13 sits here and HX-14 sits in Stratum IV, by the numbering law (harm = chapter of *Repairing the Machine* v2, HX-000 v2027.1 §4).

| Id | Harm | Operative statement (ML-2.3, verbatim) | Duty | Absolute | Verify |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| HX-07 | Centralisation and enclosure | "Network effects convert a commons into a landlordship; exit is priced out." | §H.11 | — | 2027-08 |
| HX-08 | Labour fragmentation and invisible work | "Human labour is disassembled until it is deniable, then denied." | §H.12 | — | 2027-09 |
| HX-09 | Metric colonisation | "The measure replaces the thing measured, then governs it." | §H.13 | — | 2027-10 |
| HX-13 | Exclusion by default | "Digital‑only provision removes a right from those who cannot reach it." | §H.23 | — | 2027-02 |

**Falsifiers, in substance:**
- **HX-07:** within-layer competitive displacement shown.
- **HX-08:** disclosure shown not to change conditions anywhere.
- **HX-09:** gap statements shown to stop proxy drift, or shown not to.
- **HX-13:** no outcome differential across channels.

**The estate's own position:** **HX-13 is unremedied**, because of accessibility gaps (§13).
