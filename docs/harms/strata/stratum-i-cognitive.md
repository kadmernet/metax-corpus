# Stratum I · Cognitive

> **Authority.** A **companion** page. Harm identity and absolutes come from [ML-2.3 §H.2.2 and §H.3](../../license/ml-2-3.md), and content from [HX-000 v2027.1 §6](../hx-000-register-v2027-1.md). Where this page differs from either, they govern.
