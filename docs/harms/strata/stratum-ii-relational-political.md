# Stratum II · Relational and Political

> **Authority.** A companion page. [ML-2.3 §H.2.2](../../license/ml-2-3.md) governs identity. [HX-000 v2027.1](../hx-000-register-v2027-1.md) governs content.

**Injures** the standing of the person before others and before power. **Characteristic remedy:** due process and minimisation.

| Id | Harm | Operative statement (ML-2.3, verbatim) | Duty | Absolute | Verify |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| HX-04 | The human as product | "The person is the raw material, not the customer, and cannot know what is taken." | §H.8 (and §H.24 for minors) | **§H.3(a)(ii)** sensitive inference trading | 2027-05 |
| HX-05 | Ambient surveillance | "Aggregation destroys practical obscurity; anonymity is not a property of a dataset." | §H.9 | — | 2027-06 |
| HX-06 | Algorithmic governance without due process | "Consequential decisions become unappealable because they became automatic." | §H.10 | **§H.3(a)(iii)** unappealable determination | 2027-07 |

**Falsifiers, in substance:**
- **HX-04:** a consent architecture that lets persons enumerate, price and refuse inferences.
- **HX-05:** retained records shown not to migrate to worse holders.
- **HX-06:** full contestability shown to perform worse for subjects.

**The estate's own position:** **HX-04 is unremedied.** The ad processors may profile readers behaviourally (HX-000 v2027.1 §13, added by the intake; GM-16 D-15).

**Note on HX-06 and the estate.** The estate makes determinations about persons (credentials, MX-025). Under the Family B candidate *self-administered adjudication* (UH-04), an institution that cannot provide an independent adjudicator should hold fewer judgement powers. This is a candidate, not a harm of the register.
