# MLT-09 · BCIA: Bio-Computational Intelligence Architecture

> **Conformance declaration (§H.19).** Machine-assisted consolidation. `human_read: none`.

> **Authority.** This file **adds nothing**. It restates in the seven-part form the umbrella requires
> what [BX-010](../../bcia/bx-010-document-zero-and-research-charter.md) and its siblings already
> bind. **Where this file and BX-010…BX-018 differ, they govern.**

> **Human-cell exclusion: absolute.**
> **No wet-lab protocols. No substrate cultivation. No synthetic-biology bench instruction. No organism engineering.**

---

## 1. Founding question

**What can be learned about computation from systems that compute without having been designed to, and what, precisely, does that permit anyone to conclude?**

## 2. Scope

The scope is literature synthesis, formal modelling, simulation and ethics about bio-computational principle, including the ten-book BCIA corpus read under the observed three-layer map (BX-013). The scope is **theoretical and computational only** (BX-017).

## 3. Exclusions (the seven binding bases, BX-010 §9)

| # | Basis | Forbids |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The human-cell exclusion is absolute** ([BX-011](../../bcia/bx-011-human-cell-exclusion.md)) | Any work touching human neural tissue, human cell lines, human-derived organoids or human biological cultivation. There is no research exception, no gate that opens it, and no scale at which it relaxes |
| 2 | **No laboratory instruction** | Protocols, bench methods, cultivation, organism engineering, acquisition guidance. **MX-014 extends this to every substrate class for the estate** (DEF-052) |
| 3 | **The refused inference** ([BX-016](../../bcia/bx-016-refused-inference.md)) | Arguing from silicon's limits to biology's promise |
| 4 | **Notation frozen** ([BX-014](../../bcia/bx-014-notation-freeze.md)) | Teaching, extending or deriving from BSN, LPN or AMN |
| 5 | **Non-doctrinal posture** | Presenting any BCIA position as settled while a gate is open |
| 6 | **Disclosure is never gated** | Withholding the status page, the audit, the defect register or the open-problems list |
| 7 | **Charter before experiment** | Any activity before its scope, limits and **ethics reviewer** are published |

**Costly exclusion.** The human-cell exclusion. It removes the substrate on which most of the field's current results rest.

**Also prohibited corpus-wide:** the DishBrain/DeepMind speed comparison. The two experiments are not comparable at any level of verification.

## 4. Method

As BX-017 (contribution without a laboratory) states it: synthesis, modelling and simulation, pre-registered under MX-014 §4. **Method withholding** (ML-B-1.0 §B-A.4, A-6) binds every output.

## 5. Evidence policy

- Common rule E-0, plus:
- **Every dated or quantitative claim from the BCIA books is `[VERIFY]` until remediation Gate 6 checks it** (BX-012).
- No BCIA position may cite the books as evidence for a claim about biology. The books are the object of study, not a source.

## 6. Status and gates

**`gated`.** The seven remediation gates (BX-012):
1. One canonical inventory of Volume I.
2. Taxonomy reconciled. *Triangulated, pending ratification.*
3. BSN/LPN/AMN resolved. *Unresolvable from the corpus. Retirement at Gate 7 is recommended.*
4. Universality versus revelatory grounding.
5. Stewardship versus ownership.
6. Dated claims verified or retracted.
7. Glossary, notation policy and errata workflow frozen.

**Promotion beyond `research-preview` is forbidden while any gate is open** (ML-B-1.0 Annex B.6, adopted as practice).

**Additional block:** the ethics veto holder is unnamed (M-03). Until one is named, no BCIA publication beyond the status, audit and charter surfaces is possible.

## 7. Sunset criteria

**Source:** wound down if, after three annual cycles, Gate 3 is still unresolvable **and** the Gate 6 sweep retracts more claims than it confirms.

**Conflict (GM-17 X-75).** Gate 3's recommended resolution is to **retire** the notation at Gate 7. If retirement counts as resolution, the first limb can never fire, and the sunset is defeated by the remedy. **Proposed rule (RAT-41):** for sunset purposes, Gate 3 counts as *resolved* only if the notation is **specified**. Retirement closes the gate for promotion, but leaves the sunset limb live.

## Objections (strongest counter-argument, without reply)

A programme whose subject is a ten-book corpus of uncertain provenance, frozen notation and
unverified claims, and which may not touch the substrate or teach the method, studies a text, not a
science. Its gates may never close. Its sunset may never fire. Its exclusions make the field's actual
evidence unavailable. It may survive indefinitely as a remediation exercise on documents.

## Falsifier (for this charter)

This charter is defective if any statement in it is not traceable to BX-010…BX-018, MX-014 or ML-B-1.0.
§7's proposed rule is the only addition, and it is marked.
