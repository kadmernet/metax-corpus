# MLT-000 · The MacroLifeTech Programme Register

> **Authority.** Governs the programme layer of the `metax` pillar. It sits under the Charter, under
> [MX-014](../../corpus/mx-014-research-ethics-and-exclusions.md) (which binds all research and
> prevails over any programme), and for MLT-09 under [ML-B-1.0](../../license/ml-b-1-0.md).

> **Disclosure first.** The umbrella's rule says that *a programme lacking any of the seven
> charter parts may not leave `planned`*. **No programme states all seven as written parts.** In
> particular, none gives a distinct *evidence policy*: the method and the evidence are run together.
> Yet seven of the nine declare `research-preview`. Under its own rule, **every programme is
> `planned`** until its charter is complete (DEF-049). The table below gives both statuses. The
> umbrella "publishes positions rather than findings". Every "key position" below is an **unverified
> position** (P1), not a result.

---

## 1. The umbrella

**MacroLifeTech** (display *Meta-X*, route `/metax/`, nav key `metax`) is a civilisational research
umbrella. Its single structural commitment is that **it publishes what is unsettled with the same
prominence as what is settled**.

**Identifier grammar.** `MLT-{NN}` for a programme, `MLT-{NN}-S{NN}` for a series, `MLT-{NN}-V{NN}`
for a volume. Numbers are immortal. *(The `S` series here is a programme-local namespace, distinct
from TopTech's S01…S52. See [LEX-05 CT-15](../../lexicon/contested-terms.md).)*

**The seven-part charter.**
1. Founding question (one answerable sentence).
2. Scope.
3. Exclusions, including at least one that costs the programme something.
4. Method.
5. Evidence policy.
6. Status and gates.
7. **Sunset criteria.**

*A programme with no wind-down criterion is wound down by attrition, not by decision.*

**Binding on all nine:** MX-014's three refusals and five exclusions. **No human-subject research.**
**No covert research on readers.** **No research that cannot be published in full.**

**Writing a part is not adopting it.** Under the seven-part rule, a programme may leave `planned` only when
**the Curator adopts** all seven parts. An agent-proposed part counts as *drafted*, not as *adopted*. Every
programme therefore stays `planned` (MLT-09 `gated`) until the Curator rules (RAT-39).

## 4b. Common evidence rule E-0 (applies to every programme; proposed)

- **E-0.1 Strengths.**
  - `position (P1)`: reasoned, with no measurement behind it.
  - `supported position`: at least one EV-4 Tier 1 or Tier 2 source **read by a named human**, with locator and read-date.
  - **No programme publishes `finding`.** The umbrella publishes positions.
- **E-0.2** No position rests solely on Tier 3–4 evidence (HX-000 §9). A citation not read by a human is `[CITE-UNVERIFIED]` and supports nothing.
- **E-0.3** Every quantitative claim carries its derivation or a `[VERIFY]` with a V-number.
- **E-0.4** A position held by the programme's founding question cannot be cited as evidence for that question.
- **E-0.5** Disconfirming evidence is published at the same prominence as confirming evidence. This is the umbrella's single commitment.

## 5. Falsifier

This register is wrong if any programme has published an **adopted** seven-part charter or an output.
None is held. Drafted charters exist (§4a) and are not adopted.

## 6. Obligation

- **Curator:** either accept the charter-rule status (everything `planned`, MLT-09 `gated`) or amend the rule.
- **Agents:** write no programme leaf page that presents a key position as a finding.
