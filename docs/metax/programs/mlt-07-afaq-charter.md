# MLT-07 · Āfāq: Horizons

> **Conformance declaration (§H.19).** Machine-assisted draft. `human_read: none`. Not governing until adopted.

> **Disclosure first.** The bundle says Āfāq "feeds Part J and HX-25 directly". In Family B's numbering,

## 1. Founding question

**How should an institution reason about consequences at timescales beyond any evidence it can obtain?**

## 2. Scope

- Long-horizon decision theory.
- Discounting and its ethical content.
- Scenario method and its failure modes.
- The epistemics of forecasting.
- Intergenerational obligation.
- The structure of irreversibility.

## 3. Exclusions

- **No existential-risk advocacy and no existential-risk dismissal.**
- **No probability estimates for events without a reference class.**
- **Costly exclusion.** The programme cannot produce the confident numbers the area's audience wants, and holds that producing them would itself be the harm.

## 4. Method

Analysis, with a **standing prohibition on any point estimate lacking a reference class**.

## 5. Evidence policy *(agent-proposed)*

| Strength | Admissible support |
|---|---|
| `position (P1)` | Analysis with stated value commitments. A discount-rate position must state the ethical premise it encodes |
| `supported position` | A documented historical decision whose long-horizon consequence is now observable, read by a named human |

Specific rules:
- **Every number states its reference class**, or it is not published.
- **Scenarios are labelled "for attention, not probability"** wherever they appear (key position 3, applied to the programme's own output).
- Common rule E-0 applies.

## 6. Status and gates

- **Declared:** `planned`, which is honest.
- **Proposed gate** G-Af1: one decision procedure is drafted that the estate itself could adopt for an irreversible choice it faces, for example ML-2.3 §H.27(a) applied to its own identifier immortality rule.

## 7. Sunset criteria

**Wound down if it cannot state, after three cycles, one decision procedure an institution could adopt.**

*Strength: the strongest of the nine.* It is observable, adverse, and judgeable by an outsider: either a
procedure exists in adoptable form or it does not. **Proposed sharpening:** "could adopt" is tested by
the estate adopting it.

---

## Key positions (P1)

1. **Discount rates encode an ethical position** and are almost always presented as technical parameters.
2. **Irreversibility, not magnitude, is the decision-relevant property.** Irreversibility can be assessed where magnitude cannot. *(This underlies ML-2.3 §H.27 and the precautionary asymmetry of BX-018 §3.)*
3. **Scenario methods are useful for generating attention and worthless for allocating probability**, and they are routinely used for the second.

## Objections (strongest counter-argument, without reply)

Refusing all estimates without a reference class means refusing to reason quantitatively about every
genuinely novel risk, because novelty is the absence of a reference class. Decision-makers will use
numbers anyway. A programme that declines to supply better ones leaves them with worse ones, and
calls the abstention a principle.

## Falsifier (for this charter)

The charter is defective if any MLT-07 output contains a probability with no reference class stated.
