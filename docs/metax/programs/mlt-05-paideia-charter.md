# MLT-05 · Paideia: Formation

> **Conformance declaration (§H.19).** Machine-assisted draft. `human_read: none`. Not governing until adopted.

> **Disclosure first: the method as supplied is forbidden.**
> - The source method is "outcome measurement on the estate's own cohorts, pre-registered".
> - [MX-014 exclusion 1](../../corpus/mx-014-research-ethics-and-exclusions.md) forbids "research involving human participants … no data collection from people as research subjects".
> - MX-014 exclusion 4 forbids covert research on readers.
> - **Pre-registration cures neither.** MX-014 is tier 3 and this charter is lower, and MX-014 is "unamendable toward weakness".
>
> **MX-014 prevails** (GM-17 X-81). The source method is kept in §4.1 as a record. §4.2 is the only method this programme can use unless the Curator amends MX-014, which by its own terms cannot be done in a weakening direction.

---

## 1. Founding question

**What distinguishes education that forms judgement from training that installs capability, and can the difference be assessed without destroying it?**

## 2. Scope

- Assessment theory.
- The measurement of judgement.
- Apprenticeship and tacit knowledge.
- Curriculum as a claim about what matters.
- Credentialing's social function.
- The counterproductivity threshold in educational institutions.

## 3. Exclusions

- **No learner data.**
- **No A/B testing on learners.**
- **No engagement optimisation.**
- **Costly exclusion.** The programme cannot run the experiments that would most directly test its own positions, and says so.

**Note.** "No learner data" (§3) and "outcome measurement on cohorts" (§4.1) contradict each other within the source itself, before MX-014 is even considered.

## 4. Method

### 4.1 As supplied (blocked; record only)

> Analysis plus **outcome measurement on the estate's own cohorts, pre-registered**, with the pre-registration published before data collection.

### 4.2 Permitted method *(agent-proposed)*

- **(a) Analysis.**
- **(b) Assessment of artefacts, not of persons.** The programme studies the estate's **published** assessments, rubrics and specimen sets (MX-025, RB-07). It asks whether they could distinguish judgement from capability, not whether people formed judgement.
- **(c) Learner-owned evidence, volunteered.** A learner may publish their own work and their own account of it under their own name and licence. The programme may cite it **as published work**, in the same way it would cite a book. Three conditions apply. It may not solicit it as a study. It may not aggregate it into cohort outcomes. It may not hold any data the learner did not publish.
- **(d) Others' published studies**, marked `dependent`, as in MLT-04 §5.

Under (b) to (d) the **cognitive arc stays P1**. The programme's "first obligation, to measure it or withdraw it", becomes: **withdraw it as an empirical claim, or keep it explicitly as an unmeasured design commitment.** Measurement is not available.

## 5. Evidence policy *(agent-proposed)*

| Strength | Admissible support |
|---|---|
| `position (P1)` | Analysis |
| `supported position` | Published, read-by-human Tier 1–2 assessment research, **or** a documented property of the estate's own published instruments (method §4.2(b)) |
| *never* | Any claim about the outcomes of the estate's learners as a group |

## 6. Status and gates

- **Declared:** `research-preview`.
- **Charter rule:** `planned`.
- **Additional block:** the method conflict, X-81.
- **Gate:** the Curator adopts §4.2 or withdraws the programme. The programme may not leave `planned` while §4.1 is its stated method.

**Declared conflict of interest** (source): this is the only programme whose subject is the estate itself. That is both its value and its conflict of interest, and it is declared at `/metax/programs/paideia/exclusions/`. Under §4.2(b) the conflict sharpens, because the programme would assess the estate's own instruments. **Its findings about MX-025 and RB-07 must be reviewed by someone other than their author** (the Curator) before publication, and that reviewer does not yet exist (CR-05, CR-10).

## 7. Sunset criteria

**Source:** "Wound down if the arc's outcome measurement returns null across three cohorts and no replacement thesis is available."

*Strength: unreachable.* The measurement it relies on is forbidden, so the criterion can never fire. **Proposed replacement:** wound down if, after three cycles, method §4.2(b) has not produced one published finding that an estate instrument *can* or *cannot* distinguish judgement from capability, with the reasoning stated.

## Key positions (P1)

1. **Assessing judgement requires a problem whose answer the assessor does not hold.** That is why it cannot be mechanised and why it is expensive.
2. **Any credential whose warrant is the issuer's standing rather than its detail will inflate.** This bears on UH-05 (uncalibrated attestation) and on the estate's own credentials (MX-025).
3. **The cognitive arc is a P1 claim.** It is reasoned and unmeasured, and **cannot be measured under MX-014.**

## Objections (strongest counter-argument, without reply)

Refusing to measure outcomes is the position every ineffective educational institution has
historically taken, and it is how unaccountable pedagogy protects itself. An estate that sells
formation and forbids itself from finding out whether formation happens has made its central claim
permanently safe from evidence. That is the property ST-04 says makes a claim worthless.

## Falsifier (for this charter)

The charter is defective if any MLT-05 output contains a statement about the estate's learners as a group.

## Annex · Defects filed

- X-81 / new DEF-059: the source method conflicts with MX-014 exclusions 1 and 4, and the source contradicts itself between "no learner data" and cohort measurement.
- RAT-40: adopt §4.2, or withdraw MLT-05.
