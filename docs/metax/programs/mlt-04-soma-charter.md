# MLT-04 · Soma: Embodiment

> **Conformance declaration (§H.19).** Machine-assisted draft. `human_read: none`. Not governing until adopted.

> **Disclosure first.**
> - The bundle says "3 of 7 parts complete" and then gives text for five parts. The two counts cannot both hold. This file treats the source's five as **source** and the other two as **agent-proposed** (DEF-058).
> - In the source this programme has the **weakest sunset criterion of the nine** (GM-16 Q6). §7 proposes a replacement.

---

## 1. Founding question *(source)*

**Which cognitive capabilities require a body, and which merely developed in one?**

## 2. Scope *(source)*

- Embodied and enactive cognition.
- Sensorimotor contingency.
- Morphological computation.
- The reach and limits of the extended-mind thesis.
- Robotics as an epistemic instrument rather than a product.

## 3. Exclusions *(source)*

- **No human-subject research of any kind.**
- **No BCI work.** That belongs to MLT-09 and is separately gated (ML-B-1.0).
- **No assistive-technology product design.**
- **Costly exclusion.** The human-subject exclusion removes the programme's most direct source of evidence.

## 4. Method *(source)*

Literature synthesis and simulation. Where a claim requires human data, the programme **cites others'
data and marks the claim as dependent**.

## 5. Evidence policy *(agent-proposed)*

| Strength | Admissible support |
|---|---|
| `position (P1)` | Analysis, or a simulation with published code and seed |
| `supported position (dependent)` | Published human-subject studies by others, read by a named human, **labelled `dependent`**: the programme did not and could not collect the data |
| `supported position` | Robotic or simulated evidence that bears on the claim without human data |

Specific rules:
- **A dependent claim inherits the weakest ethics of its sources.** Studies whose consent standard would fail ML-2.3 §H.18 are cited with that failure stated, or not cited.
- **"Requires a body" versus "developed in one" must be distinguished in every claim.** Evidence that a capability co-occurs with embodiment supports only the second.
- Common rule E-0 applies.

## 6. Status and gates *(agent-proposed)*

- **Declared:** `planned`, which agrees with the charter rule. **This is the only one of the nine whose declared status is honest without correction.**
- **Proposed gates:**
  - G-S1: the Curator adopts parts 5 and 6.
  - G-S2: one synthesis is published that separates "requires" from "developed in" for a named capability.

## 7. Sunset criteria

**Source:** "Wound down if the field converges on a settled position the programme has not contributed to."

*Strength: weakest of the nine.*
- "Converges" and "settled" are undefined.
- "Has not contributed to" is judged by the programme.
- The criterion rewards contribution, not truth: a programme that contributed to a wrong consensus would survive.

**Proposed replacement (awaiting the Curator):** wound down if, after three review cycles:
- (a) no synthesis has been published that assigns at least one named capability to "requires a body" or "developed in one" with stated evidence; **or**
- (b) every such assignment depends wholly on human-subject data from others, so that the human-subject exclusion has made the programme a literature review with no distinct contribution.

---

## Key positions (P1)

1. **Morphological computation is real and systematically under-counted** in comparisons between biological and engineered systems.
2. **The extended-mind thesis is stronger as a claim about cognitive systems than about minds**, and the two are routinely conflated.
3. **The evidence that abstract reasoning requires sensorimotor grounding is weaker than its advocates present.**

## Dependencies

Borders on MLT-09 (BCIA). The refused inference (BX-016), "silicon has ceilings, therefore biology", must not be made from Soma's embodiment positions either. **An embodiment argument is not a substrate argument.**

## Objections (strongest counter-argument, without reply)

A programme on embodiment that excludes bodies, meaning human subjects, interfaces and assistive work,
is studying embodiment by reading about it. Every position it can reach is a position someone with
data has already reached or refuted. The costly exclusion is not merely costly. It is total.

## Falsifier (for this charter)

The charter is defective if any published Soma position can be shown to rest on human data that the
programme itself obtained, by any route.

## Annex · Defects filed
