# MLT-08 · Mīzān: Measure and Balance

> **Conformance declaration (§H.19).** Machine-assisted draft. `human_read: none`. Not governing until adopted.

> **Disclosure first.** The bundle says Mīzān "supplies §H.13 and the §H.7 counterproductivity test".
> Both clauses **already exist** in ML-2.3, written before this programme. The programme can study and
> refine them. It did not supply them.

---

## 1. Founding question

**When does measuring a thing change it enough to make the measurement useless, and can the threshold be located before it is crossed?**

## 2. Scope

- Goodhart and Campbell dynamics.
- Specification gaming.
- Proxy selection.
- The unmeasured residual.
- Metric plurality as a defence.
- The counterproductivity test as an operational instrument.

## 3. Exclusions

- **No metrics consulting.**
- **No dashboard products.**
- **Costly exclusion.** The programme refuses the obvious commercial application of its own subject, on the ground that selling metrics while studying metric corruption is the corruption.

## 4. Method

- Formal analysis.
- Reconstruction of documented metric failures, with published sources.

## 5. Evidence policy *(agent-proposed)*

| Strength | Admissible support |
|---|---|
| `position (P1)` | Formal model with stated coupling assumptions |
| `supported position` | A reconstructed case whose sources are Tier 1–2 and read by a named human. The reconstruction must state **the coupling** between metric and consequence, measured or estimated with a range |

Specific rules:
- **The estate's own counts are a case.** Typed counts in prose (Rule 6; GM-17 X-60…X-66) are a live instance of a measure drifting from what it measures. Any reconstruction of them is published unedited.
- Common rule E-0 applies.

## 6. Status and gates

- **Declared:** `research-preview`.
- **Charter rule:** `planned`.
- **Proposed gate** G-Mz1: one §H.7 test is run on the estate. Purpose: be read and corrected. Counterproductivity condition: corpus growth outpacing `human_read` growth (UH-03). Measure it and publish the result.

## 7. Sunset criteria

**Wound down if the counterproductivity test produces no actionable threshold in any studied case.**

*Strength: good.* It is observable and adverse. **Its one ambiguity** is "actionable", which should be defined as *a threshold a named decision-maker changed a decision on*.

---

## Key positions (P1)

1. **Goodhart pressure scales with the tightness of coupling between metric and consequence, not with the metric's quality.** Decoupling is therefore a stronger remedy than better measurement. *(This bears on ML-2.3 §H.13: "do not couple a single metric to an automatic material consequence without human review".)*
2. **Publishing what a metric does not capture is the cheapest effective defence**, and it is almost never done.
3. **Metric plurality works only where the metrics are non-substitutable.** That is a testable property and it is rarely tested.

## Objections (strongest counter-argument, without reply)

Every institution that refuses metrics in the name of Goodhart ends up governed by unstated metrics,
such as reputation, anecdote and the loudest complaint. These are coupled to consequences just as
tightly and are far less inspectable. A programme that teaches suspicion of measurement may be
teaching institutions to measure covertly.

## Falsifier (for this charter)

The charter is defective if the programme publishes a dashboard or a ranked metric of any kind,
including about its own outputs.
