# ST-33 · Assessment and Academy Templates

## A1 — Credential dossier

```text
Candidate: ___   Track: [Series Practitioner | Professional Operator | Master-Fellow | MetaX Contributor]
Series: ___      Capstone URL: ___
Role statement (≤300 words): what I did, what others did.
Evidence log: | # | Artefact | Public / ON REQUEST (reason) | Proof level claimed (P1–P3) |
D1–D5 self-map: which evidence addresses which dimension (reviewer re-maps independently).
Reflection (≤600 words): where my approach failed, and what I would change.
AI disclosure (Exhibit 6 format, or "None").
Conflict declaration: employers, clients, family, equity relevant to reviewers.
```

## A2 — Evidence log row

`| id | artefact | date | hash | visibility | P-level | third-party verifier (if any) |`

## A3 — Pre-registration (capstone)

```text
Hypothesis: ___
Null: ___
Metric and measurement method: ___
Decision rule: If ___ by ___ then ___; otherwise ___.
Kill criterion: ___
Unit economics (if commercial): price / variable cost / CAC / retention / contribution / payback
Filed: YYYY-MM-DD (immutable after filing)
```

## A4 — Post-mortem

`Result vs null · decision taken · what surprised · what the Limits module predicted · published date`

## B1 — Academy application

```text
Thesis (800–2,000 words): claim · falsifier · audience · non-duplication (overlap %).
Curator eligibility: credential id or equivalent dossier; identity (escrow if pseudonymous).
Ethics reviewer (named) · Succession clause · Conflict policy · Moderation policy.
First series plan per ST-25.
academy.json draft (ST-31).
```

## B2 — Term sheet (bilingual, per series — MX-029 group 8)

`| term (en) | term (ar) | root | definition (1 sentence) | defining instrument | contested? |`

## B3 — Audit report

`Date · level · each of eleven groups: met / finding (evidence) · remediation deadline · status`
