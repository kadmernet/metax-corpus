# ST-26 · Unit Economics and Kill Criteria

> **Authority.** `operative-form`, tier 5. Two of the six companion rails in [ST-25](st-25-cognitive-arc.md).

> **Disclosure first.** **The estate has no unit economics and no kill criteria for itself.** It
> teaches both as mandatory in every commercial claim and every capstone. §4 applies the standard
> to MetaX and records the result.

---

## 1. Position

A commercial claim without unit economics is a hope. A plan without a kill criterion cannot fail,
which means it cannot be learned from. Both rails exist to make the learner — and the estate —
**state in advance what would make them stop**.

## 2. Definitions

**Unit economics:** revenue and cost **per unit** (customer, order, learner, page) with the
assumptions that produce them. Minimum: price, variable cost, acquisition cost, retention or
repeat rate, and the resulting contribution per unit and payback period.

**Kill criterion:** a pre-committed, dated, observable result that ends the effort. Form:
*"If [metric] is below [threshold] by [date], we stop [action]."*

## 3. Mechanism

| Artefact | Required where |
|---|---|
| Unit-economics sheet | Any lesson or capstone making a commercial claim |
| Kill criterion | Every capstone; every archetype path; every paid offering |
| Pre-registration | Filed before data collection; immutable after filing (identity-plane write, [ST-19](st-19-two-plane-architecture.md)) |
| Post-mortem | Published whatever the outcome |

A kill criterion **may not be amended after the date it names has passed**. Amendment before the
date is allowed and recorded.

## 5. Failure modes

Vanity metrics as kill criteria; thresholds chosen after seeing data; "we'll know it when we see it".

## 6. Limits

Unit economics suits transactional businesses and suits public-interest institutions poorly;
where a unit cannot be defined, the page says so rather than inventing one.

## 7. Falsifier

Wrong if capstones meeting this standard are no better at predicting their own outcomes than
capstones without it (measurable from post-mortems once any exist).

## 8. Obligation

The estate adopts a kill criterion for itself before selling anything.

## 9. Macro register

Institutions that cannot say what would make them stop tend to continue past usefulness,
consuming attention and money on inertia. **A published kill criterion is a promise to the reader
that the institution will not outlive its reason.**
