# ST-27 · Style Guide (English)

> **Authority.** `operative-form`, tier 5. Structure is [ST-24](st-24-page-prose-standard.md);
> terms are [ST-10](st-10-terminology-and-definition-standard.md); Arabic is [ST-11](st-11-arabic-authoring.md).

> **Disclosure first.** The corpus breaks §4 routinely: bold type is used several times per
> paragraph in most instruments, which dilutes emphasis until it signals nothing.

---

## 1. Voice

- **Declarative.** State the claim; then its evidence; then its limit.
- **No exclamation marks.**
- **Second person** for learner-facing pages; **third person** for governance.
- **Concede the strongest objection in the body**, not in a trailing disclaimer.

## 2. Banned words

*revolutionary · cutting-edge · unlock · journey · game-changer · seamless · leverage* (as a verb)
*· delve* — plus the corpus additions: *robust* (unless a test is named), *world-class*,
*empower*, *best-in-class*.

**Why:** each word signals quality without stating a checkable property.

## 3. Numbers and dates

- Every number carries a date or a `data-count-src` ([SITE-02](../site/page-contract.md) line 13).
- Dates ISO in metadata (`2026-09-25`), written out in prose (25 September 2026).
- Units always; conditions for any measured figure ([AG-017 §5](../agents/ag-017-evidence-and-citation-protocol.md)).
- No percentages without denominators.

## 4. Emphasis

One bold phrase per paragraph at most. Italic for titles and for terms at first definition.
Never bold a whole sentence to mean "important".

## 5. Spelling and names

British spelling (the corpus is British throughout). **MacroLifeTech (Meta-X)** — never the
misspelling ([C-13](../00-governance-map/corrections-log.md#c-13)). Transliterate Arabic terms with
diacritics at first use (*Ṣidq*), plain thereafter (Sidq). Pick one form per instrument.

## 6. Headings

Sentence case. Headings state the section's claim where possible ("The arc is a design choice"),
not only its topic.

## 7. Worked instance

Before: *"Our revolutionary rubric unlocks world-class assessment!"*
After: *"The rubric scores five dimensions and publishes no total, so that a strong score on one
cannot hide a failure on another."*

## 8. Falsifier

Wrong if readers rate pages following this guide as **less** clear than pages that do not (not
measured; the estate collects no reader data, [REG-06](../registries/reg-06-dataset-registry.md)).

## 9. Macro register

Marketing prose optimises for the feeling of confidence. Institutional prose must optimise for
**the reader's ability to disagree** — which requires that each sentence say something that
could be wrong.
