## PART B-A · Application and definitions

**§B-A.1 Application.** This profile applies to any Work whose Deployment involves substrate class S1 or above, or interface class I1 or above, as defined at BX-000 §1. Where a Deployment involves neither, ML-2.4 applies alone and this profile is inert.

**§B-A.2 Definitions.** Work, Licensee, Deployment, Affected Person, Author, Integrator, Operator and Commissioner carry their ML-2.4 Part A meanings. Living substrate, living-derived substrate, neural interface, substrate class S0–S4, interface class I0–I3, Custodian, Welfare Reviewer, and the extended Affected Party carry their BX-000 §1 meanings, which are incorporated and not restated.

**§B-A.3 Precedence.** On shared apparatus — evidence tiering, defect log format, role definitions, cure structure, notices — HX-000 and ML-2.4 govern and any divergence is a defect to be filed. On substrate-specific and interface-specific matter, this profile governs. Where the two conflict irreducibly, the more protective of the Affected Party prevails, and the conflict is filed.

**§B-A.4 Method withholding, binding on this instrument.** This profile contains no procedure, protocol, parameter, specification, sourcing route, or containment information, and its editors may not add any. Where a clause must describe what is controlled, it describes the control and its record. A request to add technical detail for credibility is a breach request under §B-H.19 and is logged as one rather than satisfied.

**§B-A.5 Not clinical guidance.** Nothing in this profile is advice to any patient, participant, or practitioner.

## PART B-B · The inverted grant

**§B-B.1 Nothing is granted by default.** No permission to use, reproduce, modify, distribute, or Deploy the Work arises under this profile until the preconditions of the applicable gate at §B-C are met **and recorded**. The grant is not a default that misconduct removes; it is a permission that recorded compliance creates.

**§B-B.2 Why.** ML-2.4's permissive structure presupposes an Affected Person who can be informed, can object, can appeal, and can leave. This profile's subject may be able to do none of those, or may have the capacity to do them altered by the Deployment itself. Where the subject cannot object, a grant that persists until objection is a grant that persists indefinitely. **Silence is not consent, and this clause is the operative form of that sentence.**

**§B-B.3 The cost of §B-B.1, stated.** An inverted grant will delay work that would have helped identifiable people, and will cede the unbound field to parties operating under no instrument at all, possibly making the aggregate worse. Both objections are strong. Neither is answered by asserting that caution is free. The estate's position is that irreversible injury to a possible moral patient, and irreversible injury to the faculty by which a person consents, are the two cases where the asymmetry is worth its price. That position is contested and is recorded as contested at RAT-B-03. **Any reproduction of §B-B.1 must reproduce this paragraph with it.**

**§B-B.4 Patent.** Each Licensee grants a non-exclusive, royalty-free patent licence limited to the Work as Deployed under a satisfied gate. The grant terminates automatically with the grant at §B-B.1 and does not survive termination.

**§B-B.5 No grant by estoppel, course of dealing, or silence.** Prior forbearance by the steward is not permission, and a party that Deployed without a satisfied gate acquires nothing by having done so undetected.

## PART B-C · Preconditions and the Substrate Gate

**§B-C.1 The Substrate Gate governs; the Scale Gate does not apply.** ML-2.4's Scale Gate tiers duties by the number of Affected Persons. That is correct where the variable is reach. Here it is incoherent: a single researcher holding one vessel of neural tissue does not present a thousandth of the moral problem a large facility presents, but the whole of it at small volume. Gating is by substrate class and interface class only, per BX-000 §2.

**§B-C.2 Preconditions by gate.** The preconditions are the safe harbours stated at BX-000 §6 for each entry applicable at that gate, plus the eight refusals at every gate. They are cumulative: G3 includes G2 includes G1.

| Gate | Preconditions to grant |
|---|---|
| G1 | Refusals A-1…A-8 · provenance chain (§B-H.4) · metabolic accounting (§B-H.18) · custody plan with named successor (§B-H.23) · method withholding (§B-H.19) · attribution record where I1+ (§B-H.9) · read minimisation where I1+ (§B-H.6) · non-conditioning term where I1+ (§B-H.14) |
| G2 | G1 · welfare register (§B-H.1) · interiority assessment (§B-H.3) · Welfare Reviewer appointed and independent · removability and escrow (§B-H.8) · baseline documentation (§B-H.10, §B-H.11) · scope register (§B-H.15) · outcome-verified containment (§B-H.20) · unenhanced-path measurement (§B-H.24) |
| G3 | G2 · precautionary handling with published position and stated uncertainty (§B-H.21) · external unannounced verification · funded custody surviving insolvency with automatic wind-down trigger · published sunset condition |
| GX | **No preconditions exist. No grant is available.** See §B-E.3. |

**§B-C.3 Recording.** Each precondition is satisfied only when recorded in the Compliance File at Schedule B-1, dated, and attributed to a named natural person. An unrecorded precondition is unmet. This is deliberately formal: the whole instrument turns on the record, and a record created after the fact is evidence of nothing.

**§B-C.4 The necessity doorway (*ḍarūra*), exhaustively specified.** A-2 (unconsented neural interface) is not breached where **every** element below is present. The list is exhaustive; no element may be supplied by analogy, and no other exception to A-2 exists anywhere in this instrument.

1. An immediate threat to life, or of irreversible severe injury, to the person whose nervous system is interfaced.
2. No alternative consented intervention is available within the time the threat allows.
3. The person cannot consent at the material time and has no valid prior refusal on record. **A prior refusal is binding and this doorway does not open against it.**
4. The act is the minimum acquisition or delivery required to address the threat, and no more.
5. **No retention** of signal beyond the acute episode.
6. **No secondary inference** from anything acquired, at any time, for any purpose.
7. **No scope expansion on recovery.** Anything continuing beyond the acute episode requires fresh consent obtained after capacity returns, and A-4 governs that consent — meaning consent obtained during or shortly after the intervention to *continue* it is void.
8. Recorded within twenty-four hours, naming the acting person and each element above.
9. Reviewed by the Welfare Reviewer, or where none is appointed by an independent clinician with no interest in the Deployment, within thirty days, and the review published in summary.

**§B-C.5 No doorway for the other refusals.** A-1, A-3, A-4, A-5, A-6, A-7 and A-8 admit no necessity exception, no research exception, no clinical exception, and no state-authority exception.

## PART B-D · Notices, disclosure, and the demo provenance rule

**§B-D.1** Every Deployment shall disclose: substrate class; interface class; gate; the Welfare Reviewer's identity and the basis of their independence; the Custodian's identity; the forum, retention period, and appeal service standards under §B-H.16; and the Compliance File's location.

**§B-D.2 Interface disclosure.** Any interface-mediated act shall be disclosed as such to the person before deployment and shall be recorded per §B-H.9. Where a decoder's interpretation contributes to an act, the disclosure shall say so in terms the person can act on, not in terms that are technically accurate and practically opaque.

**§B-D.3 No claim of compliance without gate.** No party shall represent a Deployment as licensed under this profile unless a gate is satisfied and recorded. Representation of compliance without a satisfied gate is a condition, and terminates.

## PART B-E · Governance

**§B-E.1 Steward.** MetaX.Academy, curator Maher, subject to §H.33 as adopted by ML-2.4 and to §B-E.5 below.

**§B-E.2 Amendment.** Ordinary duties on the ML-2.4 Part E procedure. **Refusals A-1…A-8 may be narrowed only** on the Track 2 procedure at ML-2.4 §2, with the additional requirement that the narrowing be supported by the falsifier stated in the corresponding BX-000 entry and by no other ground. A refusal may not be narrowed on grounds of commercial pressure, competitive disadvantage, or the existence of unbound competitors.

**§B-E.3 Permanent exclusion (binding on the estate).** MetaX.Academy permanently excludes substrate class **S4** (human-derived substrate, at any activity level) and interface class **I3** (write-capable invasive interface) from its own programme. No gate exists for either; no compliance route is available; no amendment to this profile may create one for the estate.

This clause binds MetaX.Academy and does not purport to bind others. It is drafted as a limit on the estate rather than as advice to the field because an estate that counsels restraint while reserving its own options has produced HX-15, and the reservation is always discovered later.

**§B-E.4 Dissent channel.** A route for raising defects in this profile, in BX-000, or in the steward's conduct of either, which does not pass through the steward, published at first issue with the receiving party named and their independence stated. Built in at birth rather than retrofitted, because HX-28 was identified before this instrument was written and the estate has no excuse for omitting it.

**§B-E.5 Withdrawal.** This profile shall be withdrawn in its entirety if: (a) moral status is resolved in a direction that makes its refusals incoherent; or (b) it is shown to function principally as a competitive barrier rather than a protection — HX-18 applied to itself; or (c) the steward can no longer maintain §B-E.4. Stated at adoption so that withdrawal never requires the consent of the party who would be embarrassed by it.

**§B-E.6 Re-derivation.** BX-000 is re-derived from primary evidence at 2029-06, or on first Deployment of any S2+ substrate or I1+ interface anywhere under this instrument, whichever is earlier. Re-derivation means re-argued, not re-formatted. Until re-derivation, no party shall represent BX-000 as an established empirical finding; it is a set of pre-registered predictions with named falsifiers, and DEF-2026-065 states so on its face.

## PART B-F · Standing, forum, and remedy

**§B-F.1 Standing for the voiceless.** A claim on behalf of a substrate may be raised by the Welfare Reviewer, any prior Custodian, any person appearing in the provenance chain or their successors, or any party the steward has recognised as a standing petitioner. **The substrate's inability to consent is never a bar to standing**, since that inability is the condition creating the need for standing.

**§B-F.2 Forum, aggregation, retention, service standards.** As §H.32, published at first issue. Retention is not shorter than the longest foreseeable claim horizon, and for interface Deployments that horizon is presumed to be the lifetime of the interfaced person unless the Licensee establishes otherwise.

**§B-F.3 Termination.** Breach of A-1…A-8 terminates immediately, without notice and without cure. Failure of a precondition means no grant ever existed, which is a different and stricter position than termination. Breach of any other duty carries the ML-2.4 cure structure: thirty days, or ninety where a shipped change is required, first cured breach deemed never to have occurred.

**§B-F.4 Survival.** §B-H.23 (custody), §B-H.16 (records), §B-F.2 (forum and retention), and A-8 survive termination, insolvency, dissolution, and change of control. **Custody duties attach to the holding, not to the holder.** There is no route by which they are extinguished by the disappearance of the party that assumed them.

**§B-F.5 Conflict tie-break.** Where A-8 (retain) conflicts with §B-H.16 (destroy on request), the Affected Person's request prevails unless a claim is filed, in which case the record is escrowed with a neutral holder. Identical to BX-000 §7.6.

## PART B-G · Interpretation

**§B-G.1** Ambiguity is resolved in favour of the Affected Party, including the substrate where §B-F.1 standing is engaged.

**§B-G.2** Where an element of a refusal is arguably absent, the refusal is construed as engaged and the Licensee bears the burden of establishing the element's absence. This inverts the ordinary construction and does so knowingly: the refusals concern irreversible injury, and the party proposing to proceed is the party in possession of the facts.

**§B-G.3 Severance.** If a clause is unenforceable in a jurisdiction, it severs as to that jurisdiction only. **The refusals do not sever.** A Licensee in a jurisdiction where A-1…A-8 are unenforceable at law remains bound as a matter of licence, and Deployment in reliance on unenforceability is breach.
