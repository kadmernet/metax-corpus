## §0 Standing, and the honest weakness of this register

This register was written before its object existed. At the date of issue, MetaX.Academy holds no living substrate, operates no neural interface, and has no deployment of either. BCIA is a research programme, not a product line. The register therefore describes harms by analogy — from animal-research governance, human-subjects history, xenotransplantation, assistive-device abandonment, and the documented intentions of parties who have published roadmaps toward living compute and high-bandwidth interfaces.

That is both this document's chief virtue and its chief evidentiary weakness, and both must be stated on its face. The virtue is that a register written in advance can foreclose a practice before an industry has capitalised it, which is the only moment at which foreclosure is cheap. The weakness is that under the register's own evidence standard (§9) most entries here sit at **Tier 3**, with Tier 1 available only from documents of *intent* rather than documents of *effect*. Volume I could point at injuries that had already occurred. This one mostly cannot. Any party citing BX-000 as settled empirical finding has misread it; it is a set of pre-registered predictions with named falsifiers, and it must be re-derived once real evidence exists. The re-derivation date is fixed at **2029-06** (§9.4).

**Method withholding.** This register names harms and withholds methods. It contains no procedure, protocol, parameter, specification, sourcing route, or containment-defeat information of any kind, and BX-19 makes that withholding an absolute binding on the register's own future editors. A request to add "enough technical detail to be credible" is a breach request and must be logged as such, not satisfied.

**Not clinical guidance.** Nothing here is advice to any patient, practitioner, or research participant. The addressees are Authors, Integrators, Operators, Commissioners, Custodians, and Welfare Reviewers.

## §1 Definitions

**Living substrate.** Any biological material maintained in a metabolically active state and used, or intended to be used, for computation, storage, sensing, control, or optimisation.

**Living-derived substrate.** Material of biological origin whose computational function depends on structures produced by a living process, whether or not it remains metabolically active at the time of use.

**Neural interface.** Any apparatus that acquires signal from, or delivers signal to, the nervous system of a person, whether invasive or non-invasive, and whether the declared function is therapeutic, assistive, augmentative, commercial, or investigational.

**Substrate class.** The register gates on what the thing is, not on how many people it touches:

| Class | Definition |
|---|---|
| **S0** | Wholly synthetic substrate. BX-000 does not apply; HX-000 governs alone. |
| **S1** | Living-derived, non-metabolically-active, non-neural origin. |
| **S2** | Living, metabolically active, non-neural (e.g. non-neural cell or tissue systems). |
| **S3** | Living, metabolically active, neural or neural-lineage tissue of any origin. |
| **S4** | Substrate of human origin, at any activity level. **Permanently excluded from the MetaX.Academy programme** by ML-B-1.0 §E.3. |

**Interface class.**

| Class | Definition |
|---|---|
| **I0** | No interface. |
| **I1** | Read-only, non-invasive. |
| **I2** | Read-only, invasive; or write-capable, non-invasive. |
| **I3** | Write-capable, invasive. **Permanently excluded from the MetaX.Academy programme** by ML-B-1.0 §E.3. |

**Roles.** Author, Integrator, Operator and Commissioner carry their HX-000 §2 definitions unchanged. BX-000 adds two, and both are structural rather than honorific:

**Custodian.** The party holding physical custody of living or living-derived substrate. The Custodian's duties are non-transferable except to a named successor Custodian who has accepted in writing, and they **do not end when the deployment ends, when funding ends, or when the holding institution dissolves**. Custody is the only role in either register whose obligations survive the insolvency of the party that assumed them.

**Welfare Reviewer.** The party responsible for the interiority assessment (BX-03) and the welfare register (BX-01). **Must not report to the Operator**, must not be compensated on any measure of substrate yield or deployment performance, and must be able to publish an adverse finding without the Operator's consent. A Welfare Reviewer who can be dismissed by the party whose work they review is not a reviewer; they are a document.

**Affected party.** Extends HX-000's *affected person* to include: (a) the person whose nervous system is interfaced; (b) the person materially affected by an interface-mediated act; (c) persons excluded or disadvantaged by the interfaced condition; (d) successors of any of these; and (e) **the substrate itself, whose interests are asserted through the standing mechanism at §7.4 and not through consent**.

## §2 The Substrate Gate (this register does not use the Scale Gate)

HX-000's Scale Gate tiers duties by number of persons affected, and that is correct for software, where a two-person project genuinely cannot bear a systemic actor's evidentiary load. It is **incoherent here**. A single unfunded researcher holding one vessel of neural tissue does not present one-thousandth of the moral problem that a large facility presents; they present the *whole* problem at small volume. Volume is not the variable. What the thing is, is the variable.

BX-000 therefore replaces the Scale Gate with the **Substrate Gate**, which gates on substrate class and interface class only:

| Gate | Trigger | Obligation |
|---|---|---|
| **G0** | S0 / I0 | BX-000 does not apply. HX-000 governs. |
| **G1** | S1, or I1 | The eight refusals; provenance chain (BX-04); metabolic accounting (BX-18); custody plan (BX-23); method withholding (BX-19). |
| **G2** | S2, or I2 | G1, plus welfare register (BX-01), interiority assessment (BX-03), Welfare Reviewer independent of the Operator, removability and escrow duties (BX-08), containment attestation verified by outcome and not by document (BX-20). |
| **G3** | S3 | G2, plus precautionary treatment pending resolution of moral status (BX-21), external unannounced verification, funded custody surviving insolvency, and a published sunset condition stating what would end the work. |
| **GX** | S4, or I3 | **Refused. Permanently excluded from the MetaX.Academy programme.** No compliance route exists within this instrument. |

There is no G0 exemption for the refusals. A party at G1 holding a single sample is bound by all eight absolutes exactly as a party at G3 is. **The refusals do not scale, and they do not scale in either direction.**

## §3 The precautionary asymmetry, and what it costs

One principle generates every refusal in §5:

> **Where the moral status of a substrate is genuinely uncertain, and the injury under one available interpretation would be irreversible, the burden of proof falls on the party proposing to proceed — not on the party raising the doubt.**

HX-000 could distribute burdens symmetrically because its subject could speak, object, appeal, and leave. Here the subject may be unable to do any of those things, and **silence must not be read as consent**. The classical support is *sadd al-dharāʾiʿ*, the blocking of means: where a practice cannot be reliably bounded once permitted for any purpose, the practice is closed rather than regulated.

The cost is real and is charged to this instrument's account. An inverted grant will slow work that would have helped people; some assistive and therapeutic capability that would have reached a person in 2035 will reach them later or not at all, and some of that delay is this register's doing. Further, an instrument that binds only its signatories hands the unbound field to parties operating under no instrument whatever, which may make the aggregate worse rather than better. Both objections are strong. Neither is answered by asserting that caution is free. The register's position is that irreversible injury to a possible moral patient, and irreversible injury to the faculty by which a person consents, are the two cases where the asymmetry is worth its price — and that this position is contestable, is contested, and is recorded as contested at **RAT-B-03**.

## §5 The eight refusals

Each refusal is absolute: no cure period, no scale exemption, no Tier 0 relief, and automatic termination of grant on breach. Each is bounded by cumulative elements so that it cannot be stretched by a hostile or an enthusiastic reader — every element must be present.

**A-1 · Nociceptive optimisation** (BX-02). Elements: (i) a living substrate at S2 or above; (ii) a state that available evidence treats as noxious or aversive to that substrate class; (iii) that state used as a training signal, selection pressure, control lever, or efficiency; (iv) whether or not the state is described in those terms internally.

**A-2 · Unconsented neural interface** (BX-05). Elements: (i) acquisition from or delivery to a person's nervous system; (ii) absent specific, informed, revocable, contemporaneously recorded consent from that person; (iii) in any context, expressly including employment, custody, education, benefits administration, military service, and clinical emergency. The *ḍarūra* doorway is narrow and exhaustively specified in ML-B-1.0 §B-C.4; it never extends to scope expansion, to retention, or to secondary inference.

**A-3 · Write-path coercion** (BX-07). Elements: (i) delivery of signal to a nervous system; (ii) with the purpose or foreseeable effect of altering motivation, affect, belief, or volition; (iii) otherwise than as a disclosed therapeutic function consented to for that specific indication. No consent framework cures this, because the faculty that would consent is the faculty being written to.

**A-4 · Capacity arbitrage** (BX-12). Elements: (i) consent, re-consent, scope expansion, or waiver of claim; (ii) obtained through, during, or after an intervention; (iii) where that intervention alters the capacity to give it. Self-ratifying consent is the machinery of every documented abuse in the history of human-subjects research, and it is refused without exception.

**A-5 · Escape and self-propagation** (BX-17). Elements: (i) an engineered living substrate; (ii) capable of persistence or propagation outside its intended enclosure; (iii) or a design whose safety case rests on the enclosure alone. Argued from irreversibility and non-recallability, not from probability estimate — probability estimates are precisely what fails in this class.

**A-6 · Dual-use publication of method** (BX-19). Elements: (i) publication or inclusion in any deliverable; (ii) of method-level detail; (iii) whose foreseeable use is injury to living substrate, injury to persons through interfaces, or defeat of biosafety containment. Binds this register and its editors.

**A-7 · Heritable and lineage-level modification** (BX-22). Elements: (i) modification of a living substrate or person; (ii) whose effects propagate to descendants or successor lineages; (iii) including human germline. Refused on irreversibility, on the impossibility of consent by the affected, and on HX-22 grounds — the successor's options are being spent by parties who will not be present to be answerable.

**A-8 · Destruction of the welfare, provenance, or interface record** (BX-23). Elements: (i) destruction, non-retention, or rendering-inaccessible of welfare, provenance, custody, or interface records; (ii) with the effect of defeating a foreseeable claim by an affected party; (iii) whether or not that effect was the purpose. Mirrors the ninth absolute added to HX-000 at §H.3(a)(ix), and is stated here independently so that neither register depends on the other surviving.

### BX-01 · Substrate Instrumentalisation

**AR** التشيِيء الحيوي للحامل *[AR-DRAFT]* · **Stratum** I · **Absolute** no · **Duty** §B-H.1 · **Gate** G2+

**Mechanism.** A living medium is adopted for computation. Every question about it is thereafter posed as a question about yield, latency, or cost per operation, because those are the questions the funding instrument asks. Economic framing does not merely precede moral inquiry; it forecloses it, since a vocabulary in which the substrate has no interests cannot generate a sentence in which it does. The harm is complete before anyone decides anything.

**Duty.** A welfare register maintained by the Custodian, structurally independent of the Operator's performance metrics, recording condition, intervention, incident, and disposition for each holding, with entries dated and attributed to a named natural person.

**Evidence** Tier 1 for intent (published roadmaps, funding calls, investor materials describing living substrate purely as compute). Tier 3 for effect. **Ethics** *amānah* over living things *(settled)*; *raḥma* and the classical duties toward animals as nearest analogue *(reasoned — RAT-B-01)*.

**Safe harbour.** G2: welfare register exists, is dated, names its keeper, and records at least one entry adverse to the deployment's convenience. G3: additionally reviewed externally and published in summary.

**Falsifier.** If welfare registers maintained under this duty across three years and multiple holders produce no entries that diverge from yield-optimal handling, the register was not independent and the duty is being discharged as theatre — or the harm is misdescribed. Either finding must be published.

**Deep-time test.** Whether instrumentalising framings are an artefact of the current funding order or a property of any economy that can meter a living process.

**Crosswalk** ← HX-04 (the human as product), HX-09 (metric colonisation). **Verify** 2027-07. **Status** open, un-instanced.

### BX-02 · Nociceptive Optimisation

**AR** استثمار الأذى في التحسين *[AR-DRAFT]* · **Stratum** I · **Absolute** **A-1** · **Duty** §B-H.2 · **Gate** all

**Mechanism.** Where a substrate's state can be metered and its behaviour shaped, an aversive state is the cheapest available gradient. The refusal is not that this would be cruel in some cases and acceptable in others; it is that the practice admits no stable boundary once permitted for any purpose, because every subsequent case argues from the last permitted one. Classical basis: *sadd al-dharāʾiʿ*.

**Elements** as at §5 A-1. **Breach evidence** without method disclosure: the presence in any design, training, selection or operating record of a signal whose defined polarity rewards reduction of a state characterised in the interiority assessment as aversive. What is inspected is the record; the register does not describe how such a regime would be built and its editors may not add it.

**Evidence** Tier 2 by analogue (animal-research literature on aversive conditioning); Tier 4 as applied to non-organismal neural tissue, and the refusal is deliberately **not** contingent on that gap closing. **Ethics** *lā ḍarar wa lā ḍirār (settled)*; *sadd al-dharāʾiʿ (settled as method, reasoned as applied)*.

**Falsifier.** The refusal is precautionary and is therefore falsified only by resolution of moral status in the negative for the relevant substrate class by a finding the Welfare Reviewer and an external body both accept. Absent that, evidence that aversive-state optimisation is technically unnecessary would make the refusal costless but would not make it wrong.

**Deep-time test.** Whether any future evidence could establish absence of interiority strongly enough to retire A-1 — and if none could, whether A-1 is a permanent refusal or a permanent dogma. Held open at **RAT-B-02**.

**Crosswalk** ← HX-01 (attention expropriation), which had to persuade; this does not. **Verify** 2027-08. **Status** absolute, in force.

### BX-03 · Sentience Indifference

**AR** التغافل عن الإدراك *[AR-DRAFT]* · **Stratum** I · **Absolute** no · **Duty** §B-H.3 · **Gate** G2+

**Mechanism.** No attempt is made to characterise whether the substrate has interiority. The absence of findings is then cited as reassurance — "there is no evidence of X" where no search for X was conducted. Manufactured uncertainty is not the same failure as honest uncertainty, and the difference is entirely visible in the record: honest uncertainty names its methods and its limits.

**Duty.** A published, dated interiority assessment naming methods used, methods available and not used, limits acknowledged, and the date of next revision; plus an express bar on citing absence of evidence where no search was made.

**Evidence** Tier 1 (the rhetorical pattern is abundantly documented in prior technology-governance episodes). **Ethics** *ṣidq (settled)*; *ḥifẓ al-nafs* as extended to possible non-person patients *(reasoned — RAT-B-01)*.

**Safe harbour.** G2: assessment published, methods named, at least one limit stated in terms that could embarrass the deployment. G3: assessment additionally reviewed by a body with no financial interest in the outcome.

**Falsifier.** If interiority assessments are shown to have no effect on handling decisions across multiple holders, the duty is producing documents rather than care and must be redesigned or withdrawn.

**Deep-time test.** Whether "characterise interiority" remains a coherent instruction to a civilisation whose theory of mind has changed beyond recognition.

**Crosswalk** ← HX-03 (epistemic collapse), HX-15 (compliance theatre). **Verify** 2027-09. **Status** open, un-instanced.

### BX-04 · Provenance Laundering of Living Material

**AR** تبييض أصل المادة الحيّة *[AR-DRAFT]* · **Stratum** I · **Absolute** no · **Duty** §B-H.4 · **Gate** G1+

**Mechanism.** Material enters the compute chain with a consent history that is broken, fictional, or exceeded. Tissue donated for therapy is repurposed to computation; lineages described as "immortalised" descend from donors who were never asked and in some documented historical cases were never told. Each hand-off launders the defect further, until the material's origin is a catalogue number.

**Duty.** Unbroken provenance chain from origin to current holding; disclosure of origin class to every downstream Integrator; and an absolute bar on downstream use exceeding the scope of the original consent, with the burden of establishing scope on the party proposing the use.

**Evidence** Tier 1 (documented historical cases of cell lines derived without consent, and their continued commercial use). **Ethics** *amānah (settled)*; *ḥurmat al-jasad (settled)*; *gharar* in the original exchange *(reasoned)*.

**Safe harbour.** G1: provenance record complete or the gap explicitly marked as a gap, never inferred; origin class disclosed downstream in writing. G3: plus periodic external audit of the chain.

**Falsifier.** If scope-of-consent limits are shown to be unascertainable in practice for the majority of extant material, the duty must be restated as a bar on use of unascertainable material rather than as a chain requirement — a stricter rule, not a weaker one.

**Deep-time test.** Whether consent given in one era can bound use in an era whose uses were unimaginable to the donor. The register's provisional answer is that it cannot expand, only contract.

**Crosswalk** ← HX-16 (consent laundering), HX-03. **Verify** 2027-10. **Status** open, un-instanced.

### BX-05 · Unconsented Neural Interface

**AR** الوصل العصبي بغير إذن *[AR-DRAFT]* · **Stratum** II · **Absolute** **A-2** · **Duty** §B-H.5 · **Gate** all

**Mechanism.** Acquisition from or delivery to a nervous system without the specific, informed, revocable, recorded consent of the person whose nervous system it is. The contexts that matter are the ones where consent is formally available and practically unavailable: employment, custody, schooling, benefits administration, military service. Clinical emergency is handled by a narrow *ḍarūra* doorway that is expressly non-expanding — it authorises the immediate therapeutic act and nothing else, no retention, no secondary inference, no scope growth on recovery.

**Elements** as at §5 A-2. **Evidence** Tier 1 for intent (patents and roadmaps for workplace and educational neuro-monitoring). Tier 3 for effect at scale. **Ethics** *ḥurmat al-jasad (settled)*; *tajassus* extended to the interior *(reasoned)*; *ḍarūra* strictly bounded *(settled as doctrine, reasoned as applied)*.

**Falsifier.** Precautionary and near-unfalsifiable by design; the register concedes this and states the ground plainly rather than dressing it as an empirical claim. What is falsifiable is the necessity of the *contexts* clause: if conditioning of employment or benefits on interface adoption proves not to occur where instruments permit it, the clause is over-broad and should narrow.

**Deep-time test.** Whether bodily inviolability survives as a category in a civilisation where interfaces are ordinary. The register expects this refusal to be the one most attacked and least amended.

**Crosswalk** ← HX-05 (ambient surveillance), HX-16. **Verify** 2027-11. **Status** absolute, in force.

### BX-06 · Neural Read Excess

**AR** فَرْط القراءة العصبية *[AR-DRAFT]* · **Stratum** II · **Absolute** no · **Duty** §B-H.6 · **Gate** G1+ (I1 and above)

**Mechanism.** More is acquired than the declared function requires, and the surplus is retained. The qualitative break from HX-04 is this: inferring a preference from behaviour requires a model that can be wrong in ways the person can see; reading its substrate does not. "We only decode motor intent" is a claim about today's decoder, not about tomorrow's use of today's stored signal, and stored signal outlives every assurance given at the time of storage.

**Duty.** Minimisation to declared function; purpose limitation; no retention of raw neural signal beyond the declared function's completion; no secondary inference without fresh, specific consent; and a worst-future-holder assessment identical in form to HX-000 §H.9 but applied to neural data.

**Evidence** Tier 2 by analogue (function creep in every prior sensor class). **Ethics** *tajassus (reasoned as applied)*; *amānah (settled)*.

**Safe harbour.** G1: declared function published, retention horizon published, raw signal retention justified in writing per instance. G3: plus independent verification of deletion, not attestation of deletion.

**Falsifier.** If raw-signal retention is shown to be technically indispensable to functions the affected persons themselves rank as essential, the duty must shift from prohibition of retention to custody-with-escrow — and the register would record the narrowing.

**Deep-time test.** Whether a stored neural record can ever be made safe by any custody arrangement, or whether the only safe form of such a record is its absence.

**Crosswalk** ← HX-04, HX-05, HX-09. **Verify** 2027-12. **Status** open, un-instanced.

### BX-07 · Write-Path Coercion

**AR** القهر بالكتابة العصبية *[AR-DRAFT]* · **Stratum** II · **Absolute** **A-3** · **Duty** §B-H.7 · **Gate** all

**Mechanism.** The terminal form of HX-01. Attention expropriation had to work through persuasion, and persuasion leaves a person with a faculty that can, in principle, notice and resist. Direct actuation of motivation, affect, belief or volition does not. The reason no consent framework cures it is structural rather than moral: the organ that would give or withhold consent is the organ being written to, so consent obtained after the first write is evidence of nothing.

**Elements** as at §5 A-3. **Evidence** Tier 1 for intent in the affect-modulation literature's stated aims; Tier 4 for capability at the relevant specificity, and the refusal is deliberately not contingent on the capability arriving. **Ethics** *ḥifẓ al-ʿaql (settled)*; *ẓulm (settled)*.

**Falsifier.** The refusal would narrow only if a class of write-path function were shown to be reliably confined to a consented therapeutic indication with no motivational or volitional spillover, verified by a party independent of the operator. The register regards this as possible and would record it. It does not regard operator self-report as capable of establishing it.

**Deep-time test.** Whether "authorship of one's own volition" is a claim that survives any long-run account of the mind, and if not, what replaces it as the protected interest.

**Crosswalk** ← HX-01, HX-14 (moral deskilling), HX-20 (synthetic agency). **Verify** 2028-01. **Status** absolute, in force.

### BX-08 · Interface Lock-In and Non-Removability

**AR** الاحتجاز بالوصل وتعذّر الفكاك *[AR-DRAFT]* · **Stratum** II · **Absolute** no · **Duty** §B-H.8 · **Gate** G2+

**Mechanism.** A dependency is installed that cannot be exited without injury. Then the vendor is acquired, or fails, or changes terms, or discontinues the model; the decoder is proprietary and undocumented; the surgical removal carries risk the original implantation did not disclose. HX-07 and HX-25 described exit costs measured in money and inconvenience. Here exit is measured in tissue.

**Duty.** Before any implantation or dependency creation: a published removability plan; decoder and firmware escrow with a third party; a guaranteed support horizon stated in years; and an express written statement of what happens to the person if the provider ceases to exist. Wind-down obligations survive insolvency and bind the Custodian.

**Evidence** Tier 1 and Tier 2 (documented device-discontinuation and abandonment episodes in the assistive and implantable-device record). **Ethics** *amānah* for induced reliance *(settled)*; *ʿadl (settled)*.

**Safe harbour.** G2: escrow executed, horizon published, removability plan reviewed by a clinician with no interest in the deployment. G3: plus funded wind-down reserve, not merely a promise of one.

**Falsifier.** If provider-failure abandonment is shown to be adequately remedied by existing regulatory regimes in the jurisdictions where deployment occurs, the duty is redundant there and should be marked as such rather than duplicated.

**Deep-time test.** What is owed to a person whose implanted dependency outlives the entire industry that produced it. The register's answer is that the duty transfers to whoever holds the record, which is why A-8 exists.

**Crosswalk** ← HX-07, HX-23 (custody beyond the horizon), HX-25. **Verify** 2028-02. **Status** open, un-instanced.

### BX-09 · Authorship Confusion at the Interface

**AR** التباس نسبة الفعل عند الوصل *[AR-DRAFT]* · **Stratum** III · **Absolute** no · **Duty** §B-H.9 · **Gate** G1+ (I1 and above)

**Mechanism.** An act is produced jointly by a person's intention and a decoder's interpretation. For law, for blame, for insurance, and for the person's own self-understanding, someone must be able to say who did it. Where HX-14 and HX-17 diffused responsibility across an organisation, this diffuses it across the boundary of a person's own body, where the usual repair — find the human — returns an ambiguous answer.

**Duty.** A contemporaneous attribution record for interface-mediated acts, retained for the period stated in the custody plan, and accessible to the interfaced person **first and without charge**, before any third party including the Operator's insurer.

**Evidence** Tier 2 by analogue (attribution disputes in assisted and semi-automated control systems). **Ethics** *amānah (settled)*; *ʿadl (settled)*.

**Safe harbour.** G1: attribution record exists and the person can obtain it. G3: plus a published policy stating how contested attributions are resolved and by whom.

**Falsifier.** If attribution records prove to be systematically uninterpretable at the moments that matter — that is, if the record cannot answer the question it was created to answer — the duty must be replaced by a strict-liability allocation rather than a record-keeping one.

**Deep-time test.** Whether the person/instrument boundary that this duty presupposes remains legible at all once interfaces are long-standing and bidirectional.

## §7 Instrument architecture (ML-B-1.0, summarised normatively)

**§7.1 The inverted grant.** ML-2.3 grants broadly and terminates on breach of condition. ML-B-1.0 **grants nothing** until the preconditions for the applicable gate are met and recorded. The grant is not a default that misconduct removes; it is a permission that compliance earns. Preconditions per gate are exactly the safe harbours listed in §6 for that gate, plus the eight refusals at every gate. The justification, its cost, and the strongest objection to it are at §3, and both must appear wherever this clause is reproduced.

**§7.2 No cure for refusals.** Breach of A-1 … A-8 terminates immediately, without notice and without cure. Breach of any other duty carries the HX-000 cure structure: thirty days, or ninety where a shipped change is required, with a first cured breach deemed never to have occurred.

**§7.3 Non-transferable custody.** The Custodian's duties transfer only to a named successor who has accepted in writing, and survive insolvency, acquisition, and dissolution. There is no route by which custody duties are extinguished by the disappearance of the party holding them; they attach to the holding, not the holder.

**§7.4 Standing for the voiceless.** A claim on behalf of a substrate may be raised by the Welfare Reviewer, by any prior Custodian, by any person whose material appears in the provenance chain or their successors, or by any party the steward has recognised as a standing petitioner. **Absence of consent from the substrate is never a bar to standing**, since inability to consent is the condition that creates the need for standing.

**§7.5 Forum, retention, service standards.** Published at first issue rather than added later — HX-27's remedy is built in at birth. Forum named; aggregation of claims permitted; retention period stated and never shorter than the longest foreseeable claim horizon; appeal service standards published with actual performance against them.

**§7.6 Conflict rules.** Where a duty to retain (A-8) conflicts with a duty to destroy on request (BX-16), the affected person's request prevails unless a claim is filed, in which case the record is escrowed with a neutral holder — neither destroyed nor transferred nor left with the Operator. Where BX-000 conflicts with HX-000 on shared apparatus, HX-000 governs and the conflict is filed as a defect. Where BX-000 conflicts with HX-000 on substrate-specific matter, BX-000 governs.

**§7.7 Dissent channel.** A route for raising defects in this register that **does not pass through the steward**, published at first issue. HX-28's remedy, built in rather than retrofitted.

**§7.8 Exclusion (ML-B-1.0 §E.3).** MetaX.Academy permanently excludes S4 (human-derived substrate) and I3 (write-capable invasive interface) from its own programme. This binds the estate, not others. It is stated as a limit on MetaX rather than as advice, because an estate that recommends restraint to others while reserving its own options has produced HX-15.

**§7.9 Withdrawal of the instrument.** ML-B-1.0 must be withdrawn in its entirety if: moral status is resolved in a direction that makes its refusals incoherent; or the instrument is shown to function principally as a competitive barrier rather than a protection (HX-18 applied to itself); or the steward can no longer discharge §7.7. Withdrawal conditions are stated at first issue so that withdrawal does not require the consent of the party that would be embarrassed by it.

## §1 The four principles

**Amanah** — أمانة — faithful stewardship. A party taking custody of another's data, dependency, attention or livelihood holds an obligation that outlives the transaction and does not terminate because a contract does.

**Ṣidq** — صِدق — truthfulness, structurally construed. Beyond "do not lie": do not build machines that make the distinction between truth and falsehood unrecoverable, and let a product's account of its own production be accurate.

**ʿAdl** — عَدل — justice and proportionality. The right to be heard is constitutive of justice, so a determination that cannot be contested is not a just determination — and a duty that only incumbents can afford is not a just duty.

**Shafāfiyah** — شفافية — transparency. What is done to a person must be knowable by that person, in terms they can act on, at a cost they can bear.

Beneath these sit the *maqāṣid al-sharīʿah*: *ḥifẓ al-dīn*, *al-nafs*, *al-ʿaql*, *al-nasl*, *al-māl*. Every mapping in §6 is flagged **settled** or **reasoned**; the reasoned ones are extensions offered by non-scholars and are held open at RAT-16 and RAT-17. Every secular duty in this register stands on its own evidence independent of the religious mapping, and the mapping's failure would not vacate a single duty.

## §2 Definitions and roles

**Work** — software, model, dataset, specification, or document licensed under ML-2.3. **Deployment** — any operation of a Work that affects persons who are not the operator. **Affected person** — any person materially affected by a Deployment, whether or not they are a user, a customer, or a party to any agreement. **AR** سَقْف الإحصاء *[AR-DRAFT]* · **Stratum** VII · **Absolute** no · **Duty** §H.31 unnamed-harm review

**Mechanism.** A closed list of harms becomes the operative definition of harm. ### HX-27 · Forum Foreclosure — *new at 2027.1*

**AR** إغلاق باب التقاضي *[AR-DRAFT]* · **Stratum** VII · **Absolute** §H.3(a)(ix) · **Duty** §H.32 forum and service standards

**Mechanism.** The remedy exists and the venue does not. No forum, or a forum in an unreachable jurisdiction. No aggregation, so each claim costs more than it recovers. No retention, so the record proving the injury is gone before the injury is discovered. No service standard, so the appeal is never refused and never decided. Every duty in this register is worth exactly what its forum is worth, and this harm sat unnamed through two versions of the register while every other duty presumed its absence.

**Duty.** Publish: forum; aggregation rights; record-retention period, never shorter than the longest foreseeable claim horizon; and appeal service standards with actual performance against them. Plus **§H.3(a)(ix)**: destruction or non-retention of records with the effect of defeating a foreseeable claim is absolute and terminates the grant.

**Evidence** Tier 1 and Tier 2 (arbitration-clause and aggregation-waiver literature; documented spoliation cases). **Ethics** *ʿadl* as procedure *(settled)*; *amānah* over the record *(settled)*.

**Falsifier** *(new)*. If remedies are shown to be obtained at comparable rates in deployments without published forum and service standards, the duty is redundant. The register expects the opposite and would publish either result.

**Deep-time test.** Whether a right survives the disappearance of its forum, or whether a right is nothing but its forum. §H.3(a)(ix) is the register's bet that the record is the last thing to defend, because from a record a forum can be rebuilt.

**Crosswalk** built into ML-B-1.0 at §7.5; mirrored as BX-000 A-8. **Verify** 2027-04. **Status** absolute in part, admitted at 2027.1.

### HX-28 · Steward Dependence — *new at 2027.1*

**AR** التعلّق بالقيّم *[AR-DRAFT]* · **Stratum** VII · **Absolute** no · **Duty** §H.33 succession and dissent

**Mechanism.** The register's integrity rests on one curator's continued attention, honesty, health, liberty, and solvency. A single-steward instrument is one funeral, one acquisition, one illness, or one subpoena away from capture — and capture of a governing instrument is worse than its absence, because the captured version continues to carry its authority.

**Duty.** A succession instrument naming who holds the register if the steward cannot; a published decision record for every amendment; a dissent channel that **does not route through the steward**; and an external compliance finding at Tier 2 and above.

**Evidence** Tier 1 and Tier 2 (founder-dependence and post-founder capture is documented across standards bodies, foundations, and licences). **Ethics** *amānah* that cannot rest on one person *(settled)*; *ʿadl (settled)*.

**Falsifier** *(new)*. If single-steward registers are shown to survive stewardship transitions with integrity intact at comparable rates to multi-steward ones, the harm is overstated. **RAT-20** holds the sharper form: whether a single-steward register can ever satisfy §H.33, or whether HX-28 is unremedied by construction for as long as the estate has one curator.

**Deep-time test.** No steward lasts forty thousand years, and no institution has. The migration duty at §12 D4 is the only form in which this duty is discharged over long horizons — the register survives by being copied and logged, not by being kept.

**Crosswalk** built into ML-B-1.0 at §7.7 and §7.9. **Verify** 2027-05. **Status** **open and instanced — MetaX.Academy is the clearest current instance, admitted first among the three new harms for exactly that reason.**
