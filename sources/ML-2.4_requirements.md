## 3. New and amended clause text (summary for drafting)
- **§H.3(a)(ix) — Record destruction defeating a claim (ABSOLUTE, new).** Destroying or failing to retain records with the effect of defeating a foreseeable claim by an Affected Person terminates the grant automatically, no cure. This is the **ninth absolute** (absolute count: 8 → 9).
- **§H.31 — Unnamed-harm review (covenant).** Annually, the licensee must record at least one harm it encountered that the register does not contain, or describe the search it made and why the search would have found one. (Duty behind HX-26.)
- **§H.32 — Forum, aggregation, retention and service standards (covenant).** Publication of: where claims can be brought; whether claims can be aggregated; record-retention periods; actual performance against appeal service standards. Carries §H.3(a)(ix). (Duty behind HX-27.)
- **§H.33 — Succession, decision record, dissent, external finding (covenant; for licensees who are also stewards).** Succession instrument; published decision record for amendments; a dissent channel that does not route through the steward (§H.33(c)); external compliance finding at Tier 2 and above. (Duty behind HX-28.)
- **§H.28 (amended) — Migration obligation.** Re-express instruments in each successor medium and language while retaining the prior expression unaltered; maintain the Migration Ledger (date, actor, diff hash).
- **§E.6 (new) — Register migration.** References must remain meaningful if links die; register maintained in substrate-independent form.
- **§E.7 (new) — Withdrawal of this instrument.** Mandatory withdrawal if the instrument becomes a barrier to entry or the steward cannot maintain the dissent channel.
